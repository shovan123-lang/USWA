Louisville Gardens
November 1st, 1990

The Nightmares, Rhodes and Fuller extract some revenge on Hot Stuff 
International when Danny Davis pinned Bobby Eaton to earn his team the victory. 

The Tag Team champions made short work of Scott Taylor and Joey Maggs, 
defending the USWA tag team titles as the competition continues to grow. 

Tonights show started with Dutch Mantell coming out and asking for a rematch
with Robert Fuller for the Southern Title. Moondog Rex came out and interrupted
Mantell's tangent. They would square off later in the night, with Rex getting
the pin over Mantell, after hitting him with a hubcap to the head. 

The Hardliners, Bill Dundee and Jerry Lawler brawled all around the Lousiville
Gardens tonight. These four men couldn't be contained under typical wrestling
rules. The Hardliners, like the The Nightmares and the Midnight express are all
making a claim to the USWA tag team titles. 

In his USWA debut Sam Houston has made his way to the territory. Sam Houston
was a crowd favorite and took a victory over the Missouri Tiger, Jeff Gaylord.
