Michael St John brings us a special report on the Top Prospect Tournamrnt: 

Eddie Marlin made a rare appearance in Evansville on Halloween night to 
announce the 1990 Top Prospect Tournament. 

On November 6th the USWA returns with a one night 8 man tournament. The rules
are simple, all of the wrestlers showcased will be have under 5 years experience
in the business. On one night the winner will be crowned the 1990 Top Prospect,
and that man will get an opportunity at the Southern Title. 

Some of the names that will participate in the tournament include Jeff Jarrett,
Reggie B Fine, Scott Taylor, Dustin Rhodes, Joey Maggs, Mark Canterbury,
Rex King of the Southern Rockers, and Tom Burton of the Dirty White Boys. 

These young prospects will have an opportunity of a lifetime to hot shot their
way to a title match in the future. 

Don't miss history in the making this Wednesday night in Evansville!
