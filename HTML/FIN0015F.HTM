Lance Russell welcomes the fans to the show, and starts off saying we have some
serious business to attend to this morning and brings out USWA Promoter Eddie
Marlin. 

Eddie Marlin announces the end of the business relationship between World 
Class Championship Wrestling from Dallas, and the USWA. Eddie Marlin says
that Kevin Von Eric and Skandar Akbar have made it perfectly clear that they
do not tend to continue doing business with the USWA. 

Lance talks about what a disappointment this is for the USWA and professional
wrestling fans in general. He then asks for Eddie to clarify the current 
Unified Championship Title situation for the fans. Marlin explains that their
have been some behind the scenes issues with Snowman, which has led to the
promotion stripping him of the title. With so many changes occuring from the
fallout of the breakup with World Class, the USWA will be holding a one night
tournament at the Mid South Coliseum on October 8th. The tournament will then
determine the NEW Unified Heavyweight Champion! 

As the fans applaud the decision, Lance Russell and Dave Brown both express
excitement over the huge news.  

Eddie Marlin also says that in the meantime, we are bringing back the Southern
Heavyweight Championship, and a champion will be crowned right here today on
television! Eddie names Dustin Rhodes and Dirty Dick Slater as the top 
contenders for the championship, and announces they will go at it today! 

Dirty Dick Slater makes his way out to the announce area, with his tag team
partner Dick Murdoch, who make up the team known as "The Hardliners". 

Dirty Dick Slater tells Lance Russell and Eddie Marlin that he can't wait to
get his hands on Dustin Rhodes and beat the life out of him. Winning the
Southern Championship would be a bonus! 

Lance tells Slater that he doubts Dustin Rhodes is going to be that easy of
a match for him, and says that he is looking forward to tonights Main Event.

A few young fans in the crowd antagonize Dick Slater, Slater pretends to run
after them in the studio while Dick Murdoch praises his tag partner to Lance 
Russell. 

Russell says that we need to head to a commercial break, and we will be
back so stick around!
