Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens 
September 13th, 1990
Attendance: 811

Terry Funk comes back to the USWA and wins a match against fellow Texan
Dutch Mantell. Funk looked impressive only a couple weeks out from the
title tournamwnt. 

The Nightmares get revenge over the Dirty White Boys. Last week Ronnie
P. Gosset interfered causing a DQ, this week, Eddie Marlin banned him
from ringside, and Ken Wayne won the pinfall. The victory didn't come without
some collateral damage. Danny Davis suffered an arm injury, and looks like
he will be missing some time. We should get more information at TV this
weekend. 

Lawler and Eddie Gilbert took to the ring tonight and chose their own
partners to team with in a 5 man elimination match. Lawler paired with the
Moondogs and the Steiners in the Steiners debut match in the USWA. They
would go on to defeat Eddie Gilbert, The Uptown Posse, Jeff Gaylord, and
Jeff Jarrett.
