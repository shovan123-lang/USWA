USWA Television Post Show Recap: 

Tonights action was mostly taped from the Louisville Gardens from last weeks
show. Impressive victories by Austin, Eddie Gilbert, Lawler and Dundee 
headlined this weeks show. 

We learned that Bret Hart will have his first title defense against 
Jacques Rougeau in our Nashville Debut. 

Lawler and Mantell continue to dominate the tag team scene on the heels of
their upcoming match with the Von Erichs, and Terry Funk seems to be in for
a world of hurt with Paul Ellering and Andre the Giant, we should know more
about all these storylines next week as we will be coming from Dallas Texas
with all the action!
