Match observations: 

Mid South Coliseum
4.27.1991
Attendance: 8,624

A jam packed Mid South Coliseum came out for tonights huge card, and the action 
did not disappoint! We crowned new tag team champions as the Steiners won back
the USWA tag titles in our main event. The party didn't last long as Dan Spivey
and Stan Hansen attacked the champions after the match. Spivey and Hansen have
come to the USWA via working agreement with AJPW and they have made it clear 
they want a piece of the Steiner Brothers! 

Opening the show was a match that was not advertised, but ended up being 
what some are calling an instant classic. Bret Hart stepped up to offer Kawada
a Unified title shot in the absense of Steve Austin. With the match confirmed
Kawada took the champion to a 34 minute match before being forced to tap to
the sharpshooter. Bret Hart continues to prove he is the best of the best in
the USWA. Post match, Eddie Gilbert came out to torment the champion. 

Andre the Giant returned to the terriory to team up with Jamie Dundee to take
on Dundees current rivals the Moondogs. Ellering made it clear that Andres 
services can be bought and paid for at any time! 

Bill Dundee defeated Terry Funk in their first ever bout. Dundee continues
to climb the ladder while Terry Funk has found himself in the middle of
a losing streak.

Jacques Rougeau gets a victory over Joey Maggs, but the story of the night was
Jeff Jarrett coming down to attack Rougeau after running down the fans in the
mid south coliseum. We should hear more on the fallout of this situation in
the coming weeks on television. 

Brickhouse Brown is back in the USWA. An impromptu match against former tag
team partner Sweet Daddy Falcone took place after Brickhouse turned down an 
offer to join the Memphis Mafia. Brickhouse will be in the first qualifying 
match for the TV title this week on Worldwide. 

Robert Fuller made short work of the Honky Tonk Man. Another big victory for 
Fuller. With Fuller and Dundee picking up big wins tonight, a singles match
between the two is sure to be in the making.
