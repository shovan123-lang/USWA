Television Recap

We are only 2 weeks away from crowning the Unified Champion in the USWA, and a
we still have two spots in the one night tournament to fill.  

This weeks television saw Jerry Lawler qualify with a victory over one half of 
The Southern Rockers, Rex King. Lawler put all participants on notice that he
will be leaving the Mid South Coliseum with the belt on October 8th. 

Dick Slater defeated Robert Fuller to defend his Southern Title here on 
Television. Fuller may have gotten the best of Slater after he beat him to
gain entry into the Unified Title Tournament, but it is Slater who still holds
the Southern title. 

Nightmare Ken Wayne and the Dirty White Boy attempted to settle their differences
on television today, but not much was accomplished once DWB's tag partner Tom
Burton made his way to ringside. We learned that Danny Davis will be missing 
upcoming shows, due to an injury suffered at the hands of the Dirty White Boys 
in Louisville last week. Steve Doll came down to help out his friend Ken Wayne. 
After the match Ken challenged the Dirty White Boy to a first blood match this
monday night at the Mid South Coliseum. 

The Steiner Brothers continue to impress the fans, racking up another victory
on television. Later in the program it was announced that they would face some
heavy competition this week when they go one on one with the Hardliners this
Thursday night. 

Eddie Marlin announced that the USWA will be making a weekly stop in Evansville,
Indiana starting this week and every Tuesday. Jerry Lawler will take on the
Snowman in singles action in a big main event in our debut in Evansville.
