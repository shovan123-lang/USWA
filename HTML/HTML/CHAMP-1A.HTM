Championship TV Recap: 

Tomorrow night at the Mid South Coliseum, the USWA will present a fully loaded
card for all the fans. Todays TV was the go home show for this week. 

Bret Hart kept his promise and has picked up singles victories over both 
Harliners and his most impressive victory, a submission win over Terry Funk
in todays Main Event. Funk and the Hardliners have had it with Bret, and they
expect Andre to make short work of him tomorrow night. Bret has made it clear
he won't be alone come this Monday. 

Sweet Daddy Falcone was a no show tonight and Scotty Flamingo took his place
in a match against Jerry Lawler, Lawler was the victor in this matchup. 

The Spellbinder defeated Dirty White Boy by disqualification when the Dirty 
White girl got involved in the matchup. Doug Gilbert and Dennis Coundrey came
out to keep the beating going on Spellbinder, until the Moondogs made the save.
These 6 men will meet in the ring tomorrow night. 

Dutch Mantell and Bill Dundee continued to have it out, with a match ending in
a double countout. When the refs restored order, Bill Dundee took the mic from
Dave Brown and asked Terry Funk to be his partner tomorrow night, taking on 
Dutch and Lawler in a tag team match. 

The Steiners picked up another win to open the show, and they look better than
ever as they get ready for face the Von Erichs with the titles on the line. 

Jeff Jarrett has asked for a no DQ/no Countout match with Austin tomorrow night.
