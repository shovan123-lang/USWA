Lance Russell brings out the USWA Champion, Jerry Lawler. 

Lawler talks about the challenge it has been night after night defending the
title at all the USWA live events. Lousiville, Memphis, Jonesboro, Evansville,
he has put the title on the line in front of all the great fans of the USWA. 

Eddie Marlin makes his way out and talks about what an honor it has been to
see the fighting champion Jerry Lawler put that title up for grabs night after
night. Marlin says that on October 29th at the Mid South Coliseum, the USWA
will be presenting a big night of action. We are calling it Grave Consequences!


Details will begin to be announced this week and next on TV, but our Main Event
has been decided. Jerry Lawler will defend that USWA Championship once again, 
against Terry Funk, but this time, both men will be locked inside the steel cage! 

Lawler takes the announcement in stride. He tells Eddie that he will fight Terry
anytime, anywhere and in any kind of match he can think of. 

Eddie goes on to talk about the winner of that match preparing themselves for
another defense at the fall classic, Thanksgiving Thunder in Louisville, KY on
November 22nd!
