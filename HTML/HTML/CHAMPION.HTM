Match for the vacant Southern Heavyweight Title:
Dick Slater pinned Dustin Rhodes with the Piledriver in 0:08:03.
Rating: * 1/2
(Dick Slater won the Southern Heavyweight Title.)
[Dick Murdoch interfered against Dustin Rhodes.]

Dustin Rhodes put up one hell of a fight against Dick Slater, but it was
interference from Dick Murdoch that led to Rhodes getting caught with that
devistating piledriver and leading to Slater being crowned the new USWA 
Southern Heavyweight Champion. Dick Murdoch pulled out a bottle of 
champagne to pour over his tag team partner in celebration. Dustin left
the ring in frustration over constantly having to watch his back with Murdoch
at ringside. 


Card rating: * 3/4


Match observations: 

Television Recap

The shocking announcement that World Class has severed their business 
relationship with the USWA has led to a lot more questions than answers as
of late in the USWA. 

Currently the USWA tag titles have been stripped of Don Harris and Brian Lee
when both men refused to sign exclusive contracts with the USWA. Jeff Jarrett
and Jeff Gaylord were the last #1 contenders, and Eddie Marlin says that
most of the top contenders were here in the USWA, which will eventually lead
to a title match being signed to crown the new champions. 

A bigger question mark was what will happen with the Unified Heavyweight
Championship. Eddie Marlin, the current USWA matchmaker and promoter has 
already started promoting October 8th at the Mid South Coliseum as the 
one night tournament to crown a new champion. We will hear more about who
will be headed to Memphis to compete in this tournament. Lots of names from 
around the world have been circulating inside pro wrestling locker
rooms as to who will be headed to the Mid South Coliseum next month. 

Dirty Dick Slater has been crowned the new USWA Southern Heavyweight Champion
with his victory over Dustin Rhodes here on Television. After the show went
off the air, we found out that Eddie Marlin has promised Dustin Rhodes a 
rematch monday night, with Dick Murdoch banned from ringside!
