Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Coliseum
January 30th, 1991
Attendance: 1,400

The long awaited return to Evansville saw a very respectable crowd witness
the Frontline team with Bret Hart to defeat the Hardliners, Terry Funk and 
Andre the Giant. 

We will hear comments from Bret Hart this week on TV, as he gets ready to face
Andre for the Unified Title. 

Eddie Gilbert defeated The Spellbinder today in singles action. Eddie told
the fans he has his eye on Steve Austin. During Austins match with John Tatum,
Eddie came out to watch the match.

Austin picks up another impressive victory, as he continues to impress. 

Sam Houston and Dirty White Boy met in the ring tonight, with Houston 
getting the victory. Houston got his hands on Bert Prentice and knocked him 
out when he jumped on the ring apron. 

The Nightmares teamed with Bill Dundee, defeating The Moondogs and Dutch Mantel
tonight. Dutch is taking the Moondogs under his wings and says he can make them
tag team champions.
