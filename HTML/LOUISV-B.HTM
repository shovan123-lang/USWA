Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
November 8th, 1990
Attendance: 2,302

The Hardliners Dick Slater and Murdoch leave the Gardens as victors over Lawler
and Kerin, putting them right in the middle of the discussion of number
one contenders to the USWA tag titles. 

In other action tonight, Robert Fuller defended his USWA Southern title over
Billy Joe Travis, another sucessful defense for the champion. Billy Joe 
was irate over losing the title match.

Moondog Rex got a little revenge on Dutch Mantell defeating him in singles 
action. He got his hands on his dog bone as the fans thought that his 
property was rightfully returned, but then Sweet Daddy Falcone came in to 
attack Rex and take the bone! 

Sam Houston continues to impress with a big win over Snowman in what some
are calling the match of the night. Houston has put impressive wins over
Snowman and Brickhouse Brown on his very early USWA resume. 

The Southern Rockers opened the show with a victory over The Twilight Zone,
Look for the Rockers and the Hardliners to be picketing Eddie Marlin and
the USWA executives for a future title shot!
