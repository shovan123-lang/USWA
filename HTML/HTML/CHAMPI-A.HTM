Match observations: 

Championship TV Recap: 

We crowned NEW Tag Team Champions today on USWA TV as the Nightmares defeated
the Dirty White Boys in tag team action making the Nightmares the 2nd team to
officially hold USWA Tag Team Gold. The Nightmares were still bruised and battered
from their match against Hot Stuff International earlier this week, but they 
pulled out the impossible upset. Tony Anthony has already demanded their title
rematch, but with the Hardliners winning the #1 contenders match in Lousiville
on Friday, they may have to wait in line. 

Thanksgiving Thunder will showcase Terry Funk vs Jerry Lawler in a barbed wire
match for the Unified Title. This will be one of the most dangerous matches we
have sanctioned in the USWA. Both men know what is on the line on November 26th.

Dustin Rhodes defeated Billy Joe Travis today, hoping a victory will keep Travis
off his back as he enters the top prospect tournament this week. Dustin 
introduced Bill Dundee to the audience and mentioned he will be in his corner
in Evansville.
