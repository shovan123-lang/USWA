Television Recap:
Studio Audience: 350

Steve Keirn breaks his silence, announcing his intentions to be the final
entry into the Unified Title Tournament. Not only did he intend, but he would
go on to defeat Dick Murdoch in the main event qualifying match to enter that
tournament. The field is now set. 

Jerry Lawler, Robert Fuller, Terry Funk, Action Jackson, Snowman, Eddie Gilbert,
Dutch Mantel, and now Steve Kerin have all qualified. Someone will be walking
out of the Mid South Coliseum as the USWA Unified Heavyweight Champion.

Jarrett and Gaylord continue to dominate the tag team scene. They picked up
a victory on TV today and then made a challenge to the Steiner Brothers. 
They said the USWA needs to crown their own tag champions since Brian Lee
and Don Harris are no longer being recognized as the promotions champions, 
since the split with World Class. Promoter Eddie Marlin came out and 
agreed that with the Unified Tournament Qualifying matches being held 
throughout the territory, the tag title scene has ben put on the back burner,
while some of the best teams in the world are here in Memphis. 

Marlin suggested that this Thursday night at the Louisville Gardens, we will
have a 6 team elimination match, with the winners being crowned as the
USWA tag team champions. Jarrett and Gaylord vs The Steiner Brothers, vs 
The Moondogs vs Hardliners vs The Nightmares vs The Dirty White Boys. 

Danny Davis made his return to the ring after suffering an injury. Davis told
the audience he received an overwhelming amount of letters and cards wishing
him well in his recovery. He then said he is back and the Nightmares plan on 
winning those USWA Tag Titles this week in Louisville! 

In other news from TV, 
The Moondogs and Uptown Possee will face off in Memphis in a stretcher match
and Eddie Gilbert defeated Alan Martin. Gilbert will face Bill Dundee in 
Memphis on Monday night in a Lumberjack match.
