Championship TV Recap:

A Huge night of action for Television this week. One of the most explosive shows
of the new year. 

The Debut of Scotty Flamingo took place tonight. Scotty is a flamboyant new
wrestler with lots of talent we saw in the ring. He made short work of the Masked
Avenger to gain a victory in his USWA Debut. 

Eddie Gilbert defeated Joey Maggs as the feud between Hot Stuff International
and Immortal continue. Eddie took some of his frustrations out on Maggs after
he defeated Billy Joe Travis making him leave the territory. 

Jerry Lawler has retained the Unified title via countout on Robert Fuller. 
Fuller was close to victory several times, but got stuck outside the ring. 
Lawler will now officially face Andre the Giant at Gauntlet for the Gold. 

The Steiners defeated the Front Line to win the 1990 Tag League tournament.
They held their trophies high and now will face the Nightmares in Memphis this
Monday at Gauntlet for the gold!
