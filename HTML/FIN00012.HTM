THE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP
Memphis, TN, Mid South Coliseum | Segment 3 (4:00)

Corey Maclin is in the ring with Eddie Marlin. Scott Steiner walks out with
the tag title belts over his shoulders, followed by Robert Fuller. Tully
Blanchard comes out last and stands apart from them in the corner.

COREY MACLIN: Ladies and gentlemen, at Star Wars Scott Steiner and Tully
Blanchard beat Stan Hansen and Dan Spivey by disqualification, and the tag
team championship stayed right where it was. Scott, you told us you had an
announcement.

SCOTT STEINER: Corey, when my brother left this company, everybody in that
locker room figured these belts were going back in a drawer. Mr. Marlin
told me I had to vacate them unless I found a partner. Well, I found one.

SCOTT STEINER: Mr. Marlin, I want it on the record tonight. Tully Blanchard
is my partner, and he's half of the USWA Tag Team Champions.

Steiner hands one of the belts to Blanchard. Blanchard takes it, looks it
over, and puts it on his shoulder.

EDDIE MARLIN: Scott, you held up your end. As of tonight, Scott Steiner and
Tully Blanchard are the USWA Tag Team Champions, and they'll defend those
belts like any other champions.

Fuller steps forward and takes the microphone from Maclin.

ROBERT FULLER: Hold on. Scott, I stood in your corner when nobody else
would. I offered to defend those belts with you. I stood out there at Star
Wars ready to go, and the referee sent me to the back on a technicality.
Now he gets the belt?

TULLY BLANCHARD: Fuller, I didn't take anything from you. You weren't
allowed out there. I was. I showed up, I got the win, and I'm the one
wearing the gold. That's how this business works.

Fuller and Blanchard step nose to nose. Steiner gets between them.

SCOTT STEINER: Robert, nobody is forgetting what you did for me. But Hansen
and Spivey want these belts back, and I need a partner who can go with
them. That's Tully.

Fuller looks at Steiner for a long moment, shakes his head, and walks out
of the ring. Blanchard smiles and raises his belt.

LANCE RUSSELL: Well, Dave, there's your new tag team champions, but I don't
know that everybody is happy about it.

DAVE BROWN: Robert Fuller sure isn't, Lance.
