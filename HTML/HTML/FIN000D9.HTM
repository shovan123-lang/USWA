Tojo Yamamoto runs into Kevin Von Erich backstage. Tojo congratulates Kevin
for his big win last week, and talks to him about the TV tournament. 

Tojo says that Eric Embry is going to represent the Bunch in this tournament,
but that The Texas Bunch would have two chances to bring the gold home if Kevin
joins up with them.

the Von Erich family. Tojo said that Mr. Funk is not going to be happy to hear
