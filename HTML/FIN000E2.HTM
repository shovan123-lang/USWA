Dave Brown talks about the big night this week back in the Mid South Coliseum.

Saturday Night April 13th

Bret Hart defends the Unified title against Hot Stuff Eddie Gilbert. Eddie 
has made it known he believes he should be the Unified Champion, today in our 
main event, Eddie and Doug will face off with Davey and Bret. 

In other action:
Andre The Giant w/ Paul Ellering vs Terry Funk
- This one has been brewing since our Fully Loaded event when Bret won the 
title and Funk slapped Andre in the face. This will be the first meeting 
for these two. 

Dutch Mantell and Jerry Lawler vs the Steiner Brothers
- Mantell and Lawler are out to cause chaos in the tag team division. Mantell 
took a cheap shot at Rick in the wheelchair when he was suffering from a 
concussion, and then went on to beat Scott Steiner in singles action with 
Lawlers help at ringside. These two teams will clash for the first time this
Saturday night! 

Kawada vs Davey Boy Smith vs Sam Houston
- You will see later in the program, the Southern Champion Steve Austin suffered
a back injury and will be away for a short period of time, Kawada, who already
won a top contenders match, will once again be put in a 3 way dance to name
a top contender for the Unified championship. The winner will face either Bret
Hart or Eddie Gilbert in the upcoming weeks. 

The Moondogs will take on Jamie Dundee, Jeff Jarrett and Bill Dundee in 6 man 
tag action. Jamie Dundee got himself in a little bit of a problem with the 
Moondogs and has asked for the help of his father and Jeff Jarrett. 

The Memphis Mafia and The Texas Bunch will be in the house and much more USWA 
action all this Saturday night at the Mid South Coliseum!
