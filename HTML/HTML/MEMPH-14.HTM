Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
February 18th 1991 
Attendance: 6,000


Monday night at the Mid South Coliseum saw a first time Main Event match
between Bret Hart and Kerry Von Erich. The match ended in disqualification 
as Terry Funk and Dick Slater attacked Bret Hart during the match. Funk took 
the mic and told Hart that Andre the Giant is going to make an example out 
of him.

A big tag team title match between The Steiners and the Nightmares took place.
This match was made after the Nightmares won a 6 team battle royal on Feb. 6th
in Jonesboro. The Steiners made short work out of the Nightmares, and they have
the Von Erich brothers next on the list. 

Sam Houston begged to team up again with Jarrett to take on Hot Stuff 
International. Tonight they got their rematch from TV, and picked up the 
victory, but it wasn't without consequences. Sam Houston suffered a minor 
back injury that is going to keep him out of action for a few weeks. 

The Dirty White Boy with Ronnie P Gossett and the Dirty White Girl at ringside,
went to a time limit draw with Kawada. 

Memphis saw the return of Doug Gilbert tonight with a victory over Scott
Taylor. Doug Gilbert is back in the USWA and will also be in Lousiville this
friday. 

The Southern Rockers opened the show with a victory over Sweet Daddy Falcone and
Action Jackson. The Rockers are looking to once again climb the tag team ranks
and they want to prove themselves with a rematch with the former champions,
The Nightmares. 


We will be in Louisiville this Friday night, with a match just signed, as Bret
The Hitman Hart will take on Terry Funk. Jerry Lawler will also be in the 
building along with all the USWA superstars!
