Match observations: 

Championship TV Recap: 

Sunday June 30th in St Louis will be the Date of Star Wars 91 and all roads lead
to that big event this summer! 

Bret Hart came out during the start of an in ring interview with Kawada and
gave him the championship match he has been waiting for since Steve Austin has
been on the shelf. This was a match of the year canidate that made Kawada shine.
From a young lion journeyman to a Unified Title shot in less than a year, Kawada
has a bright future no matter where he goes. 

Brickhouse Brown was profiled on television, showing his victory over
former tag partner Sweet Daddy Falcone, and then his TV qualifying match. 
Brickhouse will hope to continue the momentum when he goes one on one with 
Moondog Cujo next weekend. 

Jacques Rougeau made it clear that Jeff Jarrett is the next wrestler on his
hit list. And Bill Dundee defeated Terry Funk. Some are saying this could be 
the victory he needed to convince the USWA brass he should get a tv title
qualifying match.
