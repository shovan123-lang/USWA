Match observations: 

USWA Worldwide Taping
5.22.1991
Jonesboro, AR
Attendance: 420
ASU Convocation Center

USWA Worldwide continues to be your home for all the qualifying matches for the
upcoming one night TV Title tournament taking place in Nashville on June 21st!
In our main event, The Texas Bunch have guaranteed they will be represented 
in the tournament with Eric Embry getting a countout victory over Dutch Mantell. 
Mantell was furious that he won't be participating in the tournament for 
the title. 

Tonights in ring action continued with a bigtime opening six man tag. The New
Kids, Brian Christopher and Tony Williams, were shown backstage begging for Kevin
Von Eric to join them tonight to take on the Memphis Mafia. Brian Christopher
told Kevin he was full of wrestling knowledge and he and Tony are sponges waiting
to obsorbe the info! Kevin agreed, and led the team to a hard fought victory. 

USWA officials put together a singles match between Sam Houston and Rob Zakowski.
Rob showed off some flashy moves in the ring, but he was no match for the 
veteran Sam Houston. After the match, both men shook hands in a sign of respect.
Rob is still moving on in the TV title tournament, but a match like this continues
to give him the experience he needs to compete in the USWA. 

The Spellbinder also picked up a singles win over Mark Canterbury tonight.
Spellbinder is looking to rack up some consistent wins in order to get into 
the conversation for the southern title. 

In othernews, Scotty Flamingo has challenged Bill Dundee for a match this
weekend at Friday Night Fights.

The Moondogs joined Corey Maclin in the ring to challenge Jamie Dundee
and Andre the Giant to a rematch at Slamboree. This time, they will meet in an
anything goes tag team match! 

Rex King will take on Tom Prichard in singles action here on Worldwide next
week as the rilvary between the Memphis Mafia and the Texas Bunch continues
to grow. 

Also announced on the show tonight, The Steiner Brothers will be in action
in Lousivlle on Friday night fights for the first time since they were laid 
out in the ring by Spivey and Stan Hansen! The Dirty White Boy and Honky Tonk
Man are the unlucky winners of that spot on the card.
