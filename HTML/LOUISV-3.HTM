Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
March 1st, 1991
Attendance: 2,000

With a big tag title match on the horizon, both the Steiner Brothers and the
Von Erichs were in action tonight. The tag team champions made short work
of the Twilight Zone in our opening contest. On the other hand, the Von Erichs
were unable to defeat the Hardliners in their matchup. Some say this could be
a major weakness going into their title match. 

Sweet Daddy Falcone is still looking for revenge on Jerry Lawler, tonight
he picked up a victory over Oliver Peters in singles competition. Eddie
Marlin announced Lawler vs Falcone this week on television. 

Dutch Mantell was in action tonight, defeating Ernesto Jones. Mantell and 
Bill Dundee have been going at it ever since their short lived tag team didn't
work out. They will face off this week on USWA Television. 

The Spellbinder was hit over the head with a chair by the Dirty White Girl,
leading to a DQ win tonight. A rematch has been scheduled for TV this weekend.

Bret Hart made good on his promise, defeating Dick Murdoch tonight in singles
action. He will be in the Main Event on TV this week against Terry Funk before
his title match against Andre the Giant. 

In tonights main event Steve Austin retained his USWA Southern title, with a
victory over Jeff Jarrett. Jarrett will get one more chance in Memphis 
Monday night!
