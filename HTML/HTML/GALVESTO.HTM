Match observations: 

Galvoston TX
Attendance 1300

Our USWA debut in Galvoston, setting up night 2 in Dallas was a tremendous
night of action. 

Our opening match went the distance as Mark Canerbury, Scotty Flamingo, the
Dirty White Boy and Kawada went to a 30 minute draw, we were unable to determine
a contender to Austins southern heavyweight title. Kawada will take on Flamingo
1 on 1 in a top contenders match. 

Houstons own, Tom Prichard was a suprise for the fans tonight, he defeated the
Spellbinder, but he also looks to have suffered a concussion during the match. 

The Moondogs were no match for Hot Stuff International. Condrey and Doug Gilbert
have been on a rampage since they teamed together. The Nightmares, the Hardliners
and all the tag teams in the USWA should be taking notice of these guys. 

After a brief intermission, we returned with The Twilight Zone taking on Red 
Taylor and the Masked Avenger, the fans turned on this match quickly until 
Andre the Giant and Paul Ellering came out and chokeslammed everyone involved
in the match. Ellering proclaimed that Andre is on the hunt for Terry Funk! 

Jamie Dundee suffered another loss to the Honkey Tonk Man. In typical 
Honkey tonk fashion, He smashed his guitar over Jamies head when the ref wasn't 
looking. 

The Hardliners beat the Nightmares in tag action. Dick Murdoch and Slater 
continue to be a dominant force.

The Hot Stuff defeated the Hot Shot tonight as Eddie Gilbert continues a dominant
singles record. Missy Hyatt sprayed hair spray into Joey Maggs eyes after the
match. 

Steve Austin made short work of Sweet Daddy Falcone to retain his Southern
title. 

Kevin Von Eric hit the powerslam in our main event to defeat Jerry Lawler. 
These two will go at it in tag action tomorrow night at the Sportatorium in
Dallas.
