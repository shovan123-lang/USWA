Terry Funk is shown inside an unknown wrestling dojo in Japan as it's raining 
outside as you can see in the background windows.

Terry talks about the many tours of Japan he has done in his career, and his 
love for the country, the culture, and their wrestlers. Funk says that everytime
he comes here, he learns a new move, a new strategy, a new way to abuse his
opponents in the ring, and that this trip has been no different. 

Terry says that the USWA is in big trouble because his time here is over, and
he's headed back to Memphis as leader of the Texas Bunch, but that the team
won't be complete until they have the last member that they want in the 
organization, and thats Kevin Von Erich. Terry tells Kevin that he plans
on meeting him face to face this weekend!
