SAM HOUSTON IN-RING INTERVIEW
Jonesboro, AR, Earl Bell Community Center | Segment 1 (4:00)

Corey Maclin stands in the center of the ring. The fans are already on
their feet as Sam Houston walks down the aisle, but there is no hat wave
tonight and no smile. He steps through the ropes and takes the microphone
before Maclin can finish his introduction.

COREY MACLIN: Ladies and gentlemen, one of the most popular young men ever
to walk into this building, Sam Houston! Sam, the people here in Jonesboro
have always been in your corner, and I've got to ask you about the...

SAM HOUSTON: Corey, I appreciate it. I do. And I appreciate every one of
these people. But I didn't come out here tonight to grin and wave and tell
everybody how glad I am to be here.

SAM HOUSTON: I've been in this business since I was a kid. I've been in the
main event and I've been in the opener, and I never once complained about
where they put me. I laced up my boots, I gave these people everything I
had, and I went home. And you know what that got me? It got me overlooked.

SAM HOUSTON: Because somewhere along the line, this dressing room changed.
All of a sudden it's not about who can go out there and wrestle. It's about
who's got the loudest mouth, the shiniest robe, and the most sequins on his
tights. You know exactly who I'm talking about. Scotty Flamingo.

The crowd boos at the name.

SAM HOUSTON: Flamingo walks around the back like he owns the place. He
interferes in other men's matches, he runs his mouth on television, and the
younger boys watch him and think that's how you get ahead. That kind of
thing poisons a locker room, Corey. It poisoned this one.

SAM HOUSTON: So I'm done being the nice guy that everybody pats on the back
and forgets about. From here on out, Sam Houston is gonna take what's his.
And if Scotty Flamingo or anybody else wants to stand in my way, they'd
better be ready for a fight, not a fashion show.

Houston drops the microphone into Maclin's hands and walks out without
acknowledging the crowd. Maclin watches him go.

COREY MACLIN: Well, fans, that is a side of Sam Houston we have never seen
before.
