Match observations: 

Memphis, TN 
Mid South Coliseum recap: 

New Tag Team champions crowned tonight with the Von Erich Brothers getting 
the victory over The Steiners! A wild match which saw interference from the
duo of Dutch Mantell and Jerry Lawler. During the match Dutch set up a table
and Lawler piledrove Rick Steiner through the table giving Rick Steiner a 
concussion, leading to a victory for the Von Erichs. The Von Erichs seemed
like the didn't know what was going on, but some feel that Dutch and Lawler
planned this beating with Kerry and Kevin all along. 

Earlier in the night Dundee defeated Dutch Mantell to end their feud, but 
it came at a traumatic price. Official Jamie Dundee was given this match 
as his assignmnent, and when he saw Dundee getting destroyed by Dutch, he 
stepped in and interfered. Some people are unaware, but Jamie Dundee is the son 
of Bill Dundee, and it was only a matter of time until the news broke. 
We will hear from both Dundees this week on TV.
