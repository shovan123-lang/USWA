Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
September 10th, 1990 
Attendance: 1700

All eyes were on Robert Fuller tonight. The special guest referee in the
Southern Heavyweight championship main event. Dustin Rhodes and champion
Dick Slater. Fuller was the muscle that kept Dick Murdoch from interfering in
the match, but eventually when Murdoch got frustrated by not being able to
get his hands on Rhodes, he blasted the referee with a chair from behind!  
Knowing his tag team partners title was in jeopardy, Murdoch caused the
disqualification which would leave Rhodes with a win on our main event, but no
title around his waist. 

In the tournament qualifier semi main event, the legend Terry Funk defeated
Joey Maggs and will now officially compete in the tournament to crown a champion
on October 8th. Funk and Fuller are the first two to qualify. More matches will
take place this upcoming weekend on Championship TV.

Lawler and the Nightmares pulled out a victory in six man tag action over
Eddie Gilbert and The Dirty White Boys. The fans were excited to see the King
leave the coliseum racking up another win over Gilbert. 

With their tag team partners handcuffed to each other at ringside, Rex King
picked up a victory for the Southern Rockers beating Sweet Daddy Falcone with
a devistating superkick. Steve Doll, in an attempt to stop Reggie B Fine from
getting into the ring picked Fine up and they walked into the arena crowd! 
They were handcuffed together and it was a dratic move in an effort to keep 
Reggie as far away from the ring as possible. The fans got a kick out of 
watching the match with the two wrestlers in the bleachers. 

In the opening match Billy Joe Travis picked up a victory and Travis announced
his intentions to qualify for the tournament this Thursday in Louisville, KY. 

After the event, it was announced that next weeks Mid South Coliseum MAIN EVENT
would be Dustin Rhodes and Robert Fuller vs The Hardliners in a falls count
anywhere in the building match!
