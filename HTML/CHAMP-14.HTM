Championship TV Recap:

With so much happening around the USWA, todays television focused on a Southern
Title shot for Eddie Gilbert. Gilbert defeated Jeff Jarrett by countout with 
the help of Steve Austin! Austin jumped Jarrett outside the ring and led to
him being unable to return to the ring. Gilbert raised Austins hand and it looks
like we will soon see an dangerous alliance if Gilbert and Austin team together.

Dutch Mantell and Bill Dundee confronted Jerry Lawler telling him his frontline
won't be able to defeat The HARLINERS, Funk and Andre. They then got into a
verbal argument with Keirn and Tatum, challenging them to a match at the
Mid South Coliseum this week. 

The Dirty White Boy continued his winning ways with a victory over Oliver Peters.
Bert Prentice said the White Boy will take on all comers! He will have his hands
full this Monday when he faces Action Jackson in singles competition. 

The Nightmares pick up a victory today. Post match they talked about their 
victory in the tag team gauntlet. They said that they will get another match
against the Steiner Brothers soon enough!
