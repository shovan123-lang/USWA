Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
March 8th, 1991
Attendance: 950

Louisville was the site of the fallout from Fully loaded earlier this week. 
a rare appearance by the Unified Champion Bret Hart, along with his brother in
law, Davey Boy Smith headlined tonights action. 

Bret Hart defeated Eddie Gilbert in the best match of the show. Eddie Gilbert 
wanted to prove he could hang with the best in the world, and he accomplished
that task tonight. 

Scotty Flamingo took the mic to run down his opponent, Kawada in our
opening match. Scotty called Kawada the most boring wrestler in the promotion
and offered to give him coaching on charisma. Kawada was having none of it
and abruptly started the match. Kawada would get the win in a very good opening
match. 

After the ringside altercation at Fully Loaded between Dick Slater and Davey
Boy Smith, the two would meet up tonight in our semi main event. Davey
was just to strong for Slater and took the victory after a running powerslam.
The fans were excited to see Davey in action tonight. 

The Moondogs got the fans on their feet and were having a good time at ringside,
when Hot Stuff International members Dennis Condrey and Doug Gilbert came down
to change the mood. Condrey and Gilbert beat the Moondogs in tag team action.

Steve Austin retained his southern title once again defeating Jeff Jarrett. 
Missy Hyatt attempted to distract Jarrett on multiple occasions until Jarrett 
eventually layed a kiss on her! This was enough of a distraction for Austin
to hit the stun gun for the victory. 

Bill Dundee and Dutch Mantel continue their bloody feud, tonight in a texas 
death match. It was a victory for Dundee after several piledrivers took
their toll on Dutch and he couldn't make the 10 count. 

Michael St. John announced at intermission that the USWA would return to the 
Gardens on March 22nd. No matches were announced, but we should have more 
information on television this weekend.
