Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
November 19th, 1990 
Attendance: 4,200

With Thanksgiving Thunder this Friday night, attendance was a little low
for the Mid South show this week. 

In our Main Event Robert Fuller defeated Billy Joe Travis to retain his Southern
Title and Immortal members Taylor, Maggs and the Rockers held off Hot Stuff
International from doing any major damage to Fuller before his next title defense.

Sam Houston picks up a big win in a triangle match over Dundee and Tatum.
Houston continues to impress in his short time in the promotion. 

Steve Doll missed the show tonight and we had a special substitute as AX from
Demolition, who is in town for Tag League 1990 stepped in. King and Ax picked
up a victory over the Midnight Express when Eddie Gilbert ran in and attacked
Rex King. 

Misawa and Kawada from All Japan, a young team came into town and took on the
Steiners in a big match. A lot of international journalists are keeping an
eye on Misawa and Kawada, they think the young team can make some noise in
the upcoming tournament.
