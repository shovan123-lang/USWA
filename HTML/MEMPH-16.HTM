Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
October 1st, 1990 
Attendance: 1,432

The Mid South Coliseum hosted it's last Monday Night event before we crown a
Unified Champion, and in our main event tonight, Bill Dundee defeated Eddie
Gilbert in a Lumberjack match to send the fans home happy. 

Dundee holds two wins over Eddie Gilbert, and didn't qualify for the Unified
Tournament, this makes him a top contender to the USWA Southern Title coming
off a big main event victory. 

Eddie Marlin announced a 10 man battle royal, with the winner taking on Dick
Slater tonight for the Southern title. Dustin Rhodes looked like he had the
match in the bag, but Steve Doll of the Southern Rockers elimintated Dustin
at the last minute to earn the shot at the title later in the show.

During the Title Match, it was obvious that Doll was winded from the battle 
royal, and Slater took advantage of the fatigue! Slater won the match and 
once again retains the Southern Title. 

Jerry Lawler defeated Dutch Mantel to extract some revenge from his constant
interference in matches around the territory last week. This was the best
match of the evening, and a rematch was scheduled for tomorrow night in
Evansville.

In other action, Jeff Jarrett and Jeff Gaylord picked up a shocking victory 
over The Steiner Brothers. Video replay evidence shows that both shoulders
of Scott Steiner and Jarrett were down when the ref counted the pin, but 
the ref didn't see Jarrett, giving him and Gaylord a huge win.
