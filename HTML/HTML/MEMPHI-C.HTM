the Monday tradition. As the tournament begins to settle in, we saw what some
may call an upset as the young team of Misawa and Kawada defeated the Hardliners.

The Hart Foundation made their USWA debut, Bret Hart and Jim Neidhart come
to town looking for a main event match on any card they are booked on. 

In the biggest news of the night Eddie Gilbert defeated Dustin Rhodes and 
post match, Hot Stuff International left him laying in a pool of blood. None
of team Immortal could make a difference as Gilbert, Travis and the Express
