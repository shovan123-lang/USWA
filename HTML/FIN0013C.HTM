Post match interview at Ringside:

Lance Russell says that although a brutal attack took place last monday night,
The USWA champion still looked good with an impressive win over Jeff Gaylord. 

Lawler says that he felt he had accomplished something great when he defeated
Terry Funk last week at the Mid South Coliseum, but it was chaos after the
match. 

(Russell shows footage of the Hardliners locking the cage and the brutal barbed
wire attack)

Lawler calls Funk a chicken who bolted town to take dates in another promotion,
leaving Dick Murdoch and Slater to finish off the job he couldn't do. 

Lawler says Monday night, Dick Murdoch is going to wish he never stuck his 
nose in he and Funks business, and it will be Murdoch and Slater who suffer
the consequences this week in Memphis and Louisville. 

Lawler thanks Steve Kerin for stepping up and having his back when things got
out of hand last week. 

Lance mentions that Kerin will face Dick Slater in the Coliseum, and that 
both Lawler and Kerin will face the Hardliners in Lousiville with not only
a grudge match, but a number one contenders match for the DWB's Tag Team titles.
Major implications to take place around the territory this week.
