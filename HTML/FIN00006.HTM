THE NATURE BOY IN LOUISVILLE
Louisville, Louisville Gardens | Segment 6 (10:00)

"Also Sprach Zarathustra" fills Louisville Gardens. Ric Flair struts
through the curtain in a sequined robe, the Big Gold belt over his
shoulder. He takes his time coming down the aisle, slapping hands and
stopping to let the crowd get a good look. In the ring, he takes the house
mic from the ring announcer and waits for the "Wooo!" to die down.

RIC FLAIR: Louisville, Kentucky! Wooooo!

The crowd answers him.

RIC FLAIR: Now I know what you're thinking. You're thinking, what is the
Nature Boy, Ric Flair, the sixty-minute man, the limousine-riding, jet-
flying, kiss-stealing, wheeling, dealing son of a gun, doing in Louisville
Gardens on a Saturday night? Well, I'll tell you, brother. I go where the
action is. And right now, the action is in the USWA!

He holds the Big Gold belt up high. Boos mix with cheers.

RIC FLAIR: I've been all over this world. Tokyo. Mexico City. Madison
Square Garden. And there are two families in this business who built
wrestling in the state of Texas. The Funks. And the Von Erichs. I've
wrestled every one of them, in every building they've got, and I'm still
standing here wearing this belt.

RIC FLAIR: At Star Wars, I walked down to ringside and Kevin Von Erich
couldn't keep his eyes off me. And Terry Funk beat him. Some people say
that was an accident. Ric Flair doesn't have accidents.

A few fans shout up at him about Kerry. Flair points at them.

RIC FLAIR: Oh, I hear you. You want to know what I said to Kerry Von Erich
last Saturday in Memphis, when Andre the Giant left him lying in the middle
of that ring. You want to know what I whispered in his ear.

He paces, then stops and smiles.

RIC FLAIR: That's between me and Kerry.

The crowd groans.

RIC FLAIR: But I'll tell you what isn't a secret. Next Saturday night, at
the Sportatorium in Dallas, Texas, the Nature Boy steps into a USWA ring
for the very first time. And the man across from me is going to be Terry
Funk.

Big reaction.

RIC FLAIR: Terry, we've been here before. You put me through a table. I
made you say "I quit." Two years ago, you were the toughest man I ever
stepped in the ring with. But that was two years ago, and you're two years
older, and I'm still the man. And to be the man, Terry, you've got to beat
the man! Wooooo!

The lights flicker. Terry Funk's music hits. Funk walks out to the top of
the entrance with a mic, Kevin Von Erich beside him. The crowd erupts.
Flair backs up to the ropes and grins.

TERRY FUNK: Flair! You talk a lot about two years ago. Let me tell you
about next Saturday. You're not coming to Charlotte. You're not coming to
Atlanta. You're coming to the Sportatorium. Dallas, Texas. That's not your
house, Nature Boy. That's my house. And that's his house.

He points at Kevin. Kevin takes the mic.

KEVIN VON ERICH: You cost me at Star Wars, Flair. You walked down to
ringside and you took a match away from me. My family built that building.
You come to Dallas, you better bring more than a robe.

Flair laughs and spreads his arms.

RIC FLAIR: Kevin, I'll bring the robe, I'll bring the belt, and I'll bring
the limousine. And when I'm done with Terry, you can have whatever's left.
Wooooo!

Funk and Kevin stay at the top of the ramp, staring him down, and don't
move. Flair drops the mic, raises Big Gold, and leaves through the crowd
rather than walk past them.
