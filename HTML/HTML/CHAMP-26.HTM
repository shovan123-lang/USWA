Match observations: 

Championship TV Recap: 

The big story on tonights show was Jarretts win in a wild falls cout
anywhere match, defeating Doug Gilbert, who was personally chosen by his
brother Eddie, to get rid of Jarrett once and for all. 

Jarrett is coming off one of the biggest wins of his career and is once again
looking at Hot Stuff International to get his Southern Title back .

The match was all over the Arena, finished off with a powerbomb and
a pinfall a little over 13 minutes. 

Also on the card, Bret Hart defeated Condrey in a proving ground match. This
was a wild one with two of the best technitions in the company going at it in
a 22 minute master class. Condrey hit a dropkick, lariat and a DDT for the 
first nearfall, that was followed up with Bret Hart hitting a backbreaker, 
and a bodyslam. The match made it to the outside where Hart used the guardrail 
to slam Condrey into several times. He rolled him backinto the ring and set
him up for his submission for the victory. 

Kevin Von Eric was confronted by the Texas Bunch about joining the crew, his
hometown World Class friends, but he seemed hesitent to agree to anything. 
Embry reminded him that Terry Funk will be back next week, and he's going to
want some answers. 

In backstage news, Rick Steiner has informed the USWA tha the will be leaving
the promotion immediately. This brings up plenty of question about what the
future of the tag team titles looks like.
