Match observations: 

Nashville Fairgrounds
4.9.1991
Attendance: 1,100

A 5 star main event for Bret Hart in his first title defense. Fans were on their
feet for this one and Bret came as advertised to the fans in Nashville. Jacques
showed that he was a worthy opponent for the champion, but in the end, he suffered
the fate of the Sharpshooter. Bret Hart remains the USWA champion. 

Terry Funk got involved in the match distracting Bret Hart, but we got word in 
the locker room that Rougeau was not happy that Funk was at ringside and demanded
a match with him in Louisville. 

The night started off with the Dirty White Boy, The DW Girl and Ronnie P coming to
the ring, they announced the forming of the Memphis Mafia, which includes 
Sweet Dadddy Falcone, and Rex King. They went on to defeate the moondogs, along
with the debut of a new Moondog, Moondog Cujo! 

Hot Stuff International continues to climb the ranks in the tag team division,
with a victory over the Nightmares. Doug and Condrey have proved to be a great
team when they are in the ring. 

In a match put together strictly based on raknings, Kawada got a huge upset 
win over Davey Boy Smith. Kawada used a devistating brainbuster, that some say
Davey kicked out of at 2 1/2 count. Davey complained about a fast count, but
in the end Kawada gets the victory and is in line for a shot at Steve Austin or
Sam Houston for the Southern Title. 

Steve Austin defeated Sam Houston to retain the Southern title, but we found 
out after the show, Austin Suffered a back injury. The seriousness of the
injury is still unknown. 

In other action tonight:
Joey Maggs picked up a singles victory over Canterbury. The Dirty White Girl 
came down to help Mark back to the locker room.

Terry Funk was in action tonight, he made short work of young Corey Carpenter.

Honky Tonk Man won a 3 way over Jarrett and Spellbinder.
