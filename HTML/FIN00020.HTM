KERRY VON ERICH BACKSTAGE
Jonesboro, AR, Earl Bell Community Center | Segment 7 (3:00)

Corey Maclin finds Kerry Von Erich sitting alone on a bench in the back,
taping his wrists. Kerry doesn't look up right away.

COREY MACLIN: Kerry, I know Monday night was tough. Thirty minutes with
Bret Hart and you came up just short. How are you holding up?

KERRY VON ERICH: Corey, I shook his hand. I raised his hand in front of ten
thousand people. That's what you're supposed to do. But I'd be lying if I
told you it didn't eat at me.

KERRY VON ERICH: My whole life, people have been telling me what a Von
Erich is supposed to be. I went out there and gave Bret Hart everything I
had, and it wasn't enough. I keep asking myself what I left out there, and
I don't have an answer yet.

Eddie Marlin walks into the shot.

EDDIE MARLIN: Kerry, I'm sorry to interrupt. I've got some news for you.
You wanted to get back in that ring? Saturday night, Mid South Coliseum,
live on television. You're in the main event.

KERRY VON ERICH: Who've I got?

EDDIE MARLIN: Andre the Giant.

Kerry stops taping. He looks at Marlin for a long moment.

KERRY VON ERICH: Andre beat Bill Dundee. He and Paul Ellering's people put
Kawada on the shelf. Everybody's gonna tell me I'm crazy to want this one.

KERRY VON ERICH: But I asked for a chance to prove something, and you just
gave it to me. Saturday night, Andre. I'll be there.

Kerry stands up and walks out. Maclin turns to the camera.

COREY MACLIN: Kerry Von Erich and Andre the Giant, this Saturday night in
Memphis!
