THE KING SPEAKS
Jonesboro, Earl Bell Community Center | Segment 6 (5:00)

Jerry Lawler walks down the aisle wearing his crown and robe, to loud
boos with a few cheers mixed in. Lance Russell waits in the ring with a
microphone. Lawler takes his time, then takes the mic out of Lance's hand.

JERRY LAWLER: Thank you, thank you. Hold your applause, Jonesboro. I know
it's a thrill.

LANCE RUSSELL: Jerry, you asked for this time tonight, and you said it was
about Kerry Von Erich.

JERRY LAWLER: That's right, Lance. Because somebody in this company has to
tell that boy the truth, and it might as well be the smartest man in the
building.

The crowd boos. Lawler smiles.

JERRY LAWLER: Everybody saw Louisville. Kerry Von Erich and Bret Hart, side
by side. Wasn't that sweet? Two heroes, just best friends. Lance, let me
ask you something. Why would the Unified World Champion offer to team with
the man he's got to wrestle for sixty minutes in Dallas next month?

LANCE RUSSELL: Well, Bret said he understood the pressure Kerry was under.

JERRY LAWLER: Pressure. Come on, Lance. Bret Hart doesn't do anybody any
favors. Bret Hart teamed with Kerry so he could stand on the apron and
watch him. Up close. Every move. Every mistake. How long it takes him to
get up. Where his ribs still hurt from Andre's bear hug. Bret was taking
notes all night, and Kerry was too busy waving to the crowd to notice.

JERRY LAWLER: And then Kerry pulls Bret off Jamie Dundee and steals the
pin. You know what that told Bret Hart? It told him Kerry is desperate. A
man who believes he can win doesn't need to take somebody else's pin. A
man who's scared does.

LANCE RUSSELL: Jerry, Kerry Von Erich is a former World Champion.

JERRY LAWLER: And I've beaten him, Lance. Plenty of people have. Kerry
Von Erich has more talent than any man in this business. I'll admit that.
But talent doesn't last sixty minutes. Bret Hart is going to drag him
into the deep water and wait for him to drown, and now Bret knows exactly
where to hold him under.

JERRY LAWLER: I'm not telling Kerry this because I like him. I don't. I'm
telling him because this is my town, and I want to see the look on his
face Saturday night when he figures out I was right.

He hands the mic back to Lance, adjusts his crown, and walks out to a
chorus of boos.

LANCE RUSSELL: Well, whatever you think of the King, folks, he's given
Kerry Von Erich something to think about before Dallas.
