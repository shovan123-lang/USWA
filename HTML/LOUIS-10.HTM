Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
October 4th, 1990
Attendance: 2,500


In front of a sold out Louisville Gardens, The Dirty White Boys shocked the
wrestling world, defeating 5 other tag teams, and becoming the first official
USWA Tag Team Champions! The Steiners were the first team eliminated, which 
sent shock waves across the arena, with most expecting them to be the heavy
favorites. The Hardliners eliminated Jarrett and Gaylord, taking out the team
most fans felt would be a close second to win the titles. 

After the match, the Dirty White Girl and Ronnie P Gossett celebrated with
the tag team champions in the ring. This was just the beginning of a very 
exciting Championship week about to take place around the Territory for the
USWA! 

In our Semi Main Event- Terry Funk was back in the USWA leading up to this
Mondays tournament. He took on Steve Kerin and defeated him with the spinning
toe hold, gaining major momentum leading into the one night tournament. All
the superstars from around the wrestling world are making their way to Memphis
for what should be an amazing tournament. Steve Keirn maybe rethinking his
desire to participate in the tournament. We learned that Dave Brown and Lance
Russell will announce the first round matchups this weekend on USWA TV. 

Other action tonight saw big wins by the Uptown Posse, Dutch Mantel with a
big win over Robert Fuller. Both men are in the 8 man tournament.

Eddie Gilbert continues to look like a top contender with a win over Dustin
Rhodes, despite the interference from Bill Dundee.
