Match observations: 

Championship TV Recap:

We had another wild show from the studio today. The tag team champion Steiner
Brothers were in action, and they talked about their upcoming rematch with the
Nightmares. Scott said they won't delay the rematch any longer and will put the
titles on the line this Monday at the Mid South Coliseum. 

Jeff Jarrett came out tonight to discuss his frustrations with Hot Stuff 
International and losing the Southern title at the Mid South Coliseum last
Monday. Sam Houston came out and said he has been watching Eddie Gilbert and
his crew run around the USWA for too long. He suggested Jarrett picks him as
his tag team partner to face off against Austin and Gilbert tonight on TV. 
The match began shortly after and Houston and Jarrett lost when Missy Hyatt
distracted Sam Houston for the win. Sam Houston demanded a rematch in Memphis 
this Monday! 

The Von Erics were live in the studio today and announced that they have signed
exclusive deals with the USWA and they will be sticking around the territory 
for quite a bit of time. They went on to ask for the best competition in the
USWA and that's The Steiner Brothers. They said they will be at the Mid South
Coliseum this Monday to keep a close eye on the tag title match. 

in singles action Scott Taylor picked up a win over Kawada with a big elbow 
drop. Taylor will face a mystery opponent this Monday at the Mid South Coliseum.
He said he will face anyone the USWA puts in front of them. 

Our main event saw a wild 8 man tag with the Front Line picking up a victory
over Funk, Andre and the Harliners. Next week on TV, Andre the Giant will 
defend the USWA title against Robert Fuller. As the announcement was made
Bret Hart was furious and said that he will be in the studio and have a 
face to face with Eddie Marlin.
