HEROES AND HARD FEELINGS
Louisville, Louisville Gardens | Segment 12 (3:00)

The referee counts three. Kerry Von Erich rolls off Jamie Dundee as the
bell rings, and Louisville Gardens explodes. Andre the Giant is still on
the floor where Kerry's tornado punch put him, with Paul Ellering trying to
help him up. Bret Hart stands in the corner where Kerry pulled him off
Jamie, breathing hard and staring at his partner.

RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners, Bret "The
Hitman" Hart and Kerry Von Erich!

Kerry climbs the nearest corner and throws his arms up. The fans chant "Von
Erich! Von Erich!" Jamie Dundee rolls out to the floor holding his head,
and Ellering herds him and Andre up the aisle.

Kerry jumps down and turns to Bret with his hand out. Bret looks at the
hand for a long moment. He shakes it once, quickly, and lets go.

Bret takes the ring announcer's mic.

BRET HART: Kerry. Good win.

The crowd cheers. Bret holds up a hand for quiet.

BRET HART: I had Jamie Dundee in the middle of this ring. I was the one who
was going to finish it. But you pulled me off him and took the pin. That's
fine. Tonight we won. That's what matters.

He hands the mic back, steps through the ropes, and walks up the aisle
without looking back. A few boos come down from the balcony. Most of the
building isn't sure what to think.

Kerry watches him go, then shrugs and turns back to the crowd. He slaps
hands along the front row on his way out, signing autographs and taking his
time. At the top of the aisle he looks back at the ring one more time,
holds up both arms, and disappears through the curtain.

RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for coming to Louisville
Gardens. Drive home safely, and we'll see you next time the USWA comes to
town!
