Match observations: 

Mid South Coliseum 
Attendance: 4,000
Championship TV Recap: 

Terry Funk made his return to the USWA after being overseas on tour. 
He has made it clear that all the legends of World Class must stick together! 

In the ring Funk took on the Spellbinder one on one. Spellbinder is known
for his unique moveset and came out the gate with a running headsissors 
to take down Funk. In order to ground him, Funk choked him and eventually
hit him with an atomic drop. Funk continued to use illegal maneuvers and using
the ropes to rake Spellbinders eyes. With the Spellbinder stunned, Funk
hit the running bulldog, which set up the spinning toe hold. Bill Rush called
for the bell as the Spellbinder was in serious pain. 

Jerry Lawler was out to hype tonights main event with Dutch Mantell vs 
Kerry Von Erich, but he also challenged the champion, Bret Hart to a
proving ground match next week, in an attempt to get himself a shot at
the title in the near future. 

The New Kids are on an impressive run, that continued with a victory over
the Memphis Mafia. The Dirty White boy and Tony Williams started the match,
with DWB hitting some heavy offensive maneuvers. The mafia attemted illegal
double team tactics, until Brian Christopher cut off Slater on his way into
the ring. The New Kids took advantage of the double team and executed classic
tag team manuevers. Dick Slater put Christopher in an abdominal stretch 
submission hold, but Christopher was eventually able to escape, hit a snap
suplex which setup the Tennessee Jam finisher. Another victory as this team
continues to climb the tag team ranks. The Dirty White Girl gave
Downtown Bruno and earful as she helped her team to the locker room.

Kevin Von Erich celebrated with the Kids in the ring, and
it seems that Kevin has given these guys the seal of approval. 

Backstage Dave Brown spoke to the Nightmares, who had spoke out about Spivey
and Hansen, along with making their intentions known that they are gunning for
the titles. 

As we inch closer to the TV title tournament, Kawada and Scotty Flamingo
had a singles match tonight. Bill Dundee was ringisde and interfered against
Flaming and distracted the ref, giving Kawada a chanced to take the ringbell 
and knock Scotty out cold. This only makes things more contentious with the
tournament right around the corner. 

Steve Austins open challenge for the southen title have a lot of fans 
wondering who will step up at Star Wars. 

In a highly anticipated in ring intervew, Scott Steiner confirmed that Rick
has been released from his contract and Eddie Marlin will make a decision on
the matter. We will hear something this upcoming week on Worldwide. 

Rob Zakowski and Jamie Dundee went to a timelimit draw. Both talented young
superstars had fans on their feet. Jerry Calhoon completely missed Zakowski
use a chair to help him elevate and it a devistating springboard dropkick. 
Jamie Dundee had the fans going wild after a shoulderblock that forced RVD
to the floor. The timelimit expired and the fans gave both kids a standing 
ovation. 

Chris Adams of the Texas Bunch looked good in singles action. During the match
we had an insert promo with Adams talking about the success of the Texas Bunch.

Our main event saw Kerry Von Erich pin Dutch Mantell. Jerry Lawler and Bret 
Hart were both ringside during the match. During the match Bret Hart hit Dutch
with a chair, and Lawler would do the same to Kerry. Tony Faulk warned both 
wrestlers that he could DQ Dutch or Kerry if there was anymore outside 
interference. Dutch Mantell had Kerry in the Dutch Clutch multiple times 
but couldnt get him to submit. Bret Hart pulled the rope down while 
Kerry distracted Faulk. Bret Hit a DDT on Dutch and threw him back in the
ring. Dutch had one more comeback in him and got Kerry in a third Dutch Cluth.
Kerry broke the hold, Dutch goes for a lariat, a duck down to avoid the move,
and a crucifix rollup ended the match, with Kerry's hand held high.

Jerry Lawler attended to his tag partner, and told Bret Hart he would see
him in the ring next week! 

A great night of wrestling from the Mid South Coliseum. Wednesday night in
Evansville will see Jamie Dundee vs Moondog Rex for a TV title qualifying 
match.
