JAMIE DUNDEE
Memphis, TN, Mid South Coliseum | Segment 7 (3:00)

Corey Maclin is backstage with Jamie Dundee, who is pacing.

COREY MACLIN: Jamie Dundee, at Star Wars you and Andre the Giant beat your
own father, Bill Dundee, and Jimmy Valiant. How does it feel?

JAMIE DUNDEE: How does it feel? Corey, it feels like the first time anybody
in this company paid attention to me. My whole life I've been Superstar
Bill Dundee's kid. You know what my father said when I started wrestling?
He said I wasn't ready. He's been saying it for years.

JAMIE DUNDEE: And then he needs a partner at Star Wars, the biggest night
of the year, and who does he pick? His son? No. He picks Jimmy Valiant. A
washed-up legend who couldn't remember where the ring was. My father picked
a stranger over his own blood.

JAMIE DUNDEE: Paul Ellering is the only man in this business who ever
looked at me and saw what I could be. So don't ask me how it feels to beat
my father. Ask him how it feels to be wrong.

Jimmy Valiant walks into the shot, hands up.

JIMMY VALIANT: Jamie, Jamie, hold on now, brother. Your daddy loves you.
He's out there in that ring every night with your name on his lips. Come
on, let's talk about this man to man.

JAMIE DUNDEE: Man to man?

Jamie looks at Valiant for a second, then pulls a steel chain from his back
pocket and whips it across Valiant's head. Valiant drops. Jamie wraps the
chain around his fist and hits him twice more as Maclin yells for help.

COREY MACLIN: Somebody get back here! Jamie, stop it! Stop it!

Agents pull Jamie away. He shoves free, drops the chain on Valiant's chest,
and walks off.

JAMIE DUNDEE: Tell my father that one's for him.
