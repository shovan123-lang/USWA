Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
December 28th, 1990
Attendance: 1,721


Eddie Gilbert kicked off the show talking about the death of Dustin Rhodes 
and the death of his faction Immortal. He said Hot Stuff International is
the only group that will run the USWA. Out came Joey Maggs to defend the honor
of the Immortal. Gilbert defeated Maggs in a death match where falls count
anywhere in the building. 

We had A Block action for the remainder of the show.
