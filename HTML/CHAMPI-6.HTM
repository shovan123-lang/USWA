Championship TV Recap: 

Tonights TV recaped all the action from the big show at the Mid South Coliseum
last week. The event saw the highly anticipated tag match between the USWA tag
team champions, the Steiners vs the newcomers Dan Spivey and Stan Hansen. In
non title action Spivey and Hansen left the tag team champions laying 
in the ring to end the night. 

Jerry Lawler and Kerry Von Eric have made it offical, a singles match
to take place on a big television special, live from the Mid South 
Coliseum. Slamboree 91! This is a top contenders match, with the winner 
getting a future title shot against whoever the champion may be at a later
date. 


Jeff Jarrett sends Jacques Rougeau packing tonight with a victroy in our
semi main event. 

Robbie Z with his biggest victory of his career, with a win over 
Scott Taylor and a chance to compete for the Television title in 
an upcoming tournament scheduled to take place in late June 
from Nashville. 


Eddie Gilbert defeated Davey Boy Smith and kept the beatings coming long
after the bell rang. Eddie and HSI knew that Bret Hart was not in the 
building tonight, and they took advantage of that leaving Davey to fend 
for himself. Due to the actions after the bell, Eddie Marlin has booked 
a big six man tag to take place in Lousiville on May 24th. Eddie and 
Doug Gilbert, with Dennis Condrey will face Bret, Davey Boy and Kawada!
