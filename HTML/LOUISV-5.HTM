Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
February 15th 1991
Attendance: 1,645

Steve Austin and Eddie Gilbert team up for the first time to defeat Jeff Jarrett
and his hand picked partner Kawada. It was a spectacular matchup, but it was
Hot Stuff International getting the victory. Post match Sam Houston came down
to ringside to check on Jeff Jarrett. 

Robert Fuller has resigned with the USWA and had a victory over Terry Funk. 
Fuller is a top contender to Andre the Giants Unified title. 

Jerry Lawler had a victory in singles action over Sweet Daddy Falcone. Lawler
is also looking ahead to a Unified title rematch with Andre the Giant. 

Bret Hart had a standout match against the young lion Mark Canterburry. Mark
has come a long way in his USWA career, but he was no match for Bret Hart 
tonight. 

In tag team competition, Dutch Mantell and Bill Dundee befeated Kerry and Kevin
Von Eric. This match had all the fans out of their seats cheering, but the
USWA Main Stays picked up the victory over the World Class idols. 

The HARDLINERS continue their winning ways with a victory over The Southern 
Rockers. The HARDLINERS are looking for a rematch with The STEINERS to capture
the uswa titles. 

Sam Houston challenged anyone from hot stuff international to a singles match. 
Dennis Condrey took up the challenge and faced them one on one. Houston came
away with the victory. 

In opening matches, Honky Tonk man and Dirty White Boy defeated Spellbinder and
Joey Maggs.
