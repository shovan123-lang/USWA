Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
February 8th, 1991
Attendance: 1,450

Our main event saw a 3 way draw, as Sam Houston, Scotty Flamingo and Steve 
Austin went at it in the Gardens. This match would go a long way in naming a 
contender for Jeff Jarretts Southern Title, but it looks like that title will
have to be named at a later date.

The Dirty White boy continues a sucessful week with a victory over Joey Maggs.

The Hardliners over the Southern Rockers in tag action. Dick Slater and
Dick Murdoch had Terry Funk by their side tonight who was a constant distraction
at ringside. 

Bill Dundee defeated Dennis Coundrey of Hot Stuff Interntional. Dundee had
words for the Dirty White boy post match. 

In a surprise appearance at the Gardens, Kerry and Kevin Von Eric made their 
USWA debuts! They defeated the Moondogs and had the entire building on their
feet. We hope to hear more about the future of the Von Erics in the USWA. 

Kawada and Canterbury had another solid opening match with Kawada picking up
the victory.
