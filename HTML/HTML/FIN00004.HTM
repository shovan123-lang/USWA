Michael St. John says that the USWA has another busy week ahead of them
with a huge card at the Mid South Coliseum this Monday, and a special night
in Lousivlle, KY on Thursday.

Monday night @ The Mid South Coliseum:

Just announced Eddie Gilbert faces Bill Dundee in a no rules match! The referee
is there just to count the pinfall! After the bloody attack tonight, Bill
Dundee will be out for a little revenge on Eddie Gilbert! 

The Dirty White Boys will take on the Southern Rockers in tag team competition.
With the status of Brian Lee and Don Harris up in the air, this top contenders
match will help us get one step closer in forming new Tag Team Champions. 

In our Main Event, The King Jerry Lawler will take on Snowman! It's obvious
that these two men don't like each other, and we will see them come to blows
at the Mid South Coliseum!

Also scheduled on the card will be The Uptown Posse taking on The Nightmares,
Danny Davis and Ken Wayne. 

Those matches, plus we will hear from the new Southern Heavyweight Champion
for the first time Monday night after todays featured Main Event. 

Don't miss USWA action at the Mid South Coliseum this Monday night!
