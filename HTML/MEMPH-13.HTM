Midsouth Coliseum 
October 22nd, 1990 
Attendance: 3,900

This weekend on TV it was announced Lawler will defend the title against Funk
in one weeks time back at the Mid South Coliseum. The champion went on the
offense tonight, getting involved in our main event, and helping Keirn pick
up a second victory in 2 weeks over Terry Funk. After the match, Funk flipped
a switch. He called down the Hardliners, who assisted in the vicious attack on
Kerin, and also extracted some revenge on Lawler leaving both men a bloody 
mess in the middle of the ring to close the show.  These 5 men, and add Bill
Dundee will meet in 6 man action in Louisville this Friday.

Eddie Gilbert defeated Bill Dundee. Dustin Rhodes watched on from the entrance
rapmp. Eddie continues a hot streak leading into Thanksgiving Thunder. 

Scott Steiner defeated Action Jackson in a battle of the big men. 

The Dirty White Boys once again defeat Jarrett and Gaylord to keep those tag
titles. 

Robert Fuller defeated Dick Slater in his rematch for the Southern Title. 
Fuller gets the victory and looks to defend against all challengers.
