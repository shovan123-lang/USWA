Dave Brown starts the show welcoming the fans to the Mid South Coliseum, at this
time he introduces the number one contender Kawada! Kawada makes his way down 
to the ring with a translater. 

Dave Brown comments that Kawadas recent victories over Davey Boy Smith and
Sam Houston were quite impressive, and that everyone knows he is waiting 
patiently for Steve Austin to return so he can get his Southern Title Shot. 

Through a translater, Kawada says his excursion to the United States has been 
tremendously helpful in his development as a pro wrestler. He trains hard and
it has shown in his recent matches. 

Kawada says he will soon get his title shot and show the world he is one of
the best. 

Bret Harts music hits and the Unified Champion hits the ring to a standing 
ovation.

Hart says he has been watching Kawada work his tail off and he is quite impressed.
Bret says that since the day he won the title, he committed to be a fighting 
champion. Since Steve Austin isn't here, and Bret hasn't been booked for a match,
why doesn't Kawada face him tonight for the Unified Title! 

The fans go crazy for the announcement, Dave Brown looks around to see if anyone
can confirm the match. Eddie Marlin at ringside takes the mic and says yes, 
the title will be defended tonight...right now! 

With that, the fans go nuts and the bell rings to start the match.
