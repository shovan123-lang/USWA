Match observations: 

USWA Worldwide Taping
6.19.1991
Jonesboro, AR
Attendance: 420
ASU Convocation Center

Mark Canterbury picks up a win over The Honky Tonk Man, who has been critical
of Mark for taking the Moondogs under his management. The match somewhat under
delivered, but Mark wins with a back suplex that had Honky seeing stars. The 
Power of the big man Mark Canterbury was the deciding factor, lets see if that
loss will end HTM's constant bad mouthing of Mark and the Dogs.

Jeff Jarrett was challenged by Eric Embry tonight when Embry was paid by 
Eddie Gilbert to finish off Jarrett. The match was a death match non 
sanctioned by the USWA. Jarrett ended up defeating Embry, much to the dismay
of Hot Stuff International. 

It was announced that at Star Wars, a "Tag Team Mayhem" #1 contenders
match will take place. A 4 way tag team match to determine a new number one
contender to the tag titles. Qualifying matches will be singles matches
with a member of the team competing to qualify them over the next 2 weeks.

Dennis Condrey and Danny Davis went one on one representing their tag teams
with the opportunity to join Tag Team Mayhem. The match ended in a draw, and 
it was announced that instead of a rematch, both teams will enter the match. 

Eddie Guerrero pinned Moondog Spot, eliminating the Dogs from the Mayhem match,
and adding Gringos Locos to the Mayhem. We have one more team to qualify,
and that match will be next week at Worldwide tapings in Evansville, IN 
with the New Kids taking on Chris Adams and Tom Prichard. 
An interesting night for Missy Hyatt, who found a diamond studded necklas 
waiting for her in her dressing room. The note said that "Diamdons are forever".
Assuming the only man that would give her a gift like this was Austin after
she gave him a diamond studded robe, she went to thank him for the gift,
but Austin had no clue what she was talking about. Missy was frustrated and 
confused about the mysterious gift.
