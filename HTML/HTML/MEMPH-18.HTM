Match observations: 

Mid South Coliseum
4.13.1991
Attendance: 6500

In one of the most high profile matches in Eddie Gilberts career, he went toe
to toe with the Unified Champion, and took Bret Hart to a time limit draw. 
Eddie proved to be the most challenging opponent since winning the title. We
should hear more about the fallout of this match on upcoming television. 

Terry Funk and Andre the Giant finally met in the ring, Andre once again was
defeated by the small package, the same move that ended his Unified title 
reign. 

The Von Erich brothers started the night off with a victory over the Nightmares. 
After the match Kevin took the mic and called out both Lawler and Mantell and
The Steiners, they went on to challenge BOTH teams to a THREE WAY DANCE on
the 27th. 

The Memphis Mafia and the Texas Bunch came to blows tonight, which led a
match with Dirty White Boy pinning Tom Prichard. I don't think this is the
last we will see of these two groups. 

The Moondogs pinned Jamie Dundee even with the help of Bill Dundee and Jeff
Jarrett. Jamie Dundee was knocked out and for the first time, wasn't able to 
run his mouth and get himself in trouble! 

Toshiaki Kawada, who has beenin the USWA on excursion has made a big splash.
After defeating Sam Houston to become the number one contender to Steve
Austins Southern Title, Eddie Marlin moved the goal posts and put him in
a 3 way dance against Smith and Sam Houston, once again Kawada defeated both
men and is first in line for a shot at the title.
