The Hart Foundation started off the show calling out the Nightmares. They 
questioned why they didn't join the Tag League and said that they should
thank the WWF for sending so much talent down to make Tag League a reality. 
Anvil says that the Foundation didn't come down here just to be on display for
the Memphis Fans, they wanted real competition and called out the Nightmares
to defend their titles tonight! 

The Nightmares made their way down and said they would compete in a non title
match, but if the Foundation wins, they would get a title shot in the future.

With that being said the Hart Foundation defeated the Nightmares, but in doing
so, Bret Hart suffered a neck injury that will keep him out of action for
a few weeks. The Hart Foundation hope to win the titles and stick around to 
defend them at Gauntlet for the Gold! 

In tournament action the Steiners continue their dominance, but Jarrett and
Gaylord are keeping the pressure on. The Hardliners got their first 
victory in the tournament and hope to turn things around.
