Match observations: 

Louisville, KY
4.12.1991
Attendance: 2000

Jacques Rougeau opened the evening losing to Tery Funk in a hot opener. Jacques
has had 2 great matches since arriving in the USWA. Terry Funk took the mic 
and once again ran down Andre the Giant and Paul Ellering. 

Scott Steiner lost to Dutch Mantell in our second match. Scott was trying to
get some revenge on Mantell and Lawler for attacking Rick when he was still
in a wheelchair! 

Prichard and Embry defeated the Hardliners. Post match Murdoch and Slater got
into a heated argument, and we have since been told Murdoch has left the
promotion, leaving out the backdoor after his match. 

Jamie Dundee defeated Moondog Rex to earn his first singles win. 

The Moondogs over Honky, DWB and Canterbury.

Sweet Daddy took a win over The Masked Avenger. 

In a top contenders match, Kawada defeated Sam Houston and everyone in the USWA
is taking notice to Kawadas recent win streak. Kawada is due for a Southern
title match, but with Austin out, he may have to wait. 

In our Main Event, Hot Stuff International, the Gilbert Brothers defeated
Davey and Bret Hart. Eddie got the pinfall over our current champion Bret Hart.
Eddie took the mic to end the show telling Bret he proved tonight that he can
be the next Unified Champion!
