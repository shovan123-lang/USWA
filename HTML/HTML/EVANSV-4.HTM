Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Coliseum
October 23rd, 1990
Attendance: 1,020

Eddie Gilbert was the star of the evening in Evansville with a big win over
Bill Dundee. Gilbert called out Dustin Rhodes, who wasn't at the show tonight.
They will meet one on one in Louisville, KY on Friday night. 

Robert Fuller defeated Sweet Daddy Falcone defending his Southern title. Next
week he will be in the Main Event on halloween night, defending the title
against a new number one contender to be determined on TV this weekend. 

Joey Maggs picked up the victory over Moondog Spike in Spike's final match with
the promotion. Spike will be leaving the territory, but plans for a singles
career are still in the works for his former partner Moondog Rex.
