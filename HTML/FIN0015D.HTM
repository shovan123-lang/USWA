Michael St John talks about this Monday Nights big night of action at
the Mid South Coliseum. 

Jerry Lawler will team with Jeff Gaylord and Jeff Jarrett to take on The
Snowman Brickhouse Brown, and a mystery man. 

Jarrett and Gaylord make brief comments telling Snowman and Brown that they
plan on winning this match, and that they have Lawlers back on Monday Night. 

Rex King will go one on one with Sweet Daddy Falcone. His tag team partner
Steven Doll will be handcuffed to Reggie B. Fine to keep him from getting
involved in the match! 

Eddie Gilbert will team with the Dirty White Boys to take on Bill Dundee and
The Nightmares Danny Davis and Ken Wayne! 

The double main event has a tournament qualifying match scheduled as 
Terry Funk makes his return to Memphis to take on Joey Maggs. 

A video plays of Terry Funk talking about how he heard about the tournament
that was going to happen and he has made it a priority to get to Memphis and
be apart of this historic event. Funk says that he has dreamed of winning that
Unified Title since the day it was revealed to the wrestling world. Funk then
says that Joey Maggs won't be a problem when they face off at the Mid South
Coliseum! 

The second half of the main event will be Dustin Rhodes getting his rematch
with Dick Slater for the USWA Southern Heavyweight Championship, but this
time Robert Fuller will be the special guest referee! 

St. John wraps up the segment with ticket information and tells the fans 
not to miss the action Monday night!
