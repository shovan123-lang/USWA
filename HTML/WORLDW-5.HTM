Match observations: 

USWA Worldwide Taping
6.05.1991
Jonesboro, AR
Attendance: 400
ASU Convocation Center

Our Main Event was a qualifying match for the TV title Tournament that is only
two weeks away. Nashville, TN on a friday night, we will crown the first ever
USWA Television Champion! Until then, we still have two more qualifying matches 
to go, and tonight Doug Gilbert and Sam Houston met to decide who moves on! 

The match didn't take long as Doug Gilbert immediately took Houston to the floor
throwing him into the guardrail, and the stairs before setting him up for a
devestating powerbomb through the announce table. Houston tried to beat the
count, but Gilbert hit him with a Lariat that woukd keep him out of the ring,
giving Gilbert the countout victory in under 3 minutes.Rob Zakowski came
to check on Houston and help get medical attention to his friend. Hot Stuff
International will be represented in the TV title tournament! and after 
tonight, it looks like Gilbert is going to make it a brawl no matter who he
ends up facing! 

With Art Barr out of action due to an injury sustained by Spivey and Hanson, 
Eddie Guerrero teamed up with the Nightmares to take on The Moondogs, led to the
ring by Mark Canterbury. The hardcore team of Cujo, Spot and Rex did everything
they could to use their power against the Nightmare trio. 

The Youngster Eddie Guerrero started the match with Moondog Rex, it was Rex
who had the upperhand, hitting Eddie with a Jawbreaker, Powerslam and setting
up an early double team to slow down the highflyer. 

Eddie eventually tagged in Danny Davis whos technical skills help even the
playing field against the brutality of the dogs. Danny was able to hit a series
of sentons before Cujo reversed an irish whip that sent Davis into the ref
Tony Faulk. Cujo went for the Cobra Clutch, but no referee was there to call
for a submission.

Eddie made the tag in and hit Cujo with a Shoulderblock, Kneedrop and Bodypress
to get the fans on their feet. Spot made his way into the match and he and Eddie
went toe to toe trading a series in punches and kicks.

Later in the match Spot put a spinning toe hold on Danny Davis, who reversed
the hold with a small package, but he was only able to get a 2 count. 
Frustration set in for Davis who tossed the ref out of the ring. Mike Sparks
made his way down to officiate the match. While there was no ref, Spot grabbed
a dog bone and atempted to hit Guerrero with it. Eddie countered into an armbar
until Tony Faulk was back on the job. Eddie finished the match with a 
headsissors into a dropkick. Once again Rex bumped the ref, but Tony was able
to recover and count the 3 giving the Nightmares and Eddie the win. 

Eddie was definitely green in the match, but Danny Davis was able to captain the
team to a well fought victory.

Brickhouse Brown, who has already punched his ticket to the tournament was
featured in singles compeition. A quick victory showcasing just how powerful
he is,and has heads turning ever since he returned to the territory. 

Our tag team showcase had the New Kids in action. Tony Williams showed off his
athleticism starting things off with a swingging DDT, Handsbring Closeline into
a boston crab submission. The crowd was going wild for The New Kids at this
point, especially the ladies in attendance. Christopher hit the Tennessee Jam for a
two count, and a full nelson faceslam that took the Avenger out of the match. 
Tony Williams hit a wikid kick finisher on Riddle for the win. 

A big match on Championship TV with the Memphis Mafia takes place this week. 
The New Kids will have their work cut out for them, but they are becoming fan
favorites in a very short time in the USWA.

Before our match between Jeff Jarrett and Tom Prichard, our cameras caught Eddie
Gilbert paying Tom Prichard a handful of cash to "take care of Jarrett" in their
semi main event matchup. Tom's technical skills were on full display in this one,
countering almost everything that Jarrett through at him. The Texas Bunch made
their way down to the ring as Eric Embry joined the commentary team during the
match and ran down Jeff Jarrett, while promoting that Terry Funk is returning
this week on Championship TV. 

During the match Jarrett had two nearfalls via sunset flips. Jarrett was 
another star that the fans have really gotten behind. Prichard got Jarrett 
in a sleeperhold, and then hit him with a powerbomb, that had the arena gasp 
thinking Jarrett was done, but he was able to kick out at the 2 1/2 count 
leading to the crowd erupting! 

Prichard went for a second sleeperhold, which Jarrett reversed into an armbar.
Prichard couldn't escape the hold and referee Jerry Calhoon called for the
bell. Embry and Chris Adams bolted for the ring in an attempt to beat down 
Jarrett, who headed to the back unskathed by the faction. A good match infront
of a hot crowd.
