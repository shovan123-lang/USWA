Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
October 25th, 1990
Attendance: 2,000

A large crowd on hand tonight in Louisville to witness Terry Funk pin the
USWA Champion Jerry Lawler in a wild six man tag match. Funk teamed up with
two of the toughest men in the USWA, Dick Slater and Murdoch to defeat Lawler
and his friends Keirn and Dundee. Terry Funk defeating Lawler and laying them
out puts the momentum in the challengers favor heading into their steel cage
match this Monday in Memphis. 

The crowd was stunned as The Midnight Express showed up at the Louisville 
Gardens. Dustin Rhodes won his singles match by DQ when Dennis Condrey and
Bobby Eaton showed up and helped Gilbert put the boots to Dustin Rhodes. Gilbert
took the microphone and said that this would be the start to his rule of the
USWA! 

Jeff Jarrett defeated Billy Joe Travis in singles competition. Billy looked to
be frustrated with this loss to the young upstart. 

The Dirty White Boys defeated the Southern Rockers to retain the tag titles
tonight in tag action Ronnie P. Gossett proclaimed his team to be the best
duo here in the Mid South. 

Robert Fuller took on Sweet Daddy Falcone in singles competition to defend his
USWA Southern Title, keeping his winning streak alive. 

Dutch Mantell opened the evening with a victory over Moondog Rex. Rex is 
beginning a singles career, as Moondog Spike has given notice to the promotion,
and has made his intentions to leave the territory known to Eddie Marlin and
the Executive team.
