Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
September 24th, 1990 
Attendance: 1,923

Bill Dundee and Jerry Lawler get the tag team victory over Snowman and Eddie
Gilbert, despite interference from Dutch Mantel. Dutch attempted to help Gilbert
and Snowman by throwing Gilbert the ringside bell in order for Eddie to use on 
Lawler, but The King intercepted the bell and clocked Gilbert, leadig to the
pinfall victory. 

Nightmare Ken Wayne gets the victory in the first blood match over Tony 
Anthony. Both men did everything they could to draw blood early. The ringside
steps, the announcers table, taking the padding off the turnbuckle post, but
it was Ken Wayne bringing in a pair of brass knuckles and knocking The Dirty
White Boy right in the face that led to the ending. 

The Hardliners picked up an impressive victory fighting the brawling Moondogs,
with their own stlye. It was a brawl that went all over the arena, and ended
with Murdoch and Slater on top. All four men were a bloody mess at the end of
this one.

In other news from tonights show, 

Jeff Jarrett and Gaylord defeated the Uptown Posse after Moondog Rex interfered
in the match. Jarrett took the mic and proclaimed him and Gaylord the number
one contenders to the USWA Tag Titles, and he openly challenged the recently 
debuting Steiner Brothers to prove themselves. 

Steve Keirn, the Fabulous one, picks up a victory tonight. Steve has been
seen on a few cards as of late, but we still don't know what business he has
back in the USWA. 

In the ongoing feud between the Southern Rockers and the Dirty White Boys,
Tom Burton defeated Steven Doll, making him submit to an STF to open the show.
