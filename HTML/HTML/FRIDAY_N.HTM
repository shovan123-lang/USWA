Match observations: 

Louisville, KY
5.24.1991
Attendance: 2381

A wild Tag Match that was announced starting off the show, ends in a 
victory for the Von Erich brothers. Even with interference from the 
Memphis Mafia, Kevin was able to use a camera cable to choke out Lawler,
and grab a win for the Von Erichs in this no countout, no dq match! 

The night before Slamboree saw plenty of twists and turns heading into
the weekend. 

In a battle of the Mafia vs the Texas Bunch, Rex King picks up a win
for the group with constant distractios from the other members. 
King continues to position himself as the face of the Mafia, and this
was a big win going into his match against Kevin Von Erich this week on
Worldwide in a TV title tournament Qualifyer.  

Bill Dundee brings his winning streak to 6 in a row defeating Scotty 
Flamningo, in a match that some are speculating could be the finals of
the TV tournament. 

Bret Hart was scheduled to be in action tonight, but he was brutaly attacked
by Hot Stuff International on his way to the arena. Medical personale on 
site ruled him unable to participate in any in ring action. 

Steve Austin extracted revenge on Danny Davis, who tossed him over the
top at Gauntlet for the Gold. Austin continues to beast down Danny, until
his tag partner Ken Wayne came own to help, but Condrey and Doug Gilbert held
him off so Austin can continue to pummel the bloody Davis. The roar of the
fans, caused Austin to notice Gringos Locos flying down to the ring and  
sent HSI running to the back. Eddie and Art checked on their fallen friends
and helped Danny to his feet. 

Chris Adams and Tojo Yamamoto are on a recuriting mission, and they made it
clear that Sam Houston is on their wish list. Adams painted a picture of
him and Houston as the USWA tag team champions, and told him to think about
what they could do together with proper representation from the Texas Bunch. 

The Steiner Brothers had an excellent match against Honky Tonk man and the
Dirty White boy in their first title defense. Rick took the microphone after
the match and told Spivey and Hanson they would meet them anytime any place!
