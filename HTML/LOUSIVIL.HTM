USWA MAGAZINE: ARENA REPORT
Louisville, Kentucky | Louisville Gardens | July 13, 1991

KERRY STEALS THE SHOW, AND MAYBE THE SPOTLIGHT

Night four of the Red, White and Bruised Tour packed 4,500 fans into
Louisville Gardens, and they went home with plenty to talk about. In a main
event that went nearly twenty-seven minutes, Kerry Von Erich and Unified
World Champion Bret Hart beat Andre the Giant and Jamie Dundee. But it was
how they won that had the building buzzing.

With Paul Ellering doing everything he could from the floor to slow Kerry
down, the Modern Day Warrior finally turned the tide with a tornado punch
that sent Andre crashing out of the ring. Bret had Jamie Dundee trapped in
the middle of the ring and was going in for the finish when Kerry pulled
him off, caught Jamie in a backslide, and took the pin himself at 26:55.

A week after Andre put him to sleep in Memphis, it was the win Kerry
needed. Bret didn't see it that way. On the mic afterward, the Hitman told
his partner "good win," then reminded everyone that he had Jamie beat
before Kerry took the pin. "That's fine," Bret said. Then he shook Kerry's
hand once, walked up the aisle and never looked back. Kerry stayed behind
and celebrated with the fans. Iron Man II at Reunion Arena on August 17 is
a little more personal now.

THE NATURE BOY SPEAKS

Ric Flair held court in the ring before intermission, Big Gold over his
shoulder. Asked what he whispered to Kerry Von Erich in Memphis, Flair just
smiled: "That's between me and Kerry." Then he made it official. Next
Saturday, at the Sportatorium in Dallas, Flair has his first USWA match,
against Terry Funk.

He didn't get the last word. Terry Funk and Kevin Von Erich walked out to
the top of the entrance, and Funk told the Nature Boy the Sportatorium is
his house. Kevin reminded Flair that he cost him at Star Wars. Nobody threw
a punch, but Flair left through the crowd rather than walk past them.

AUSTIN AND STUDD TEAR UP LOUISVILLE

Steve Austin and the Diamond Studd fought for more than twenty-one minutes,
and the referee never got close to counting a pinfall. The brawl spilled to
the floor, and when Studd started to take control on the outside, Missy
Hyatt jumped in to help her man. Neither man made it back inside before the
count of ten: a double count-out. Studd wasn't finished. He powerbombed
Austin through a ringside table, and Missy could only stare at the
wreckage. Austin keeps the Southern Title, but this one isn't over.

CHAMPIONS ROBBED OF A FINISH

Scott Steiner and Tully Blanchard, the team now calling itself Calculated
Force, were less than six minutes into their match with the Memphis Mafia
when Stan Hansen and Dan Spivey stormed the ring. The referee threw the
match out and disqualified Dirty White Boy and Rex King. Then Hansen and
Spivey went to work on the champions with a chain and a dog collar until
Robert Fuller ran in to make the save. Fuller made the save for the same
men who took his tag title spot.

Earlier, Hansen and Spivey sent a message of their own, beating the New
Kids when Hansen powerbombed Tony Williams at 18:06. They want Steiner and
Tully in a dog collar match, and after Louisville, they may get it.

JARRETT GETS EVEN

Jeff Jarrett made Doug Gilbert submit to the Las Vegas Leglock in 17:32.
Eddie Gilbert, at ringside for his brother, grabbed the mic afterward to
remind everyone he beat Jarrett in the Star Wars cage, and said Jarrett's
Unified Title shot belongs to him. Jarrett didn't answer. He didn't have
to. Somebody was going to need help getting Doug to the back.

ALSO IN LOUISVILLE

Jim Powers opened the show with a strong win over Sweet Daddy Falcone,
hitting a flying cross body at 15:08. Falcone shook his hand afterward.
That's two straight wins for the newcomer. Scotty Flamingo made it two for
two in his Invitational Series, but he needed a low blow to put away Scott
Taylor at 17:09. Sam Houston watched it all from the aisle and stared
Flamingo down on his way out. The Nightmares, the number one contenders to
the tag titles, beat Eric Embry and Tom Prichard of the Texas Outlaws when
Ken Wayne hit the Flying Stomp at 15:29.

NEXT: DALLAS

The tour moves to Jonesboro on Wednesday for Moondog Spot vs. Mark
Canterbury, then to the Sportatorium in Dallas next Saturday for Ric Flair
vs. Terry Funk.
