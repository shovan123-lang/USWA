HONKY TONK MAN AND MARK CANTERBURY
Evansville, Veterans Memorial Coliseum | Segment 5 (4:00)

The Moondogs are still in the ring, barking over the jobbers they just
flattened. The Honky Tonk Man struts out with his guitar and a mic,
swiveling his hips as he joins them.

HONKY TONK MAN: Thank you, thank you very much! Now that's what I call a
performance! You see that, Evansville? That's the sound of two dogs that
finally found a real master.

Rex and Spot bark and pound the mat.

HONKY TONK MAN: Now, I know you people miss old Mark Canterbury. The dog
catcher who lost his dogs! Mark spent all those years trying to keep these
boys on a leash, and the minute they got a taste of the Honky Tonk Man,
they knew they'd been barking up the wrong tree.

HONKY TONK MAN: Let's be honest. Mark Canterbury is a nobody without these
two. He's out there now cutting promos in a dark locker room, saying he's a
man on a mission. Mark, the only mission you're on is looking for a job.
I'm the coolest, rockinest, rollinest Honky Tonk Man there ever was, and
there ain't nothing you can do about it!

Eddie Marlin walks out to the top of the aisle with a mic. The crowd
cheers.

EDDIE MARLIN: Honky, I've heard just about enough. Mark Canterbury has been
asking me for a match, and I'm going to give him one. Next week, at the
Earl Bell Community Center in Jonesboro, it'll be Moondog Spot against Mark
Canterbury!

The crowd pops. Honky's smile drops. Before he can answer, Mark Canterbury
charges down the aisle, his hands taped, and slides into the ring. The
Moondogs back into the corner. Honky hides behind them, guitar raised.

MARK CANTERBURY: You think squashing nobodies makes you tough? Try facing
the man you betrayed! I fed you. I put you on TV. I dragged you out of the
gutter when nobody else would touch you!

Mark steps toward them. Spot and Rex slide out under the bottom rope and
pull Honky out with them.

MARK CANTERBURY: That's right, run! Run, Spot! Next week, there's nowhere
to go. Next week it's you and me, and I'm gonna remind you who held the
leash!

Mark stands alone in the ring as the Moondogs and Honky back up the aisle,
yelling at him the whole way.
