Match observations: 

USWA Fully Loaded
March 4th, 1991
Mid-South Coliseum
Attendance 10,000

With a sold out Mid South Coliseum, Bret Hart was crowned the third ever Unified
champion. Our main event was absolutely wild. Andre the Giant came out to the 
ring with Terry Funk and the Hardliners at his side. Out comes the challenger,
Bret Hart and along side Bret is Davey Boy Smith! Before the bell Eddie
Marlin came to the ring and announced to the fans this match would be no 
countout and no disqualification and we must have a winner by pinfall or 
submission.

Bret Hart attempted the Sharpshooter on several occations, but couldn't 
lock on it on Andre's massive legs. The Hardliners mauled Bret Hart outside
the ring, and Davey Boy Smith made the save, even giving Dick Slater a 
running bulldog on the entrance ramp. 

All Hell broke loose as constant interference by Funk and the Hardliners had
Bret fighting off everyone, until he was able to apply an inside cradle and
shocked the world, defeating Andre and becoming the USWA champion. After
the match Davey Boy Smith and Bret made their way back to the curtain, while
Terry Funk and the Hardliners berated Andre for losing the title. Terry
Funk SLAPPED Andre across the mouth and thats when the Giant snapped! 
Andre would chokeslam Funk and double clothesline the Hardliners before leaving
the ring. 

A wild wild main event with our new champion. 

In other action tonight, Jerry Lawler and Dutch Mantell defeated Funk and
Bill Dundee. This match stole the show as they had a wild brawl all over
the ringside area. Dundee and Dutch have taken their rivalry to the extreme,
and something must be done to end this blood feud. 

Hot Stuff International started the night off with a six man tag match, 
defeating the Moondogs and the Spellbinder. Eddie Gilbert took the mic
and said later tonight Austin will defend the Southern title and it will
be a clean sweep for the crew. 

The Hardliners defeated the Southern Rockers climbing their way back up
the ladder in the tag team scene. 

In our semi main event, the Steiner Brothers defeated The Von Erichs in one
of their toughest challenges to date. At the end of the match all 4 members
shook hands and left the ring together. The Steiners have been one of the
most winningest teams in the short history of the USWA. It will be interesting
to see whats next for the tag team champions. 

Honky Ton Man defeated Joey Maggs in singles competition, and Scotty Flamingo
would go on to defeate Kawada earlier in the evening. 

In our Southern Title Match, Steve Austin defeated Jeff Jarrett to retain
the championship. Sam Houston made his surprising return to the USWA, 
bad back and all, Houston would attack Austin during the match, helping 
Jarrett get the upper hand. In the end Austin would get the pinfall
victory over Jarrett and is moving on as champion.
