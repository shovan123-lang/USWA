Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
October 29th, 1990 
Attendance: 4,500

A great crowd on hand witness what truly will be Grave Consequences for Terry
Funk and the Hardliners. All roads now lead to the spectacle of the fall season,
Thanksgiving Thunder at the Louisville Gardens. 

Jerry Lawler once again retains the USWA Championship, but it didn't come 
without another brutal attack by Terry Funk and the Hardliners. After 
Lawler pinned a bloody Terry Funk, Dick Murdoch led the charge for the 
Hardliners to rip the door off the cage and ambush Lawler from behind.
It was Slater who then chained the door back on and the trio once again beat
Lawler in the middle of the ring.
Lawler would feel a new level of pain as Dirty Dick Slater pulled out a strand
of barbed wire and raked it over the forehead of the USWA champion. Officials
attempted to break the door down on the cage to end the violence, but it wasn't
until The Hardliners and Funk were finished with Lawler that the medical 
officials could hit the ring and help the fallen champion. 

Eddie Gilbert formally introduced his wrestling stable as Hot Stuff 
International,and he told the wrestling world tonight that they should get 
familiar with those three words. Dustin Rhodes introduced Joey Maggs and 
Billy Joe Travis has his partners, as the three made a pretty good team for
Condrey and Eaton to deal with. It wasn't until Billy Joe Travis went for his
guitar, and everyone thought the end was near for Eddie Gilbert, when Travis
turned on his partners smashing the guitar over the head of Dustin Rhodes.
Travis would go on to maul Joey Maggs and this led to Eddie Gilbert getting a
pinfall victory over Dustin once again. The beating wouldn't last long as
the Southern Champion Robert Fuller made his way down to the ring, trailed
by The Nightmares who cleared the ring of Hot Stuff International. Fuller
promised on TV he would have Dustins back and he saved him for another 
beating at the hands of H.S.I. 

In a special challenge match The Southern Rockers upset Action Jackson and
The Snowman in tag team competition. 

Moondog Rex picked up another victory over The Masked Avenger. 

Simply Irresistabel Jeff Jarrett picked up one of the bigger wins of his young
career defeating Brickhouse Brown.

Newcomer to the USWA Scott Taylor made his debut over Duane Gill in our opener.
