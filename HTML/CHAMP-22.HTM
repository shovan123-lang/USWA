Championship TV Recap:

The Hardliners may have opened a can of worms they didn't want to do after
getting involved in Terry Funk and Jerry Lawlers match at Grave Consequences. 

Tonight, as the Hardliners are continuing to build a resume as the number
one team in the USWA, they took on the Steiner Brothers, but picked up a loss
when Lawler and Steve Kerin got involved in the match. Lawler and Kerin are
Scheduled to face The Hardliners in singles and tag matches all around the
territory this week. 

The Top Prospect Tournament was announced on TV and we had Scott Taylor in 
action today. Plenty of USWA superstars will participate in the big night
of action next weekend in Evansville. More Tournament participants will be
in Evansville this week to highlight the talent that will be involved.

Sweet Daddy Falcone attacked MoonDog Rex today, taking his dogbone and leaving
him laying on the floor. It seems like Falcone took a payoff from Dutch Mantel
to do the dirty work today on television. 

Rhodes, Fuller and the Nightmares had their hands raised over Hot Stuff 
International last Friday. They will continue their feuds this week.

Louisville is the place to be this week when Lawler and Kerin go for the #1
contender spot against the Hardliners!
