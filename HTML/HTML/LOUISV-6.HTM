Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
October 18th, 1990
Attendance: 984

Terry Funk was unsuccessful in defeating the champion Jerry Lawler in 
tonights Main Event. Funk pulled out all the stops in this bloody encounter,
even wrapping Lawlers leg around the ring post and attacking it with a nearby
steel chair. 20 minutes into this physical battle, Lawler pulled out a chain
and hit Funk between the eyes, busting him open even worse than he already was.
Lawler hit the fistdrop to retain the title, leaving the Gardens champion once
again. 

Action Jackson and The Snowman have been teaming up as of late. They are 
claiming to be the strongest tag team in the USWA, and one team has taken 
exception to that. In our Semi Main event, The Steiner Brothers defeated
Snowman and Action Jackson. Rick Steiner wrestled circles around Action Jackson
until he was able to hit him with the Steiner Bulldog to get the pin. As 
the Steiner walked back to the locker room, Snowman vowed that this wasn't
the last the Steiners have heard from them. 

After weeks of matches that led to determining a top contender, Robert Fuller
passed all the tests to earn tonights Southern Title match against Dick Slater,
the first ever champion. In 13 minutes Robert Fuller made the champion submit to 
the Fuller Leg Lock to a ruckus crowd on hand. Slater couldn't hold on once
Fuller had the hold on in the middle of the ring. Dick Murdoch came down to 
check on his fallen tag partner as Fuller celebrated his championship in the
crowd with the Louisville crowd. 

In other action from Louisville tonight, Dustin Rhodes defeated Eddie Gilbert.
Gilbert continues to cry about consperiacy theroies as to why he is not the
Unified Champion. Dustin shut him up at least for one night in Louisville. 

Dick Murdoch defeated Bill Dundee in singles competition. Murdoch really 
toyed with Dundee in this one. Too much power and too much strength for
Bill Dundee tonight.
