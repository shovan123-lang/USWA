THE TEXAS OUTLAWS
Jonesboro, AR, Earl Bell Community Center | Segment 5 (4:00)

Corey Maclin is in the ring. Terry Funk walks out first, followed by Chris
Adams, Eric Embry, Tom Prichard and, last, Kevin Von Erich. Kevin is still
favoring the left leg. Embry has the World Class flag draped over his
shoulder.

COREY MACLIN: Terry Funk, the Texas Bunch, and joining them tonight, Kevin
Von Erich. Terry, what is...

TERRY FUNK: Corey, the Texas Bunch is dead. I buried it. Texas Bunch sounds
like something you'd sell at a church picnic. These boys aren't a bunch of
anything.

TERRY FUNK: Every man standing in this ring got run out of somewhere. World
Class went under and these promoters looked at us like we were yesterday's
newspaper. Well, yesterday's newspaper is back, and it's mad as hell. From
this night on, we are the Texas Outlaws!

ERIC EMBRY: And Memphis found out on Monday what happens when you cross us.
Sweet Daddy Falcone is still trying to figure out what hit him.

The crowd boos as Embry waves the flag.

COREY MACLIN: Kevin, a lot of people never thought they'd see you standing
alongside Terry Funk.

KEVIN VON ERICH: Corey, I made Terry a deal. If I lost, I'd ride with him.
I lost. My daddy raised me to keep my word, so here I am.

KEVIN VON ERICH: But let's be clear about why I lost. Terry didn't beat me
on Monday. Ric Flair did. I turned my head for one second because the
Nature Boy decided to stick his nose where it doesn't belong.

TERRY FUNK: And that's the part I can't stand, Corey. I didn't ask for any
help from Ric Flair. Me and Ric go back a long, long way, and Ric knows it.
He wants to come down here and play games with my family? Then he's gonna
have to deal with all of us.

TERRY FUNK: Flair, you've got the gold belt and the fancy robes. We've got
nothing to lose. You think about that.

CHRIS ADAMS: And for anybody else who wants a piece of the Outlaws, I've
got a match later tonight. Whoever they put across from me is gonna find
out the hard way.

The Outlaws raise their fists together as the boos rain down.
