WELCOME TO LOUISVILLE GARDENS
Louisville, Louisville Gardens | Segment 1 (5:00)

The house lights drop. A spotlight hits the ring. The ring announcer stands
in the center with a microphone, and the crowd of 4,500 is already on its
feet.

RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, good evening, and welcome to
Louisville Gardens! The United States Wrestling Association is proud to
present night four of the Red, White and Bruised Tour!

The crowd roars.

RING ANNOUNCER: Tonight, you are going to see one of the biggest cards this
building has seen all summer. Let me tell you about tonight's main event.
Ladies and gentlemen, the Unified World Heavyweight Champion, Bret "The
Hitman" Hart, teams with the Modern Day Warrior, Kerry Von Erich!

Huge cheers. A few Hart signs and a lot more Von Erich signs go up.

RING ANNOUNCER: They will face the Eighth Wonder of the World, Andre the
Giant, and Jamie Dundee, with Paul Ellering!

Loud boos, louder for Jamie than for Andre.

RING ANNOUNCER: Also tonight, the USWA Southern Heavyweight Champion,
"Stunning" Steve Austin, with Missy Hyatt, goes one on one with the Diamond
Studd! The USWA Tag Team Champions, Scott Steiner and Tully Blanchard, are
in action. So are Stan Hansen and Dan Spivey, Jeff Jarrett, the Nightmares,
Scotty Flamingo, and the newest star in the USWA, Jim Powers!

RING ANNOUNCER: And, ladies and gentlemen, tonight, live, in this ring, we
will hear from the Nature Boy, Ric Flair!

The building pops, then a wave of "Wooo!" rolls through the crowd.

RING ANNOUNCER: Merchandise tables are open in the lobby, and there will be
a fifteen-minute intermission. So get comfortable, Louisville. Let's get it
started! Our opening contest is scheduled for one fall, with a twenty-
minute time limit!
