Friday Night Fights on June 28, 1991, in Louisville delivered a gritty,
storyline-heavy card that leaned into chaos and character tension. 
Andre the Giant opened the show with a countout win over Bill Dundee, who 
retained his World Television Title by escaping the giant's grasp outside 
the ring. Dundee retains, at the cost of serious physical damage. 

Brickhouse Brown followed with a quick, emphatic win over Haruka Eigen, 
while Stan Hansen’s bout with Robert Fuller turned volatile-ending in a DQ 
after interference muddied the waters around Scott Steiner’s tag partner 
stakes. With Fullers loss, Scott is left without a partner to defend the 
tag titles with at Star Wars. 

The Moondogs tore through Oliver Peters and Red Tyler in a wild No-DQ sprint, 
with Spot cinching the win via figure-four. Backstage, tensions escalated 
between Jerry Lawler and Kerry Von Erich, foreshadowing their later clash. 

The eight-man tag featuring Hot Stuff International, The Nightmares, 
The New Kids, and Los Gringos Locos ended in a time-limit draw, but not 
before showcasing Eddie Guerrero’s aerial finesse and Brian Christopher’s 
fiery offense. Scott Taylor scored a lightning-fast win over Corey Carpenter, 
and Kerry Von Erich got a measure of revenge by defeating Lawler via 
countout—though the finish left room for future escalation.

The night closed with a brutal No Holds Barred war between Jeff Jarrett and 
Eddie Gilbert, where Jarrett secured the number-one contender spot after a 
bodypress in a match that spilled across the arena.

With a ***½ overall card
rating, the show balanced fast-paced action with storyline progression, 
setting the stage for major developments in the Southern title scene and 
tag division.
