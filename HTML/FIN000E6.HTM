delight. Paul Ellering asked the fans if they wanted the match to end, with that
out came Andre the Giant to a huge ovation. Andre cleared the ring of Alan and
Ernesto, until The Hardliners made their way to the ring. The Hardliners cornered
Andre, but Davey Boy Smith ran down to even the odds. 

The match saw Davey Boy Smith execute his running powerslam on Dick
Murdoch for the victory.
