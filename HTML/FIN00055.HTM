Ring Announcer David Wolff asks the fan to give a warm welcome to 
BoBo Brazil! 

The fans go wild for this announcement as BoBo has the respect of all the fans 
in the mid south. 

BoBo Brazil takes the mic and thanks all the fans for supporting him
throughout his career. BoBo says he has been watching USWA wrestling and
can't help but notices a man that is coming up in the territory, and 
knows that with a little guidance, he can be a future world champion,
that man is Brickhouse Brown! 

Out comes Brickhouse from the back as the fans cheer him on.

Brickhouse says it's an honor to meet BoBo and appreciates the comments. 
Brickhouse says that he has aspirations of holding gold in the USWA, and
that starts tonight, when he wins the USWA Television title! Bobo tells
Brickhouse that he has been watching Doug Gilbert, and Scotty Flamingo
and knows they are always looking for an edge! He will be ringside and has
his back tonight!
