Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens 
September 6th, 1990
Attendance: 550

Bill Dundee and Jerry Lawler teamed up tonight and picked up a victory over 
"Hot Stuff" Eddie Gilbert and an old foe of Lawler, Dirty Dutch Mantell. 
Lawler gave Eddie Gilbert a taste of his own medicine when he hit him with 
the Piledriver for the victory in our tag team main event. Bill Dundee thanked 
Lawler for helping him extract a little revenge on Gilbert tonight. 

The Dirty White Boys and The Nightmares faced off after both of them played
prominent rolls in the Gilbert/Dundee match at the Mid South Coliseum. 
The rule book was thrown out early as both teams brawled around the arena,
leading to a double countout. 

Dustin Rhodes helped Jeff Jarrett and The Missouri Tiger Jeff Gaylord pick up
a win tonight. Dick Slater had Gaylord set up to execute a flying knee drop,
when Dustin Rhodes ran out and pushed him off the top turnbuckle. This let
Gaylord make the hot tag to Jarrett who hit his missle dropkick from the 
second rope for the victory. Dick Murdoch was not happy with Rhodes getting
involved in Hardliners business! 

The Uptown Posse pick up a win over The Southern Rockers when Sweet Daddy
Falcone rolled up Rex king in a small package. Falcone called for Reggie B.
Fine to throw him his cain and he was about to use it in King when Steven Doll
grabbed the cain from behind and cleared the ring.
