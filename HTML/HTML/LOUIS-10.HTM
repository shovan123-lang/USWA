Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
February 22nd 1991
Attendance: 1,001

For the first time in the Lousiville Gardens, Bret Hart faced Terry Funk in 
our main event. Unfortunately for the fans, it was a quick ending as Hart 
beat Funk by countout. Hart is two weeks away from his matchup with Andre
The Giant for a shot at the Unified title. Hart will be on Championship TV
this week to have a face to face discussion with Eddie Marlin. 

The Von Erich brothers looked great tonight with a victory over the Twilight Zone.
All roads lead to a tag title match coming up in two weeks. 

The Honky Tonk Man was back in town and took a victory over Action Jackson. 
Honky Tonk told the fans he has a bone to pick with Joey Maggs. 

Lawler was back in action tonight taking on his long time rival Bill Dundee.
Lawler picked up the victory and the fans went wild. 

Mark Canterbury made it back to the Gardens and defeated Sweet Daddy Falcone
in singles competition. 

After a time limit draw in Memphis, Kawada defeated the Dirty White Boy in 
singles competiiton. Kawada has come up with some big wins as of late, and 
will soon be taking on some stiffer competition. 

The Souther Rockers continue their winning streak with a victory over the 
Moondogs. The Rockers continue to climb the ladder and put their names in the
hat for a title match. 

Doug Gilbert in his second match back in the promotion defeated the Spellbinder
in our opener. Dennis Condrey was in Gilberts corner tonight, and their is a lot
of speculation that Doug will be joining his brother Eddie in Hot Stuff 
International.
