Match observations: 

Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Coliseum
October 16th, 1990
Attendance: 1500

The USWA draws it's largest crowd in Evansville headlined by Jerry Lawler 
defending the USWA Unified Title against Terry Funk. A bigtime Main Event
for Evansville. 

Lawler gets the pinfall and defends the title over Funk to send the crowd home
happy. A bloody Terry Funk continued to go after Lawler after the match, but
he was in no condition to continue after a brutal piledriver by the champion. 

The Dirty White Boys defended the tag titles over Jarrett and Gaylord clean
in the ring holding off the number one contenders for another night. 

Eddie Gilbert proclaimed to the Evansville crowd that he should be the USWA
champion, but it was Dustin Rhodes who came out to shut him up. Gilbert 
attacked Rhodes from behind and hit him with a hotshot to get a quick victory.

Our opening match would finally determine the #1 contender to the Southern
Title. That match took place with Robert Fuller defeating Billy Joe Travis.
After the match the southern champion Dick Slater came in the ring and had 
a staredown with Robert Fuller. They will meet in Louisville on Thursday night. 


The HARDLINERS impressed in tag team competition gaining a victory over
the Nightmares who just defeated the Uptown Posse in Memphis last night. 
Dick Slater and Dick Murdoch continue to be one of the most dominant teams
in the territory. 

Snowman and Action Jackson defeated the Steiner Brothers tonight in their 
biggest win since beginning to team. Danny Davis of the Nightmares got involved
in the match as a little revenge from Memphis.
