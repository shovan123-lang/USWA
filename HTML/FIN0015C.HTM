Upon the return from commercial break, Dustin Rhodes is with Dave Brown
at the announce table. Rhodes talks about being promised a rematch for the 
Southern Heavyweight Championship without having to worry about Dick Murdoch
saving the champion, but that wasn't the case. 

Dave Brown says he has good news for Dustin as he just got word that he will
get that rematch with Slater this week at the Mid South Coliseum, and Eddie
Marlin has confirmed that a SPECIAL GUEST REFEREE will be in the match to 
make sure Murdoch doesn't get involved. 

Robert Fuller makes his way out to a nice crowd reaction. Fuller announces
that he will be the special guest referee, and if Murdoch thinks he is going
to change the outcome of the match this monday night, to think again. Fuller
said he will take his job seriously and we will have a fair outcome! 

Out come the Hardliners Dick Slater and Murdoch who start complaining. Slater
says that Robert Fuller couldn't possibly referee a match fairly. 

The tension rises between all four men until Dirty Dick Slater cheap shots
Robert Fuller with the Southern Heavyweight Championship Belt! 

Wrestlers and executives from the back come out to break up the fight, but 
Fuller warns Slater that later today he will get his payback when he takes
him out of the Championship Tournament.
