Match observations: 

USWA Worldwide Taping
4.24.1991
Jonesboro, AR
Attendance: 550
ASU Convocation Center

In a matchup between The Texas Bunch and the Memphis Mafia, Tom Prichard picked 
up the win over Dirty Dick Slater hitting him with a powerbomb for the pinfall
victory. 

Moondog Cujo was spotlighted in singles action, hitting his opponent with a 
dog bone when the referee wasn't looking. 

The Nightmares continue to show why they are former USWA tag champions. Danny
Davis and Ken Wayne will be back next week in a featured match against The
Moondogs. 

Rex King got his first singles action since the departure of Steve Doll and
looked very impressive. Rex has aligned himself with the Memphis Mafia and
should be in line for a qualifying match at the Television Title. 

We heard from Doug Gilbert who also referenced his desire to compete for 
the new championship. Gilber represents Hot Stuff International and says 
they are on a mission to hold all the gold in the USWA! 



In some backstage news and notes, Promotor Eddie Marlin has signed a new deal 
with both the Soldiers and Sailers Memorial Coliseum in Evansville, and The
ASU convocation Center located on the campus of Arkansas State University to
host events every other Wednesday for a new syndicated program, USWA WORLDWIDE.
Worldwide will focus in on showcasing USWA talent and identifying up and
coming talent to the Association. 

Each and every week we will have a featured matchup. Along with this new venture,
Eddie Marlin announced the debut of the USWA Television Title! This new 
championship will be up for grabs in the coming weeks in an 8 man tournament
to take place at a later date. Qualifying matches will take place on Worldwide
for the next several weeks.
