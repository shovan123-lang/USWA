Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
November 22nd, 1990
Attendance: 6,000
Super NO Vacancy

Thanksgiving Thunder is in the books and what a memorable night it was as 
Jerry The King Lawler defeated Terry Funk inside the barbed wire ring.
Lawler overcame all odds and overcame the largest of odds when Andre The
Giant made his way down to the ring and attacked the King during the match.
Lawler attempted a fireball on the Giant but it had no effect. He was still
able to distract the Giant in order to hit Funk with the Piledriver and retain
his unified title. We should learn more about Andre and his motives in the
weeks to come. 

Eddie Gilbert, the leader of Hot Stuff International defeated Robert Fuller
to become the Southern Heavyweight champion. A great match that ended with
the top prospect Jerry Lawler coming down the aisle and making sure Eddie knows
he is in line for a chance at that title. 

In an amazing match, the Steiner Brothers defeated the Hardliners in a no 
countout no DQ matchup. These two teams will meet on TV this week to open
the Tag League and the Hardliners will have a chance at revenge! 

The Midnight Express defeated the Tag Champion Nightmares by DQ when the Dirty
White Boys came in and attacked the tag champions. The Express were not 
happy with the interference from the former champions and all three teams 
began to argue in the ring. 

Dustin Rhodes defeated Billy Joe Travis inside the steel cage, but Hot Stuff
International still left the Gardens on top with the Southern Title later
in the evening. 

Gaylord and Jarrett continue their hot streak defeating another hot team
the Southern Rockers. The tag team scene has never been hotter, and it's 
a perfect time for tag league to start.
