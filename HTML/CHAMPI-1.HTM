Match observations: 

USWA Championship Wrestling| June 29th, 1991
Memphis, TN |Venue: Mid South Coliseum 
Attendance: 6,500 AVG: Ticket $12.00
Gate: $78,000 Broadcast: Live weekly TV

Dave Brown moderates a tense face-to-face between Bill Dundee and 
Jamie Dundee.

Jamie, flanked by Paul Ellering, continues to dismiss 
his father’s legacy, claiming Ellering is the true father figure.


Bill fires back, revealing his mystery partner for Star Wars: 
Jimmy Valiant.

The segment ends with a heated stare-down, setting the 
emotional tone for their tag match against Jamie and Andre the Giant.

Brickhouse Brown & Jeff Jarrett vs. Hot Stuff International


Result: Brickhouse pins Steve Austin with a rollaway at 14:43.
Notes: Missy Hyatt interferes against Brickhouse, but he overcomes the 
odds.Brickhouse gains major momentum heading into Star Wars; Austin and Gilbert 
show cracks under pressure.

Fuller picked up a win in singles action. He's looking to get back on track
after his loss in Louisville, knocking him out of the tag team title scene. 
After the match Fuller told the fans that he let them down, but at Star Wars
like it or not, he will be in Scott Steiners corner. 

Dutch Mantell joined the texas bunch to take on Dirty White Boy and Rex King.
Ronnie P. Gossett and Dirty White Girl both got involved, but Mantell 
and Embry persevere. Mantell and Embry solidify themselves as a serious threat 
to the mafia heading into Star Wars.



The Dirty White Boy picked up a singles victory tonight. Dirty White Girl again 
plays a role, continuing the Mafia’s dirty tactics. Scott Taylor shows heart 
but remains outgunned; Mafia continues to be a dominant force. 

The Moondogs won a quick squash match. The dogs continue to look like 
dangerous opponents in the tag team division. 

Dave Brown moderated an in ring face to face confrontation bettween Champion
and Challenger. Both men were respectful but intense. Bret focused on his
keys to victory being percision and legacy, while Kerry emphasized 
heart and pride! There was no physical interaction, but the crowd was 
electric as both men vow to leave it all in the ring at Star Wars. 

In the main event, Chris Adams gets the win over Kevin Von Erich while
the Texas bunch hovered around the ring the entire time. Terry Funk
took the mic after the match and told Kevin that when they compete at
Star Wars, if Funk wins, Kevin must join him in leading the Texas Bunch!
