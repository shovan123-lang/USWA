JIMMY VALIANT AND BILL DUNDEE
Jonesboro, Earl Bell Community Center | Segment 8 (3:30)

Backstage. Corey Maclin stands with Jimmy Valiant, a wide bandage taped
across his forehead, and Bill Dundee beside him.

COREY MACLIN: Jimmy, this is the first time we've heard from you since
Jamie Dundee attacked you with a chain in Memphis. How are you feeling?

JIMMY VALIANT: Corey, I've been hit with a lot of things in my life, baby.
Chairs, boots, tables, a few ex-wives. But I never got hit from behind by
a boy I was trying to help. That's the one that stings.

JIMMY VALIANT: I walked over there to calm that young man down. I've known
Jamie since he was this tall, running around the back of the Coliseum. And
he wrapped a chain around his fist and laid me out.

COREY MACLIN: Jamie Dundee says you're a washed-up legend his father picked
over his own blood. And he wants you in Dallas Saturday night, no
disqualification.

JIMMY VALIANT: Then he's got me. No DQ. No rules. No Paul Ellering hiding
behind the referee. Saturday night at the Sportatorium, Jamie, the Boogie
Woogie Man is coming for you, and I'm bringing everything I've got.

Bill Dundee puts a hand on Valiant's shoulder.

BILL DUNDEE: Corey, Jimmy's fighting a fight that should've been mine. And
I'll be right there in Dallas to make sure Ellering keeps his hands to
himself. Jamie, you remember something. Saturday is Jimmy's. But August
the third at the Mid South Coliseum, you get your daddy, and you get this
title shot. Whatever Jimmy leaves of you, I'll take care of the rest.

JIMMY VALIANT: Woooo, baby! Dallas!

Valiant points at the camera and walks off with Dundee.
