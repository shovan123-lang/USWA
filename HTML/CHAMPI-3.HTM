Championship TV recap:
Season 2- Episode 23

Bret Hart fended off Jerry Lawler in tonights main event. A victory by
Jerry Lawler in this Proving Ground match would have led him to a future 
Heavyweight title match. Kerry Von Erich was ringide at the announcers desk to
watch the match from the front row. Kerry gave credit to Bret for being a 
great wrestler, but he vowed to be the next USWA heavyweight champion. 

When the match was over, Kerry hit the ring to shake hands with Bret. Dutch
Mantell made his way down to check on Lawler, who was furious with this loss.
Jerry took the mic and said he has had enough of the Von Erichs and the Harts
and he threw out a challenge for next week at the Coliseum. A tag team tornado
cage match! Dutch and Lawler vs Kerry and Bret! Even Dutch Mantell was a little
caught offguard by that one. 

Steve Austin had an easy night at the office showcasing many of his devistating
offensive maneuvers in an open challenge. Missy Hyatt was ringside and was 
showing off Austins new wrestling robe he gave him. Steve noticed there were
missing diamonds inside the robe and shrugged it off, but did look frustrated
with Hyatt's poor quality clothing. 

Bill Dundee stirred up some frustration from Scotty Flamingo, when he praised
Kawada for his victory last week, knowing full well that his interference is
what cost Scotty the victory. Flamingo made it personal when he accused
Bill Dundee of being a worthless father to his son! Flamingo let the world
know that Jamie Dundee is his son and he doesn't even aknowledge him! 

Dundee had enough of Flamingo and the two had to be seperated. These two
will face off in the first round of the TV title tournament in less than
a week in Nashville. 


Eddie Marlin was on hand this week and requested that Scott Steiner 
bring the tag titles to the arena so they could officially be vacated.
Scotty told Eddie that it isn't right that he has to let these titles
go, because they were never pinned in the ring! Marlin said their is no
choice. Robert Fuller made his way down to the ring and told Scotty that
he isn't exactly the biggest fan of Hansen and Spivey and if Marlin would 
allow it, he will be Scotts partner at Star Wars! 

 
Terry Funk and Kevin Von Erich continue to have tension with each other as 
Funk is growing impatient with Kevin not joining his faction. Kevin will
take on Eric Embry in the first round of the TV tournament this Friday! 

Eddie Gilbert had an opportunity to talk about the recent happenings in
Hot Stuff International. Eddie gave a warning to Jeff Jarrett that after
he attacked his brother, he's out for revenge!
