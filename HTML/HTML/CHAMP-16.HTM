Match observations: 

Championship TV Recap:

Jeff Jarrett defended his Southern Title in todays Main Event, but it wasn't
the type of victory he was looking for. The referee disqualified Scotty Flamingo
when Eddie Gilbert came to ringside and blasted Jarrett with a chair. 
Missy Hyatt, who made her USWA debut tonight was a major distraction at ringside
throughout the match. Gilbert is looking for a rematch for the Southern Title,
and he is going to get it this Friday in Louisville, KY. 

Bret Hart finally has a date for his Unified Title match against Andre The Giant.
March 4th at the Mid South Coliseum. Terry Funk couldn't leave well enough alone
and ended up getting involved in Hart's business. They will meet 1 on 1 this
Monday at the Mid South Coliseum. 

In other action today, The Spellbinder picked up a victory over Sweet Daddy 
Falcone. Nebula of the Twilight Zone came out and attacked Falcone, until
Falcone attempted to unmask him. Twilight Zone will take on Falcone and a 
partner of his choice at the Mid South Coliseum. 

Mark Canterbury defeated Kawada in singles competition. These two youngsters
will be competing all week around the territory. 

The Hardliners were in tag team competition tonight and looked devistating. 
It was announced that on Jonesboro this Wednesday we will have a 6 team 
gauntlet to crown the number one contenders to the Steiners tag titles.
