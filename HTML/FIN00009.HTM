HOT STUFF HAS A PROBLEM
Jonesboro, Earl Bell Community Center | Segment 10 (2:00)

The referee raises Brickhouse Brown's hand. Dennis Condrey rolls to the
floor, holding his ribs. Doug Gilbert storms around the ring to him, with
Missy Hyatt hurrying behind.

DAVE BROWN: Brickhouse Brown with the win, and Dennis Condrey is going to
hear about it. Look at Doug Gilbert!

Doug gets in Condrey's face, shouting and jabbing a finger at him. Condrey
straightens up and shouts back. The ring mic catches part of it.

DOUG GILBERT: Saturday night, Dennis! We've got Brickhouse Saturday night
and you just made him look like a million dollars!

DENNIS CONDREY: I've been doing this since you were in diapers, kid. Don't
you tell me how to work.

Doug shoves Condrey back a step. Missy jumps between them, one hand on each
man's chest, and pushes Doug toward the aisle.

MISSY HYATT: Not here! Not in front of them!

Doug walks off first, still yelling. Condrey stands alone at ringside for
a moment, then picks up his jacket and follows a few steps behind.

LANCE RUSSELL: Dave, I've never seen that before. Dennis Condrey has been
with Hot Stuff International from the start.

DAVE BROWN: And Brickhouse Brown and Diamond Studd get the Gilberts on
Saturday. If that's how Hot Stuff walks into Dallas, they've got trouble.
