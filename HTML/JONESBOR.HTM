Match observations: 

Jonesboro, AR
February 6th, 1991
Attendance: 1,003
ASU Convication Center

Our long awaited retun to Jonesboro saw a big time main event. A Six team
gauntlet match for the Tag Team Number one contenders spot on the line.
The Nightmares left the gauntlet with the victory and will once again face
the Steiners for the tag titles in the upcoming weeks ahead. 

Jerry Lawler returned to action defeating Scotty Flamingo with the piledriver.
Bill Dundee was seen around ringside during the match. 

Dutch Mantel gets another victory this week, defeating Sam Houston. Bill Dundee
was also seen ringside during this matchup. 

The Honkey Tonk Man came out and ran down Jonesboro and it's fans. he challenged
anyone in the back to face him tonight. Joey Maggs took him up on his offer and
pinned the loud mouth! 

The Steiners looked better than ever in tag team action tonight. 

Sweet Daddy Falcone picked Perry Action Jackson as his partner to face the 
Twilight Zone. these two teams went to a 20 minute draw. Action Jackson
was close to unmasking Nebula, and it seems like everyone in the USWA wants
to unmask this duo. 

Kawada defeated Mark Canterbury in our opening match.
