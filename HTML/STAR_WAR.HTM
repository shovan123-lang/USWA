Match observations: 

USWA Star Wars 1991| July 1st,1991
Memphis, TN |Venue: Mid South Coliseum 
Attendance: 10,085 AVG: Ticket $12.00
Gate: $121,020.00, Broadcast: Pay Per View.

The Mid South Coliseum was rocking from bell to bell as the USWA roster 
collided across 10 matches in what some are calling the biggest event
in the short history of the USWA. 

Rapid fire tag action, high impact submission holds, and a steel cage brawl kept
the rowdy Memphis crowd invested from the opening armlock to the final 
sharpshooter. The overall card earned 3 1/4 stars from the prominent wrestling 
journalists across the globe.

Bret Hart faces his toughest challenger to date for the USWA Unified title. 
In a deliberate, technical masterclass, Hart methodically wore down Kerry's
limbs. Kerry rallied mid-match, unleashing a series of German suplexes, 
but Hart's rinG generalship and infamous Sharpshooter forced the tap.
Bret defended the title, and after the match helped Kerry to his feet. 
Both men shook hands as fireworks blasted throughout the arena in celebration
of Bret's victory. Kerry raised the hand of Bret Hart, but the look on Kerry's 
face told a story beyond respect. Self doubt, frustration, and embarassment 
looms large within Kerry Von Erich.

In the semi main event. Jeff Jarrett and Eddie Gilbert take their feud
to another level. Jarrett already won a number one contenders match over
Eddie Gilbert in Lousivlle, so a Unified title shot is in his back pocket. 
a 4 1/2 star match between these two saw steel cage warfare at it's finest.
Gilbert slammed Jarrett throat-first into the mesh, then delivered
a brutal piledriver through the ropes. Jarrett was bloodied and beaten,
and Gilbert escaped victorious- earning instant credibility and puts Hot 
Stuff International in the main event spotlight.   

The biggest shock of the night came in the Terry Funk/Kevin Von Erich match.
it was the match of the night according to the wrestling journalists that
were in attendance. The match was brutal, unforgiving, and unforgettable. 
Funk rounded Kevin into the match for ten minutes before The Natureboy
Ric Flair made a shocking appearance at ringside. The distraction
forced Kevin Von Erich into a Funk spinning toe hold. Kevins leg gave way-
and the crowed gassed as he passed out. Kevin Von Erich is now officially 
aligned in the Texas Bunch, a misfit group of former World Class 
Championship Wrestling outkasts. Ric Flair has shown up in the USWA, and
hended the night with a staredown with Terry Funk and Kevin Von Erich
in the ring. 

In tag team action, Scott Steiner and Robert Fuller made their way down
to the ring to take on Spivey and Hansen. Referee Downtown Bruno reminded
Scott that Robert Fuller can not team with him tonight. Scott then said he 
had a new partner for the night, and out walked Tully Blanchard! Former AWA
wrestler Tull Blanchard made his way to the ring and accepted the challenge. 
The match wasn't much as Dan Spivey choked out Scott Steiner until Bruno 
called for the DQ. 

Steve Austin of Hot Stuff International defended the southern title with
the help of the Gilberts and Condrey. The Gilberts interfered in the match,
giving way to a stun gun by Austin leading to the pin. Brown attempred to
get revenge, but HSI continued to beat him down in the ring, Austin 
began to whip Brown with the title belt, until from the crowd came out
The Diamond Studd!!! Studd cleared the ring, and sent HSI flying back
to the lockerroom. Austin was furious as he realized that all those mysterious
notes came as a warning that the Studd was coming for his title! Austin 
accused Missy of knowing about this the whole time, but she begged Austin
to believe her that she had nothing to do with it! 

 Earlier in the night we had The Texas Bunch getting the in over the Memphis
Mafia. Lawler was invited to join the team, but he couldn't be the difference
maker. After the match, with Sweet Daddy layed out, Eric Embry draped a 
World Class flag over him to the boo's of the crowd.

Andre the Giant once again dominated the tag match, in particuarly the 
returning Jimmy Valiant. Bill had one opportunity to get his hands on Jamie,
but Jamie coward behind Andre, who was able to seal the victory for Ellering
and Jamie with an elbow drop that knocked the wind out of Valiant, making
the fans go silent as their returning hero was left laying. 


In a preshow match, the talented CMLL superstar El Hijo del Santo made his debut
taking on the USWA's young upstart Scott Taylor. Santo turned Taylor inside out 
before locking him in a surfboard maneuver. The crowd was with them on every
transition, erupting when the Luchador locked Taylors limbs in the center
of the ring. Scott stuggled valiantly for nearly ninteen minutes, but the
torque proved to be too much. An impressive debut for El Hijo del Santo! 

In our second preshow match, a shocking turn of events, when the Moondogs 
turned their back on Mark Cantebury and aligned themselves with the 
Honky Tonk Man!  

Michael St. John intoduced Rocky Johnson to the crowd as a special guest.
The roar of approval from the fans hinted at the potential mentorship
for Power Pro Wrestlings next generation.
