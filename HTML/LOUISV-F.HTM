Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
October 11th, 1990
Attendance: 1,100

Jerry Lawler pins Terry Funk in our tag team main event tonight, giving himself
and Bill Dundee a victory to send the Louisville fans home happy. Terry Funk
is demanding a rematch for the Unified Heavyweight title, Eddie Marlin is
set to discuss the #1 contender situation on television tomorrow. 

Jarrett and Gaylord stole the show with a big victory that some fans are calliing
an upset over The Steiner Brothers. Jarrett hit Rick Steiner with a Steinerline
giving his team the win and being declared the top contenders to the tag
titles currently held by the Dirty White Boys. 

The Unified title tournament results have played a large roll on the matches
that were booked around the territory this week. After Eddie Marlin announced
a second chance "tournament" in Evansville, Robert Fuller came out on top.
It was later decided that Eddie Gilbert and Steve Keirn would face off in 
Louisville, with the winner to take on Robert Fuller tonight. The winner
of that would then be recognized as the #1 contender to Dick Slaters Southern
Title. 

Keirn defeated Eddie Gilbert today, and then went on to face Robert Fuller,
it was Fuller who took the victory and is now recognized as the top contender
to the title. Meanwhile last week on TV Dundee and Billy Joe Travis went to a
time limit draw in a match that was going to determine the next contender. 
They are scheduled for a rematch on TV this week, and we are getting word that
the winner of that will then go up against Robert Fuller at the Mid South
Coliseum. After Monday we will FINALLY have a new contender and the matchup
for the title will take place right here in Louisville next week! 

Action Jackson and Snowman have teamed up and picked up their first win as a 
unit over The Southern Rockers. As singles competitors, these two were bruisers,
it will be interesting to see them pair up. They would automatically be
thought of as the most powerful team in the USWA. 

In other action from the Gardens, Dustin Rhodes, The Dirty White Boys and the
Moondogs all leave the arena with victories.
