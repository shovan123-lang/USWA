Lance Russell sits down with Bill Dundee and talks about the match Eddie Marlin 
has set up for this saturday night. Bill will go one on one with Terry Funk in
a match that will elevate the winner in the Unified championship scene. Bill
Dundee says that this will be the first time he and Terry lock up in the USWA. 
They have teamed together in the past and he has nothing but respect for Terry,
but when it comes to providing for his family, and the opportunities that come
with becoming a champion, he has to put the respect aside and do what he has
to do to win the match. Dundee tells Funk to bring his best to the Mid South
Coliseum next weekend, because he will be gunning for Funk.
