Location: Mid-South Coliseum, packed crowd, spotlight on the ring.
Talent: Bret Hart (babyface), Kerry Von Erich (babyface), Dave Brown (interviewer)
Context: Star Wars is days away. The USWA Heavyweight Title is on the line.

[Camera fades in. Dave Brown stands mid-ring with a microphone. The crowd 
buzzes as Bret Hart and Kerry Von Erich stand on opposite sides of the ring,
staring each other down.]

Dave Brown:
“Ladies and gentlemen, we are just days away from Star Wars, and the main event
is set—Bret ‘The Hitman’ Hart versus Kerry Von Erich for the USWA 
Heavyweight Championship. Tonight, for the final time before that showdown,
these two competitors meet face to face.”

[Dave turns to Bret Hart, who steps forward, calm and focused.]

Bret Hart:
“Kerry, I’ve got nothing but respect for you. You’ve carried the Von Erich 
name with pride, and you’ve earned every cheer that comes your way. 
But at Star Wars, respect won’t win the match. Skill will. Precision will. 
And I’m coming to prove that I’m the best there is, the best there was, 
and the best there ever will be.”

[Crowd pops. Kerry nods, then steps forward.]

Kerry Von Erich:
“Bret, I respect you too. You’ve wrestled all over the world, and you’ve 
earned your stripes. But this isn’t Calgary. This is Memphis. This is 
Texas pride. This is family. And when I step into that ring at Star Wars, 
I’m not just fighting for a title—I’m fighting for everything 
my name stands for.”

Bret: (nods)
“I know what your name means, Kerry. But this isn’t about legacy—it’s about 
who’s better. You’ve got heart. I’ve got technique. Let’s see which one 
holds up under pressure.”

Kerry: (leans in)
“You want pressure? You’re gonna feel it when the crowd roars and I’ve got 
you locked in the Iron Claw. You’re gonna feel it when you realize this 
isn’t just another match—it’s a war.”

[Dave Brown steps between them, sensing the tension.]

Dave Brown:
“Gentlemen, the fans are ready. The stakes couldn’t be higher. At Star Wars, 
one of you walks out the champion. Let’s keep it clean, let’s keep it 
fierce—and may the best man win.”


[Bret and Kerry stare each other down. No handshake. No cheap shot. 
Just pure intensity. The crowd roars as the camera fades out.]
