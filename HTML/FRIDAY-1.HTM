Match observations: 

Friday Night Fights
Nashville, TN 
Attendance: 1,426

Almost 1,500 fans were in attendance for our followup show in Nashville. 
A $17,112 gate and a new record for the city saw a NEW TV champion crowned
after weeks of qualifying matches on tv. 

Bill Dundee, a Memphis legend, defeated Brickhouse Brown, giving Brickhouse his 
first loss since his return to the ring. 

The return of BoBo Brazil to the territory was a welcome surprise for
everyone, as he was a huge fan favorite in the 60's and 70's in these
parts. BoBo was ringside for the finals of the TV title tournament, and
helped Brickhouse Brown back to his feet when he realized his dream of 
winning the title didn't come true. BoBo will be working in the USWA 
performance center as we look to scout and develop the future USWA
wrestlers. 

Eddie Marlin talks about Friday Night fights next week. Jarrett vs Gilbert
no holds barred! in Lousiville, KY. 

Also announced on the show BoBo Brazil made the announcement that Nashville 
will be the new home of the USWA Performance Center. They will be hosting 
shows once a month with wrestlers that are training and wrestling legends
coming in as coaches. All tickets are $3 at the door!
