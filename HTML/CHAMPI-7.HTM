Championship TV Recap: 

The main event we have been waiting for all year was aired tonight on
television as The Steiner Brothers won the USWA Tag Team titles in our 3 
way dance. The celebration would be short lived as a new team made their way
to the ring to attack the new champions. Stan Hansen and Dan Spivey took
down the tag champions and put the USWA on notice! 

Gringos Locos made their USWA debut taking the Nightmares to a draw in tag
action. Eddie and Art Barr come to the USWA from AAA, and called out a few
different teams when we got words from the new team. 

Robert Fuller beats the Honky Tonk Man in singles action. Michael St.John
mentioned that Fuller was on a roll as of late. Eddie Marlin joined St. John
to announce our next big night at the Mid South Coliseum will be taking place
on May 25th. Marlin says we will have more details on the card on Worldwide,
and next week on Championship TV, but he has signed the first match of the
show, which will be Fuller vs another wrestler on a roll, Brickhouse Brown!
