KERRY VON ERICH
Evansville, Veterans Memorial Coliseum | Segment 8 (3:00)

Backstage. Lance Russell stands with Kerry Von Erich. Kerry wears a USWA
T-shirt, his ribs taped underneath. He looks tired but steady.

LANCE RUSSELL: Kerry, Saturday night in Memphis, Andre the Giant put you in
that bear hug, and you passed out rather than give up. How are you feeling?

KERRY VON ERICH: Lance, I'm sore. I'm not gonna lie to you. My ribs feel
like Andre's still hanging onto 'em. But I'll tell you what I felt the
most. When I was in that hold, and things started going dark, I could hear
those people in the Coliseum. They were pulling for me. They were screaming
my name. And they had my back.

KERRY VON ERICH: I want to thank every one of those fans, because they
respected that I didn't quit. I didn't give up. My family never taught me
how to give up. Andre didn't make me say uncle, Lance. He just put me to
sleep.

LANCE RUSSELL: But Kerry, that's two big losses now. Bret Hart at Star
Wars, and Andre on Saturday.

KERRY VON ERICH: I know it. And I'm not gonna stand here and make excuses.
I'm not just some notch on Bret Hart's belt, and I'm not just a stepping
stone for Paul Ellering. But surviving isn't enough. I've got to get back
to winning. That's the bottom line.

KERRY VON ERICH: And I get my chance Saturday night in Louisville. Andre's
gonna be standing across the ring from me again. Only this time, I'm not
alone. Bret Hart's gonna be right beside me. We've had our differences, and
we'll have more. But Saturday night, Andre and Jamie Dundee are gonna find
out what happens when a Von Erich and a Hart are on the same side.

Kerry nods to Lance and walks off.
