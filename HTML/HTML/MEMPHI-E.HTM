Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
December 31st, 1990 
Attendance: 9,126

Sam Houston defeats the debuting Spellbinder. The Spellbinder seems to be a
mythical, magical character and the fans really were into him. Sam Houston
and the Spellbinder shook hands post match. 

Joey Maggs got some revenge for Immortal with a victory over Bill Joe Travis. 

The rest of the evening was A Block action.
