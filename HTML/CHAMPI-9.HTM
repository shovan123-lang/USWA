Match observations: 

Championship TV Recap: 
Eddie Gilbert has made it known he is coming for the Unified title, and he's 
a serious challenger to Bret Hart! Gilbert went the distance with the champion, 
and has demanded a rematch from Eddie Marlin. 

The Steiners have a lot of momentum going into the three way dance this weekend.
Can they win back the USWA tag titles? We will find out this Saturday night! 

The big announcement of STAR WARS 91' will take place in our debut of St. Louis
this June. Eddie Gilbert gets his rematch with Bret Hart, and Eddie Marlin 
promises this will be the showcase event of the summer! 


Next Week on the show, 



Backstage Notes: The Steiners contracts have ran out. Scott Steiner has expressed
his desire to move on from the territory, but Rick Steiner was willing to stay.
Eddie Marlin has had multiple tag teams split, keeping one of the team members 
on payroll. He was not willing to make this agreement with the Steiners and 
both parties have agreed to a 10 show extension, which should take the Steiners
into late June. 

Jacque Rougeau's contract has expired and he will be leaving the territory. His
final show will be the May 11th tapings at the Mid South Coliseum where he will
face Jeff Jarrett. Jacque has had great matches with everyone he has stepped
in the ring with and the USWA has interest in bringing him back in the future.
