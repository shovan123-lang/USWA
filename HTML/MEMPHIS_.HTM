Match observations: 

Mid South Coliseum
5.11.1991
Attendance: 6,500

A wild night of action at the Mid South Coliseum this week.

The returning Brickhouse Brown continues to rack up victories, tonight 
against Moondog Cujo. Brickhouse has qualified for the TV title tournament,
and the USWA is on notice that he could very well be the first ever TV champ! 

Hot Stuff International made short work of the newcomers The New Kids. Condrey
and Gilbert continue to dominate the tag division. 

Rob Zakowski gets a victory over Scott Taylor to qualify for the TV title
Tournament. This was Rob's biggest victory to date, and it will be interesting
to see how he competes with the other stars of the USWA. 

In one of our featured matches of the night Jeff Jarrett defeated Jacques
Rougeau on Jacques final night in the promotion. The fans knew this was his
last night in the promotion and chanted "hey hey hey, goodbye" all throughout
the match. Jacques was very impressive in his time here, and USWA leadership
would like to try to get him back in the future. 

Steve Austin in his 6th title defense, challenged his toughest opponent to date
in Kawada. Kawada has been impressive during his excursion to the United 
States, but it was Austin tonight with the victory, continuing the prove
that Hot Stuff International is the best thing going today. 

Eddie Gilbert is on a mission to become the Unified champion, Doug and Eddie
defeated Bret and Davey recently, then Eddie went the distance with the Champion
Bret Hart. Tonight, Eddie continues to build his resume with a victory over
Davey Boy Smith. Post match out came Hot Stuff International and the
3 men continued to attack Davey until security broke it up. With Bret
Hart not in the building tonight, Davey had to be helped to the back. 

A wild brawl with the Texas Bunch tonight. We were introduced to a new leader 
of the group, Terry Funk! Funk, the wild one from Amarillo said he will 
take the group to new heights! 

Jerry Lawler came out and was challenged by former rival from a different 
promotion, Kerry Von Erich. Eddie Marlin said we will have more information on
this in the weeks to come. 

In our main event, Stan Hansen and Dan Spivey make their in ring debut, with a
victory The Steiner Brothers. Hansen and Spivey did all their talking in the
ring tonight. After getting involved in our main event at 3 way dance, they
backed it up with a victory over one of the most dominant teams in the USWA.
