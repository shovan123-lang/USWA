Backstage Dave Brown is alongside Eddie Marlin talking about how the card 
for Star Wars is shaping up. Eddie says that he is still working on finishing
up the details of the card.

Dave says that he knows there is a new match to be announced and wants to give
Eddie the floor.

Marlin says that we have two factions that have been running around the 
USWA causing chaos, breaking the rules, and doing anything they can to
eliminate the other. That would be the Memphis Mafia and the Texas
Bunch. Ever since the USWA aquired World Class Championship Wrestling,
Those boys have been on the warpath. Well at Star Wars these two groups
will meet in 8 man tag action. Let them all in the ring to settle their 
difference the way we do here in the USWA!
