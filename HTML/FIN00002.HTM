THE FLAMINGO INVITATIONAL SERIES
Evansville, Veterans Memorial Coliseum | Segment 2 (3:00)

Scotty Flamingo struts to the ring in a pink sequined jacket and
sunglasses. He takes a mic from the ring announcer and waits for the boos
to swell before he speaks.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: Evansville, Indiana. Take a good look, because this is the
closest any of you will ever get to a real star. Scotty Flamingo. The
Franchise of Flash.

The crowd boos louder. Flamingo smiles and drinks it in.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: Now, I've been looking around this locker room, and I'll
tell you what I see. A lot of guys collecting a paycheck and calling
themselves champions. Well, Scotty Flamingo is done waiting around for
somebody to hand him an opportunity. So starting tonight, I'm making my
own. Ladies and gentlemen, I give you the Flamingo Invitational Series.

He spreads his arms like he's unveiling a monument.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: Every week, I put out an open invitation. Anybody in the
USWA who thinks they're good enough can step in the ring with me. And every
week, I'm going to beat them. By the time I'm done, there won't be one man
in this territory who can say he's better than Scotty Flamingo.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: And Sam Houston, I know you're watching. You got a lot to
say about flashy loudmouths poisoning the locker room. Well, you're not
invited, cowboy. The Invitational is for wrestlers. Go ride a bull.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: First invitation goes out tonight, to a man who's been
around a long, long time. Too long, if you ask me. Sweet Daddy Falcone,
come on down and get your lesson.
