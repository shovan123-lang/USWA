Match observations: 

USWA Worldwide Taping
5.01.1991
Evansville, IN
Soldiers and Sailers Memorial Coliseum 
Attendance: 365

Our Main Event was the first qualifying match for the television tournament. 
The returning Brickhouse Brown took on Mark Canterbury, getting the 
pinfall victory just under 15 minutes. Brickhouse used the BrickKick to put
Henry away. Sweet Daddy Falcone joined Dave Brown and Michael St John at
ringside to comment on the match, and his rivalry with his former tag partner.

We had the debut of a few newcomers to the USWA, the first being Rob Zakowski.
Rob comes to the USWA from Battle Creek Michigan and was trained under the
original sheik. He picked up a quick win over The Masked Avenger. 

The New Kids made their USWA debut. Tony Williams and Brian Christopher 
racked up a victory showing off the speed and quickness of this new 
combination. Post match Brian and Tony introduced themselves to the crowd
and aknowldeged that the USWA has one of the best tag team divisions in all
of the United States. 

In other action tonight, the Texas Bunch made their way to the ring to watch
Eric Embry make short work of Ernesto Jones. Tojo and Miss Texas mentioned
a match next week, Prichard and Embry taking on any two members of the Memphis
Mafia, here on worldwide! 

Jacques Rougeau defeated Omar Atlas and mentioned his upcoming match with Jeff
Jarrett!
