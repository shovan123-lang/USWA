Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
February 11th 1991 
Attendance: 8,600

As we get closer to another big event at the Mid South Coliseum, we had a big time
tag team main event tonight. The Frontline, John Tatum and Steve Keirn defeated
Bill Dundee and Dutch Mantel. All 4 men have a goal of helping Lawler beat down
Terry Funk and Andre The Giant, but egos have gotten involved, and fighting within
has occured. 


The big news out of the arena this week is Steve Austin defeating Jeff Jarrett
and winning the Southern Title. With the help of Missy Hyatt and Eddie Gilbert,
the Stunning one has climbed to the top of the USWA. 

In other action tonight, the Dirty White Boy continues to climb the ranks with
a victory over Action Jackson. Bert Prentice and the Dirty White Girl took the 
mic post match and challenged the Spellbinder for a match next week at the 
Coliseum. 

Sam Houston defeated Hot Stuff Internationals Dennis Condrey. Houston looks 
to take on all of Hot Stuff International and becomes allie of Jeff Jarrett.

Von Eric brothers beat the number one contenders the Nightmares. The Von Erics
have bursted on the scene and could potentially become the next contenders
to the Steiners tag tittles. 

Spellbinder defeated Mark Canterbury. Spellbinder may have his hands full with
the challenge for the Dirty White Boy. 

Scotty Flamingo continues to pick up victories, tonight he defeated the upstart
Kawada.
