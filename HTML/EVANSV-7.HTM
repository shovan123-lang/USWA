Match observations: 

Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Coliseum
October 2nd, 1990
Attendance: 418

Last week in Evansville The Dirty White Boys walked out on the Steiner Brothers.
This week, Eddie Marlin made sure that wouldn't be the case. In our main event
the Steiners pick up a big win going into the 6 team elimination match in
Louisville, defeating The Dirty White Boys by submission in our main event.
The Dirty White Girl's attempted interference was put to a stop when Rick
Steiner grabbed her and layed a big kiss on her! The fans left Evansville
happy to see the Steiners as the victors. 

Dirty Dutch Mantel challenged Jerry Lawler to a strap match in our semi main 
event. The match went the 20 minute time limit, but the referee demanded it
continue at the request of the fans. Dutch took advantage of the extended time
to hit all 4 turnbuckles and win the match over Lawler. 

In a battle of the big men, Action Jackson defeated Snowman in singles
action, Brickhouse Brown defeated Billy Joe Travis, The Hardliners teamed with
Eddie Gilbert and picked up a win over Dustin Rhodes and the Nightmares. 

Sweet Daddy Falcone defeated Moondog Rex after interference from Moondog Spike.
The war between the Uptown Posse and the Moondogs doesn't look to be coming to
an end anytime soon.
