Match observations: 

Slamboree 91
May 25th, 1991
Mid South Coliseum
Attendance: 5200

Kerry Von Erich will head to Star Wars to take on the USWA Unified
Champion on July 1st. Our main event saw a back and forth between two
of the top wrestlers in the USWA, but in the end Kerry hit Lawler
with the tornado punch, and Lawler walked out of the ring leading
to a countout victory. 

Hansen and Spivey looked unstoppable in tag action, picking up a win over
Gringos Locos. When the bell rang, Spivey refused to let up the young
tag team until the Nightmares came out and cleaned house. Stan Hansen
could be seen telling the Nightmares they picked the wrong team to mess
with! 

In what was supposed to be a featured match, Bill Dundee put his 6 match
winning streak on the line against Kawada with title implications on the
line. Things were getting agressive outside the ring when Scotty Flamingo
came out and just clobbered Dundee in front of the ref, leading to a DQ
win for the Superstar.

Another special attraction tonight was the return to the ring of Andre
The Giant and Paul Ellering teaming with Jamie Dundee to take on the 
Moondogs in a hardcore match! It seems like the Dogs are now under the
guidance of Mark Canterbury, and tonight they went the distance in a
hardcore match. All 4 men were exhausted as the time limit expired.

Brick House Brown picks up another big win, this time over Robert Fuller
in singles action. Brown has a ton of momentum going into the TV title
Tournament next month in Nashville. 

Backstage Notes: 
Eddie Marlin came out for an opening statement to the press. He said that the
5,200 in attendance we loud and proud to see the moment Kerry Von Erich defeated
Jerry Lawler, and earned his first Unified title match at our biggest show
of the year, Star Wars. 

Eddie confirms that Star Wars will take place at the Mid South Coliseum and 
that prices for the show will be $15 for all tickets. He is promising a night
that fan won't want to miss. 

Once the floor was open to questions, the first media member asked about future
shows being availble on VHS, and the overall strategy of the merchandising
department. 

Eddie said that he is continuing to build a management team that is going to
expedite the growth of the company. As you know Jerry Jarrett has joined in 
the role of working on broadcasting and media distribution. Merchandising 
is the next step. We have started to sell a limited amount of items at the
arenas, and we are bringing in Mike Graham from Championship wrestling from
Florida as an executive to oversee merchandising. We are expecting to have
a amount of merchandise available at Championship Wrestling from the 
Mid South Coliseum on June 1st. 

Eddie responded to a question about the future of Championship TV and the
WMC TV 5 Studios. Eddie said that Studio wrestling will always have its 
place, but the limited seating is hindering the growth of the company.
Eddie says they need to be able to sell more tickets on a weekly basis, and 
the Studio has its limitations. We need to be able to bring in big name
superstars and to do that we need to find ways to increase our revenue. 
Thats just the facts. 

Dave Brown, who was hosting the presser introduced the number one contender
Kerry Von Erich to the press pool. Kerry said that tonight was a confirmation
that he is the best wrestler in the world. He thanks Eddie Marlin for
bringing him and his brother into the promotion, and at the biggest show in
the short history of the USWA, he is going to walk away as the new Unified 
Heavyweight champion. Kerry says that the Von Erich brothers are the future
of Memphis wrestling and will continue to be the face of Texas wrestling. 

A couple questions from the audience, one asked Kerry if he feels he had a 
better match tonight with Lawler than he did in 1988. Kerry said that both
matches were different. A different time and different stakes. Tonight
he put everything into winning that match, and now looks forward to facing
Bret Hart for the title. 

Final question, a media member ask Kerry how he feels about becoming the face
of the USWA? Kerry says that his fathers company will always hold a place
in his heart, but he knows that he is exactly where he needs to be.
