Dave Brown is standing by with Chris Adams near the entrance ramp, when
Chris Adams and Tojo Yamamoto walk into the shot. 

Chris says that the Texas Bunch continue to be the most powerful faction 
the USWA has ever seen. 

Dave brings up his loss to Bill Dundee, but Chris blows it off, saying that
Bill had the hometown advantage, and he will soon enough have gold around 
his waste. 

Tojo says that they continue to bring in the best wrestlers from around
the world, and today they want to talk to Sam Houston! Chris says 
that they have been scouting Houston since the moment he got to the USWA,
and that with the right guidance, and with representation from Tojo
Yamamoto, Houston would have the chance to make a lot more money in
this buisness if he joined the Texas Bunch. Adams tells Sam to think 
about it, because Championship gold around their waste would be 
bigger paydays in their future!
