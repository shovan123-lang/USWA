Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
September 27th, 1990
Attendance: 1,010

Jerry Lawlers impressive week came to an end in Louisville at the hands of
Eddie Gilbert. With the help of Dutch Mantel, Lawler was pinned by Gilbert,
giving Eddie Gilbert reason to believe, he may walk out of the Mid South 
Coliseum on October 8th, the Unified Champion. 

In the semi main event; Bill Dundee failed to get revenge on the Snowman and
fell victim to the Powerslam. 

The Hardliners and The Steiner Brothers went to a double countout. These two
teams were put in the ring together to increase the competition, and thats
exactly what happened tonight. Although nothing was solved,the tag team 
division is getting pretty intense here in the USWA. 

Dustin Rhodes picks up another win over Billy Joe Travis as he continues to 
stay in the Southern Title picture.
