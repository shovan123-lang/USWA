Championship TV recap:
Season 2- Episode 24

With Star Wars right around the corner, the USWA had a gigantic main event
for this weeks Mid South Coliseum show. Bret Hart and Kerry Von Erich, who
will compete for the Unified title, teamed up to take on Jerry Lawler and
Dutch Mantel inside a steel cage! The match ended when Lawler hit a devistating
neck breaker on Kerry, and escaped the cage. After the match, Bret Hart was
frustrated with Kerry for the loss and the two got into a heated shoving 
match to end the show. 

We will hear from both men this week on Worldwide TV from Evansville, Indiana.
It was also announced that this Friday in Lousiville, Kerry will go one on
one with Jerry Lawler before his title match at Star Wars! 

Our Semi Main Event tonight had a captains rules match between Jeff Jarrett
and the Nightmares taking on Hot Stuff International. The captains never did
get pinned, but it was Danny Davis getting the pinfall on Doug Gilbert, winning
the six man match for the team. This pinfall will add fuel to this rivalry,
as both teams head to Tag Team Mayhem at Star Wars. 

We learned that Jarrett and Brickhouse will team against Austin and Eddie 
Gilbert in a featured matchup next week.

Our opening contest saw Eric Embry losing to Dick Slater. We
got word after the show that Dick Slater suffered a pretty serious injury,
and could be out for an extended period of time. This was some bad timing as
Eddie Marlin announced an 8 man elimination match for Star Wars between
the Texas Bunch and the Memphis Mafia. It looks to me that the Mafia maybe
in need of a new partner! 

The new TV Champion Bill Dundee came out and thanked the fans for being by his
side, and talked about the TV title tournament until Jamie Dundee comes out
and tells Dundee he won't hold that title for long, because Andre the Giant
will be getting that first title shot at Friday night Fights!  

Brickhouse Brown made short work of Sam Houston finishing him off with a 
series of power moves, and a a Sideawalk Slam! Brown doesn't want to let that 
TV title tournament loss affect him from keeping his momentum going in singles 
competition.
