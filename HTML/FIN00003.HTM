SAM HOUSTON AND SCOTTY FLAMINGO
Jonesboro, Earl Bell Community Center | Segment 3 (2:30)

The bell has rung. Scotty Flamingo stands over Jim Powers with his arms
raised, then snatches the house mic from the ring announcer.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: Three for three! Three invitations, three victories. Jim
Powers, you were a nice young man and you tried real hard, but you just
met the Franchise of Flash.

The crowd boos. Flamingo soaks it in.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: I told you people. The Flamingo Invitational is for the
best this territory has to offer, and so far, the best this territory has
to offer isn't good enough.

Sam Houston walks through the curtain and down the aisle. No hat, no smile.
The crowd comes alive. Flamingo backs into the corner.

SAM HOUSTON: Scotty! You keep saying it's for the best in the territory.
Fine. Put me in it. Next week. Anywhere you want. Send me that
invitation.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: Sam, Sam, Sam. Invitations go to people who matter. I
checked the list, and your name isn't on it. Maybe you could try the rodeo.
I hear they're hiring clowns.

Houston grabs the bottom rope to climb in. Flamingo slides out the other
side, mic still in hand, and backpedals up the aisle.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: By invitation only, cowboy!

Houston stands in the ring, staring after him as the fans chant "Sam!
Sam!"
