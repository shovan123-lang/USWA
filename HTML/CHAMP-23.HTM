Television Recap:

Dutch Mantell walked out of the TV 5 studios as the number one contender to
Robert Fullers Southern Title, and his title opportunity will take place this
Tuesday night in Evansville, Indiana. 

Speaking of the Southern champion, Robert Fuller made it clear that he has
Dustin Rhodes back after witnessing The Midnight Express and Eddie Gilbert
brawl 3 on 1 with Dustin in Louisville. 

A recap of the actions that took place in our six man tag main event from
The Gardens of Friday night kicked off the show. Terry Funk claims he is 
walking out of the Mid South Coliseum as the champion, when he vows to beat
Jerry Lawler in a steel cage.

The Snowman and Action Jackson will get their wish on Monday night taking on
The Southern Rockers. Jackson and Snowman called out the duo after picking up
another win in tag team competition. 

Moondog Rex tests his luck as a singles wrestler starting tonight. Rex defeated
1/2 of the Twilight Zone to get started on the right foot. 

Eddie Gilbert had some words for Dustin Rhodes after their match last Friday.
Those comments were aired on television tonight, as we will see Gilbert and
the Midnight Express in action against Dustin Rhodes and 2 partners of his
choosing.
