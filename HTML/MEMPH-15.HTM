Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
October 8th, 1990 
Attendance: 5,121
Gate: $66,573

Jerry Lawler leaves the Mid South Coliseum as the USWA champion! The finals
came down to Terry Funk and Jerry Lawler, and after a long night of action in
Memphis, Terry Funk submits in the sleeper hold by the King! 

The fans went crazy as the new face of the USWA was crowned! 

Lawler defeated Dutch Mantell in the opening round, and then went on to win
via DQ by over Eddie Gilbert. Gilbert couldn't believe the match would end
in a disqualification when the referee noticed he had aquired a set of brass
knuckles to use on Lawler. It was that one small mistake that would go on to
cost Eddie Gilbert the title. 

Terry Funks path to the final consisted of a victory over Robert Fuller and 
Steve Keirn. Funk made both stars pass out to the spinning toe hold. 

Jerry Lawler will be hitting all the towns this week to show off his new 
Unified Title. We hope to hear more about his plans as the champion. 

Buisness Notes: Tickets for the show were on sale for $13 all seats. The show
drew the largest gate of $66,573 and largest attendance in USWA history!
