Match observations: 

Jonesboro, AR
October 19th, 1990
Attendance: 1,342

The USWA debuts in a new town as Jonesboro Arkansas welcomes the USWA! We knew
we would have to put on a special show and our Main Event had the wrestling
world talking. Jerry Lawler defeated Terry Funk after a 35 minute classic. 
Several times the fans thought Terry Funk was going to walk out the champion.
Funk came into tonights match bruised and battered after some violent matches
this week, but he saved his best for tonight in Jonesboro. Funk attempted to
light up a branding iron to prod the King with, but fortunately he wasn't 
able to make contact with the champion. Lawler eventually ended the match 
with the Piledriver clean in the ring. Lawler retains the title as we head
to TV this weekend. 

Eddie Gilbert pinned Dusty Rhodes in our semi main event. Gilbert took the mic
and said it was only a matter of time until he assembles a group of men to 
take over the USWA!
