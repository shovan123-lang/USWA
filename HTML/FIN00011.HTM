SAM HOUSTON AND SCOTTY FLAMINGO
Memphis, TN, Mid South Coliseum | Segment 2 (2:00, after the match)

Sam Houston has just put away his opponent. The referee raises his hand.
Houston doesn't celebrate. He rolls out of the ring and starts up the aisle
when Scotty Flamingo's music hits. Flamingo walks out on the stage in a
pink sequined robe with a microphone, and the crowd boos.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: Hold it, hold it, cowboy. Don't run off yet. I watched you
on TV Wednesday night, Sam. No more smiling, no more waving the hat. You've
got a new attitude, and you came looking for me. Well, here I am.

Houston stops halfway up the aisle and stares at him.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: You said I poisoned the locker room. Sam, the locker room
was dying of boredom before I got here, and you were the reason. Look at
you. You're a washed-up rodeo act. You're a walking Marlboro ad with no
ratings!

The crowd boos louder. Flamingo laughs into the microphone.

SCOTTY FLAMINGO: The people don't pay to see a cowboy ride in circles, Sam.
They pay to see the Franchise of Flash. So go on back to the barn, and
leave the main events to the men who actually draw money.

Houston drops his hat and charges up the aisle. Flamingo's eyes go wide. He
throws the microphone at Houston and sprints through the curtain before
Houston can reach him.

LANCE RUSSELL: Flamingo wanted no part of him!

DAVE BROWN: Lance, Scotty Flamingo can run his mouth all he wants, but he
just found out Sam Houston isn't playing anymore.

Houston stands at the curtain, breathing hard, then picks his hat up off
the floor and walks to the back.
