A pretaped interview with Eddie Gilbert is aired. The interview took place
after the events at the Louisville Gardens. 

With the Midnight Express by his side, Eddie Gilbert tells Dustin Rhodes that
tonight was just the beginning of a long nightmare for the USWA. Eddie Gilbert
goes on a tirade blaming Eddie Marlin, the USWA Board of Directors, and the
fans for what is about to take place as he and the Midnight Express are going
to run this territory soon enough. 

Gilbert challenges Dustin Rhoads to find anyone he can to face him, Eaton and
Condrey on Monday night.
