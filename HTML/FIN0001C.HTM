MARK CANTERBURY BACKSTAGE
Jonesboro, AR, Earl Bell Community Center | Segment 3 (2:30)

Camera opens on Mark Canterbury in a dim locker room. His hands are taped
and his eyes are burning. The footage was recorded after his match at Star
Wars.

MARK CANTERBURY: You know somethin', I spent months draggin' the Moondogs
outta the gutter. I gave 'em direction, gave 'em purpose, gave 'em a shot
at redemption. I stood by 'em when nobody else would. And what do I get in
return? A chair to the back and a handshake with that greasy, rhinestone
reject, the Honky Tonk Man.

MARK CANTERBURY: Honky, you strutted down that ramp at Star Wars like you
were Elvis reincarnated. But all I saw was a coward hiding behind two rabid
dogs who forgot who fed 'em.

MARK CANTERBURY: Moondogs, you wanna bite the hand that fed you? Fine. But
now you're gonna see what happens when that hand balls into a fist.

MARK CANTERBURY: I ain't a manager anymore. I'm a man on a mission. And
that mission starts with stompin' out the fleas you picked up in Memphis
and sendin' you back to the pound.

MARK CANTERBURY: Honky Tonk Man, you better tune that guitar real sweet,
because the next time I see you, I'm gonna smash it over your head and play
the blues on your broken pride.

MARK CANTERBURY: You lit the fire, boys. Now I'm bringin' the heat.
