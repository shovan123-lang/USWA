USWA News and Notes: 

The USWA schedule is getting a little crazy these days, as the TV studio that
hosted weekly television is under major renovation, forcing Championship TV
to go in a different direction. On top of that the Mid South Coliseum has leased
out the venue to a local sports team. Weekly shows will be on hiatus, but 
USWA will be moving their events to every 2 weeks on Saturday nights while 
the local team is on the road. It is a fluid situation as we move forward.

With the Von Erichs under a long term USWA contract, the promotion was able to
pull off a television deal in Dallas and the Dallas, FT Worth area. We expect a
heavy emphasis on wrestlers that are popular in the Texas region. No 
word yet on what the monthly Lousiville shows will look like. 

USWA will debut at the Dallas Sportatoroium on March 30th and run a spot
show the night before in Galvaston  TX at the Moody Arena. 

Moondog Spot has resigned with the company, and Steve Kerin along with 
Hollywood John Tatum have left the promotion for other opportunties. Both left
on good terms.
