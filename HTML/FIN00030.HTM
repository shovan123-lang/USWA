Location: Dimly lit backstage studio, two chairs facing eachother with 
Corey Maclin seated between Jamie Dundee and Bill Dundee. Paul Ellering 
enters the room, standing behind Jamie, arms crossed. silent, but looming. 

[Camera fades in. Cory Maclin looks into the camera, voice steady.]

Corey Maclin: "Folks, we've got a situation that's been boiling over for 
weeks now. A family torn apart, a title nearly lost, and a tag match
at Star Wars that could settle-or escalate- everything. 
I'm joined now by Jamie Dundee, his new advisor Paul Ellering, and the 
Superstar himself, the USWA Television Champion, Bill Dundee. 

[Camera pans to Jamie, cocky smirk, legs crossed, chewing gum. Bill sits
upright, bruised but composed.]

Corey: 
"Jamie, lets start with you. You forced your own father to defend the tv 
title against Andre the Giant, Why?" 

[Footage of the match from Friday night fights airs over Jamie's response]

Jamie Dundee: (with a smug look on his face)
"Why, Because it's time the old man learned what pressure feels like. He's 
been riding the Dundee name for years, acting like he's the only one who
ever mattered in Memphis. I gave him a chance to prove he still had it-
and he barley survived."

Bill Dundee: (cuts in, voice low but firm)
"You didn't give me a chance, Jamie. You threw me to the wolves. You knew 
what Andre would do to me. That wasn't about proving anything- it was about
punishment." 

Jamie: (laughs)
"Punishment? No, Dad- that was a lesson. And Paul here? He's the only one 
who's taught me what it means to be the man in this buisness. Not some washed
up baby kissing goody two shoes clinging to the past!" 

[Ellering nods silently, eyes locked on Bill.]

Corey: 
"Bill, you've got a tag match at Star Wars. You've said you're bringing a
mystery partner. Care to reveal who's stepping into the ring with you?"

Bill Dundee:
"Jamie wants to talk about legacy? Fine. I'm bringing someone who knows what
loyalty means. Someone who's fought beside me, bled beside me, and never
turned his back on family. At Star Wars, it'll be me and the Boogie Woogie
Man himself- Jimmy Valiant! 

[The arena crowd cheers offscreen. Jamie rolls his eyes]

Jamie:
"Valiant? That fossil? You think he's gonna save you from Andre? From me?"

Bill: (leans in)
"I don't need saving. I need revenge. An Jimmy's not coming to dance- he's
coming to fight. You want to talk about father figures? You threw yous away.
Now you've got to face the consequences of your actions, something you've 
never done in you life!"

Jamie: (Stands up, pointing)
"You're gonna regret this, old man. Star Wars is the end of your story. 
Me and Andre-we're rewriting the Dundee legacy." 

Bill: (stands too, nose to nose)
"Then bring you pen, son. Because I've got the ink-and it's blood red." 

[Corey Maclin steps between them as Ellering pulls Jamie back. Camera
fades out on Bill staring down his son, fists clenched.]
