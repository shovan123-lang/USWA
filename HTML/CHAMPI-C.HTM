Match observations: 

USWA Recap: 

As we roll into April, the tag team scene is heating up. The Steiner brothers
have demanded a match with Dutch Mantell and Lawler after Mantells attack on 
Rick in Dallas. Meanwhile, the Tag Team Titles were sucessfully defended in 
Texas. 

The Texas Bunch have made their way to the USWA and are scheduled to be in 
Nashville, Louisville and Memphis this upcoming week. 

The Dirty White Boy and Ronnie P. Gossett have been very vocal about putting 
together a new group, mostly of wrestlers who's tag team partners have left
the promotion. 

Jacques Rougeau has signed a short term contract to compete in the ring. The
contract can be extended if he defeats Bret Hart for the title in Nashville.
