Location: Backstage interview area, dim lighting, USWA banner behind the mic
stand. 

Talent: Kerry Von Erich, Jerry Lawler, Lance Russell.

[Camera fades in on Lance Russell standing beside Kerry Von Erich, who's taping
his wrists and looking focused.]

Lance Russell:
"Ladies and gentlement, I'm here with Kerry Von Erich, who's got a lot riding
on tonights match. Kerry, last week you and Bret Hart came up short in that
brutal cage match against Dutch Mantell and Jerry Lawler, What's you mindset
heading into tonight?"

Kerry Von Erich: (calm but instense)
"Lance, last week was a war. Dutch and Lawler pulled every dirty trick
in the book, and yeah- we lost. But I'm not here to cry about it, I'm here
to finish what started in that cage. Lawler wants to play games? Tonight, 
I'm not playing, I'm ready for a fight!" 

Jerry Lawler:
"Oh, give me a break, Kerry. You and Bret strutted in like you were God's 
gift to wrestling, and you got beat. Clean. In the middle of the ring.
You're lucky Dutch didn't leave you in a stretcher. 

Kerry: (Stepping forward)
"You call that clean? You used brass knuckles and Dutch held Bret down while
you climbed out" You didn't win- you escaped." 

Lawler: (grinning)
"Escaped? No, Kerry- I survived, I gave you a piledriver that left you
laying in the ring, because you weren't focues. Thats why Bret is sick of you!
I survived that cage match, because thats what a champion does! Which brings
me to tonight. You see, when I beat you -and I WILL beat you- I'm going 
straight to Frank Morell and demanding a title shot! And when Bret Hart 
retains his title at Star Wars, I'll be waiting. If you somehow pull off
a miracle and beat Bret? Well then, I'll be gunning for you, Tornado! 

Kerry: (eyes locked on Lawler)
"You want a shot at the belt? You gotta go through me first! And tonight, Jerry,
you're not walking out. You're crawling."

Lawler: (laughs and leans in) 
"Just remember, Kerry- last week, you lost. Tonight, you fall. And at Star Wars?
You better watch your back, because my schedule looks wide open on July 1st! 

[Lawler walks off, leaving Kerry staring after him, fists clenched.
Lance Russell looks into the camera.]

Lance Russell:
"Well folks, the tension's are boiling over. Kerry Von Erich vs Jerry Lawler-
tonights semi main event could reshape the entire title picture heading
into Star Wars. Don't go anywhere!"

[Fade Out]
