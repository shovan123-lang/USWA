Cameras catch Kevin Von Eric backstage talking to a few of the members of the
production team when Brian Christopher and Tony Williams show up. 

Tony tells Kevin that the Memphis Mafia put a target on their back, and they want
to fight ...tonight. 

Brian says that they need a partner, and there is nobody in the company they 
would rather go to war with than Kevin! 

Kevin mulls over the opportunity, and tells the boys he has never backed down
from the chance to fight and agrees to team with the Kids!
