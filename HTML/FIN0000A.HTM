BRET HART
Evansville, Veterans Memorial Coliseum | Segment 10 (3:00)

Backstage. Dave Brown stands with the Unified World Heavyweight Champion,
Bret Hart. Bret wears his sunglasses and has the title over his shoulder.

DAVE BROWN: Bret, Saturday night in Louisville, you'll be teaming with the
man you beat at Star Wars, Kerry Von Erich, against Andre the Giant and
Jamie Dundee. What did you think when you saw what Andre did to Kerry in
Memphis?

BRET HART: Dave, I watched every second of it. And I'll tell you what I
saw. I saw a man who wouldn't quit. Kerry Von Erich passed out in the
middle of the ring before he'd give Andre the satisfaction. You can say a
lot of things about Kerry, but nobody can say he hasn't got heart.

BRET HART: People want to know why I'd stand next to him Saturday night.
It's simple. I know what it is to carry a name. I grew up in the Hart
family, with my father's name hanging over everything I did. Kerry grew up
a Von Erich. That kind of pressure, most people in this business will never
understand. I understand it. So I'll always have Kerry's back. On Saturday,
against Andre, I'll be there.

DAVE BROWN: So does that mean you see Kerry as an equal?

Bret takes off his sunglasses.

BRET HART: No, Dave. It means I respect him. That's different. I beat Kerry
Von Erich, one, two, three. He hasn't proven he's better than me. He hasn't
proven he's better than anybody yet. Saturday night, I'll watch his back.
But I'm the best there is, the best there was, and the best there ever will
be. And Kerry Von Erich still has to prove it in the ring.

He puts the sunglasses back on and walks off.
