Match observations: 

Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Coliseum
September 25th, 1990
Attendance: 350

The USWA debuts tonight in Evansville, in a move that should bring weekly
USWA action to this great city, Eddie Marlin made sure to kick off the first
event with a bang! He ordered our main event of Jerry Lawler vs Snowman to
be a taped fist match. 

In our main event Lawler took the victory over Snowman, this gives Lawler real
momentum as we get closer to the Unified title tournament. 

In our semi main event, The Steiner Brothers pick up a victory over the
Dirty White Boys after Tom Burton left the ring and refused to return. Rick
and Scott took the countout win, but vowed for a rematch next week with no
countouts or disqualifications. 

Bill Dundee gets the best of Eddie Gilbert tonight. A suprising submission
victory for the Superstar over his hated rival Eddie Gilbert. 

Billy Joe Travis, Action Jackson, and Joey Maggs all pick up wins in action
taking place earlier in the card.
