Match observations: 

Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Coliseum
November 14th, 1990
Attendance: 1,100

A Jam packed Memorial Coliseum saw Jeff Jarrett crowned as the first ever
USWA Top Prospect of 1990. Jarrett would rack up three different victories
to crown himself the champion for the year and secure his future Southern Title
opportunity. 

In the opening match Jarrett defeated Sweet Daddy Falcone in a 14 minute 
back and forth contest to gain his first victory of the evening. 

Scott Taylor chalked up a big win in the first round defeating Rex King of
the Southern Rockers. Taylor several times looked like he would be defeated,
but he squeeked out a victory and was able to punch his ticket to round two. 

Dustin Rhodes with Bill Dundee in his corner made short work of Dirty White 
Boy Tom Burton. In under 1 minute Dustin hit a big DDT and knocked Tom out of
the match, and out of the tournament. 

The biggest man in the USWA to date, youngster Mark Canterbury defeated Joey
Maggs in the first round. Mean Mark looks to be a big road block for Dustin
in the second round. 

We started the second round of action with Jeff Jarrett defeating Scott Taylor.
Jarrett took advantage of a ringside argument between Taylor and Rex King to
defeate Taylor and make his way to the finals. 

Dustin Rhodes with what some are calling an upset, defeated Mark Canterbury 
making his way to the finals.

In our final match Jeff Jarrett defeated Dustin Rhodes with a little distraction
from Billy Joe Travis! Travis made his way to ringside, and although Dundee
was at ringside to handle the distraction, young Dustin Rhodes couldn't stay
focused and Jarrett hit his second rope dropkick to pin Dustin and win the
Top Prospect trophy! 

A big win and a big highlight of Jeff Jarretts young career!
