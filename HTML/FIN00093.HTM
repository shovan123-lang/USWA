Lance Russell makes his way to the ring and invites Kerry Von Erich to

Kerry Von Erich will take on Bret Hart for the Unified Title! 

Bret takes the mic first and tells a story about his father and Kerrys

title. Something that holds deep meaning to him. 

With that said, Kerry knows that everyone in the USWA has a target on his

he has Brets back until they get to Star Wars! 

Bret starts arguing that he doesn't need anyone to watch his back. As the
