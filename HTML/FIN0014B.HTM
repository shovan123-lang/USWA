Dave Brown brings out Jerry Lawler to a large ovation. Lawler talks about 
what a wild time it has been in the USWA between splitting up with World Class,
and having to crown a new champion. Lawler says he is thankful that he earned
his opportunity in the tournament. 

Brown shows footage of the finals vs Terry Funk. Lawler says that Terry
