HOT STUFF INTERNATIONAL
Evansville, Veterans Memorial Coliseum | Segment 9 (4:00)

The Hot Stuff International locker room. Eddie Gilbert sits on a bench,
flipping through a stack of newspaper clippings. Missy Hyatt stands next to
him. Steve Austin leans against the lockers with the Southern Heavyweight
Title over his shoulder.

EDDIE GILBERT: Missy, you read this one? "Hot Stuff International: Austin
and Who?" That's what they're writing about us now, darlin'. Austin and
who.

MISSY HYATT: Oh, Eddie, nobody reads that stuff.

EDDIE GILBERT: Everybody reads that stuff. Every promoter in this country
reads that stuff.

STEVE AUSTIN: Eddie, what's your problem? I'm the Southern Champion. That's
what they write about.

EDDIE GILBERT: And that's fine, Steve. You've got the belt, and you've got
your little problem with the Diamond Studd. The man's walking around
pointing at your waist. You go handle that. That's your business.

Eddie stands and tosses the clippings onto the bench.

EDDIE GILBERT: But Hot Stuff International is bigger than one belt. I'm the
one who built this. I'm the one who walked into that cage with Jeff Jarrett
and walked out the winner. And I'm sitting around this locker room watching
everybody else in this territory get title shots while I'm holding the door
open for you.

STEVE AUSTIN: Nobody asked you to hold any door.

EDDIE GILBERT: No. Nobody asked. I'm just saying, Steve, there are bigger
prizes in this company than the Southern Title. And Hot Stuff Eddie Gilbert
is starting to think about them.

Austin pushes off the lockers and steps toward Eddie. Missy slides between
them.

MISSY HYATT: Boys, boys. Eddie's right, Steve. Hot Stuff ought to have more
gold. Can you imagine? Two champions in one stable? Everybody would be
writing about us.

She turns to Austin and smooths his collar.

MISSY HYATT: But Eddie, honey, Steve has the belt right now, and somebody
has to keep Studd away from it. We're a team. That's what makes us Hot
Stuff.

EDDIE GILBERT: Sure, Missy. We're a team.

Eddie picks one clipping back up, reads it again, and smiles.

EDDIE GILBERT: Bigger prizes, Steve. Bigger prizes.

Austin stares at him, then walks out. Missy looks at the door, then back at
Eddie, unsure which way to go.
