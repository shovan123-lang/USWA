Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
September 3rd, 1990 
Attendance: 1200

Snowman uses brute force to pin Jerry Lawler in tonights main event. The crowds
coming out to see these two do battle, unfortunatley have drawn the line
between race when it comes to who to cheer for. Snowman continues to claim he
is innocent of any aligations of attempting to sell the Unified Title for drugs. 
Tonight he put an end to Lawler bad mouthing him. 

Dustin Rhodes was promised a rematch for the Southern Heavyweight Championship
with Dick Murdoch banned from ringside. That was the case for 90% of the match,
but when Dustin finally had Slater right where he wanted him, Murdoch ran down
to break up the pinfall and Slater was disqualified. Rhodes walked out of the
coliseum a winner, but he couldn't walk out with the title. 

The Nightmares picked up a solid victory over The Uptown Posse. Rex King
came down to take Reggie B Fine out of the equasion. A little payback for 
Reggie's distraction during the Southern Rockers match on TV this past weekend.
King tripped up Sweet Daddy during the match, which led to The Nightmares
taking the victory. Ken Wayne shook the hand of Rex King, as King raised the
hands of the Nightmares in victory. 

Eddie Gilbert and Bill Dundee tore the house down in the Mid South Coliseum. 
This wild brawl saw everything that makes a Memphis wrestling brawl what it
is today. With no disqualifications or countouts, it was only a matter of time
until Ronnie P. Gossett unleashed his Dirty White Boys on Dundee. Tonight
Dundee had backup as Danny Davis came out to even the odds and take out 
Tony Anthony. Davis then jumped Gilbert from the outside to give Dundee
the advantage. Near the 30 minute mark, with both men a bloody mess, it was
Eddie Gilbert who piledrove Bill Dundee through a table, which Tom Burton 
set up earlier in the fight. Gilbert walked out with a victory and a crimson
mask. 

Our opening matchup saw The Tennessee Stud Robert Fuller back in USWA action
defeating Billy Joe Travis. Fans cheered on Fuller after a solid opening 
match.
