Match observations: 

USWA Worldwide TV Taping| June 26th, 1991
Evansville,IN|Venue:Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Coliseum 
Attendance: 640 AVG: Ticket $5.00
Gate: $3,200 Broadcast: Syndication network. 

Television Title dreams were on the line in tonights main event. Toshiaki 
Kawada, who continues to impress everyone since his arrival, defeated long
time nemisis Scotty Flamingo. It was a back and forth affair until Kawada 
hit the Brain Buster for the clean finish. Fans erupted after seeing Scotty
finally be put in his place. 

We heard from Kerry and Bret as they look back at the cage match loss
at the coliseum last Saturday. Bret blamed Kerry for being too emotional
when he gets in the ring, a flaw he will exploit at Star Wars. 
Kerry goes on to defend his style and vows to bring the intensity that 
Bret finds to be a weakness, into the ring when he beats him to become
the USWA Champion. 

Steve Austin confronts Missy Hyatt about the letter he found on Saturday,
he wants her to explain the Diamonds run deep meaning. Missy swears to not
know what it is about or who its from. Austin is fuming, wondering who is
playing games with him. 

Jamie Dundee and Andre the Giant were in the building tonight. After 
a victory in the ring, Jamie tells the audience to take a look at Andre,
because he is about to be your next TV champion. Jamie says that Andre
is gonna beat Bill Dundee worse than his daddy beat him as a child. 

Other takeaways from the show included The New Kids beating Adams and Prichard.
The win gives them a spot in the tag team mayhem match at Star Wars. Winner
gets a future title shot at the tag champions. 

Terry Funk recruted Dutch Mantell to join them in the 8 man tag, replacing
Dick Slater.

Jeff Jarrett cut a phenominal promo to promote this fridays no holds barred 
main event! Fans took notice of the new attitude in the young Jeff Jarrett,
some even think he will pull this off on Friday!
