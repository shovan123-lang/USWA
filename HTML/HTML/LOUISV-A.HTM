Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
November 15th, 1990
Attendance: 1,900

Terry Funk defeated the Unified Champion in his Texas Death Match stipulation and
has all the momentum riding into Thanksgiving Thunder right here next week! 
The title wasn't on the line tonight, but next week we could be looking at a brand
new Unified champion to be crowned in Lousivlle, which is exactly what Funk told
the audience to end the evening. 

Moondog Rex defeated Dutch Mantell in a bone on a pole match to retreive his 
bone and pick up a victory over the dirty one tonight. 

Coming off his top prospect victory Jeff Jarrett and partner Jeff Gaylord 
upset Dundee and Dustin Rhodes in tag team action. Jarrett is a young superstar
who continues to climb the ranks here in the USWA. 

In a big 10 man tag the Southern Champion Robert Fuller led The Hardliners and
Steiner Brothers into victory over Uptown Possee, Dirty White Boys and 
Eddie Gilbert. Gilbert will get a chance at the Southern Title next week! 
Television this week will sort out a lot of the questions aimed at the tag
team titles, as we have plenty of teams that are challenging for the gold that
the Nightmares just won. 

In earlier action tonight Billy Joe Travis picks up a victory over Joey Maggs
after taking a chair shot from Dustin Rhodes for the DQ. These two continue to
fight it out all throughout the territory and will meet inside a steel cage next
week at Thanksgiving Thunder! 

Top Prospect Mark Canterbury defeated Scott Taylor, and Sam Houston continues
to look good, picking up another victory over Snowman in our opening match.
