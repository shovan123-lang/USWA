Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
January 14th, 1991 
Attendance: 9,750

Block A Tag League finals has ended and the Steiners have won the Block. They
tied with Misawa/Kawada, but due to Steiners defeating Misawa Kawada in
Louisville on 12.14.  

Other news tonight from the Mid South Coliseum includes The Nightmares 
retaining the Tag Titles over the Hart Foundation. This long awaited match
was worth the wait as the Nightmares, who went in as Champion underdogs got
the victory. 

Joey Maggs has put a wrench in the plans of Hot Stuff International. Billy
Joe Travis hit the ring very confident in his matchup. He challenged Joey to
a loser leaves town match, saying he will get rid of him just like they did
Dustin Rhodes. In an upset, Joey defeated Travis, and Travis will have
to leave the territory for at least 90 days. Eddie Gilbert came to ringside
and was irate at this ending. 


The Front Line defeated Demolition after the two teams had tied in the finals
of the Tag League. The Front line will meet the winners of the B Block next week!
