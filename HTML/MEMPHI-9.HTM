Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
January 28th, 1991 
Attendance: 6,600

In a rematch for the Tag Titles, the Steiners defended the straps
against former champions the Nightmares. Post match the Moondogs came to ringside
and stared down the former champion Nightmares, it seems that they maybe looking
for chance to climb the ranks in the tag team division. 

The Moondogs were sucessful earlier in the evening defeating the twilight zone
in tag team competition. They attempted to unmask the team, but they ran off 
before that could take place. 

In a fantastic match, Bret Hart defeated the young man from AJPW, Kawada. Kawada
is going to be staying with the USWA on excursion for the next few months. 
Mark Canterbury has already stated he wants a match with the young Kawada. 

Bert Prentice brought out the Dirty White Boy to take on rival Bill Dundee. 
DWB gets the victory and continues to impress. The Dirty White Boy will have
his hands full on our next card when he faces Sam Houston. 

Jeff Jarrett was victorious in his first defense of the Southern Title, defeating
The Honky Tonk Man. HTM says he is the most popular man in Memphis, and he
has his eyes on collecting all the gold in the USWA. 

The Frontline with a big victory over the Hardliners tonight. Dick Slater and
Dick Murdoch haven't been on the same page as of late, with a disappointing
Tag League Perfomance, and another loss tonight, they maybe questioning 
their future as a tag team as the losses continue to pile up.
