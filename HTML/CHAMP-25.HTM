Television Recap:
Studio Audience: 301

Steve Keirn leaves the studio with a victory in todays main event over
Terry Funk with the help of Jerry Lawler. Funk has been very vocal all week
since losing in the finals of the Unified Heavyweight title that he should
get a rematch, but with a loss tonight, that could derail his plans. Funk
pointed at Lawler after the loss and promised to extract revenge on the 
champion. 

Dick Slater will soon know the top contender to his southern title, but 
Eddie Marlin didn't want him out of action for too long. Tonight he picked
up a victory over Moondog Rex. The champ looked good and later said he is
ready to take on anyone that the USWA puts in his path. 

Dick Murdoch joined Lance and Dave on commentary for Slaters match, but
that was after he picked up a quick victory over Joey Maggs in singles
competition. The Hardliners have been dominant in both singles and tag team
competition. 

Jeff Jarrett and Jeff Gaylord were in action today. A quick victory on TV 
after going into Louisville and defeating the Steiner Brothers in order to be
named #1 contenders to the Dirty White Boys tag titles. That match takes place
Monday night at the Mid South Coliseum. 

Snowman and Action Jackson have formed a new team and they claim to be
the strongest team to ever step in the USWA ring. They made short work 
of the Viet Kong Express tonight.
