Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
October 15th, 1990 
Attendance: 4,500

Coming off the heals of crowning Jerry Lawler the Unified champion in a 
tournament last week, the USWA is back at the Mid South Coliseum.

Jeff Jarrett and Jeff Gaylord battled to a draw against The Dirty White Boys
despite a ton of outside interference. Ronnie P. Gossett attempted to get
his hands on Jeff Jarrett, and Scott Steiner went after Jeff Gaylord.
Despite all of these distractions, Jarrett and Gaylord refused to lose. 
The match went to the 30 minute timelimit and the Dirty White Boys leave the
Mid South Coliseum with the titles still in hand. 

Dick Murdoch and Steve Keirn went one on one in a high profile midcard match.
Terry Funk came out to attack Steve Keirn, but Lawler came after Funk and 
this match was thrown out. Lawler and Funk continued to go after eachother
all throughout the arena until Terry Funk had to be stretchered out of the
arena. This led to us losing the Main Event of the evening. 

Eddie Gilbert opened the night complaining about losing the Tournament last 
week. He earily proclaimed that he doesn't trust the USWA and the system. 
This led to a match with Dustin Rhodes who had enough of Gilbert. Gilbert 
took the victory with a Piledriver. 

Billy Joe Travis picked up a victory over Bill Dundee in an unexpected rematch 
from last weekends TV.
Robert Fuller was scheduled for the match but wasn't at the coliseum. 

The Nightmares defeated Uptown Posse in tag team competition. The Nightmares 
are on a tear since the return of Danny Davis.
