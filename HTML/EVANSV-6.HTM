Match observations: 

Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Coliseum
October 9th, 1990
Attendance: 632

Attendance in Evansville was up tonight. Most fans came out to find out the 
results of the Mid South Coliseum on Monday. They were treated to an interview
in the ring by Jerry Lawler, until Terry Funk came out and made a mess of the
place. Challenging Lawler to a match with the Unified Title on the line.
Eddie Marlin will be in the studio this week to discuss the future title
defenses he has in mind for Lawler. 

Speaking of, Eddie started the evening announcing the second chance tournament.
A 4 man tournament that put all the first round losers from the Unified 
tournament in matches with the winner being promised a Southern Title match
in the future. That tournament happened with Dutch Mantell defeating Action 
Jackson, and Robert Fuller over Snowman. Fuller went on to defeat Mantell
and will get that Southern Title match in the coming weeks. 

Our main event saw the Uptown Possee defeat The Moondog in a tag team cage
match! A wild bloody match, but Brickhouse Brown and Sweet Daddy Falcone 
defeat the team of The Moondogs. Brown hit an amazing moonsault to get the
pinfall. Now the Uptown Posse may have just forced themselves into the
tag title picture.
