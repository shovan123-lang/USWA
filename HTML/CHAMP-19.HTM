Backstage Note: 
Eddie Marlin spoke to a few media members after the taping today. He mentioned
the success of the promotion since September, mainly in Memphis and Louisville
bringing in great crowds. 

Marlin had some figures to share. He said that in only 4 months the USWA
hosted 100,402 fans all around the Territory, along with a total revenue
of $938,694.00 

Championship TV had an average of 7,533 viewers every week on WMC TV 5.
Eddie reconfirmed the TV deals he struck with WEHT 25 and WFTE 58 in
Louisville and Evansville. This will bring the USWA show to over a million
homes. We expect TV numbers to increase this year, and we are continuing to
look for emerging markets to grow in.

Eddie has hired Jerry Jarrett, the famous promoter, and father of Jeff Jarrett,
to run a media and distribution division of the company. Jerry will be working
with Media companies from around the country to get USWA Television distributed
in as many cities as we can.
