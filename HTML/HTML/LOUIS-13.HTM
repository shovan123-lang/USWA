Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
March 22nd, 1991
Attendance: 700

USWA championship wrestling drew 700 rowdy fans to the gardens this week with a
loaded show. 

In our opening bout, the Southern Rockers were to square off against the 
Hardliners. Steve Doll took the mic and told the fans that he felt that the
Rockers have been flying under the radar here in the USWA, and it was time to 
make a splash. Doll looked at Eddie Marlin who was sitting ringside and said
that if they won their match tonight, they wanted a shot at the tag team belts,
but if they lost, they would leave town for 90 days. Rex King seemed a little
hesitant to the plan, but Eddie Marlin agreed to the terms. In the end, Steve
Doll took the Gourdbuster from Murdoch and it looks like that will be the last
we see of the Southern Rockers for quite some time. 

Davey Boy Smith continues to climb the ranks in the USWA. Scotty Flamingo 
came out with the microphone and told the crowd that he was impressed that 
at least the USWA put him in the ring with someone who had personality, unlike
Kawada. Davey couldn't care less about Scotty's issues with Kawada and got
the pinfall. Post match Kawada came to ringside and teased a fallen Scotty
Flamingo. 

With Rick Steiner on the concusion list, Scott Steiner was here in singles action
to take on Kerry Von Erich. It was a short match that ended when Scott 
manhandled Kerry, causing him to pass out from the STF in under 10 minutes. 
The fans applauded both teams. 

Steve Austin came out and ran down the crowd, and Jeff Jarrett and challenged
anyone in the USWA to come take a swing at him for the Southern Title. 
Steve was already scheduled to be in action against the Masked Avenger. Needless
to say, The Avenger didn't put up much of a fight against our Southern Champion. 

The Nightmares teamed up with Jeff Jarrett against Hot Stuff International. This
was a fast pace all action six man tag which saw Jarrett get the win for his 
team, pinning Doug Gilbert for the victory. It doesn't look like Jarrett is
done with Hot Stuff Internation quite yet. 

The big rookie, Mark Canterbury got a surprise win hitting The Spellbinder
with an inverted DDT. Canterbury has continued to work out in the USWA 
training school and tonight may have been his biggest victory here in the 
Gardens. Fans are still on the sidelines weather they like him or not. 

Jamie Dundee made his official in ring debut tonight, but couldn't get past
the rookie Scott Taylor. Taylor rolled up Dundee after a big elbow. Taylor
took the mic and gave credit to Jamie for all the hard work he's been doing
at the USWA training center, and said that in time, he will make his dad 
proud. 

Our main event was a classic USWA brawl. No countout, No DQ, Lawler and 
Mantel who have been on a tear, defeated Terry Funk and Dundee when Lawler
hit the piledriver on his nemisis Terry Funk. It was a blood bath in the
Gardens and Funk and Lawler both were opened up the hardway. This was 
a big victory for Lawler and Dutch, leading into their title match with
the Von Erichs at the Sportatorium in 8 days.
