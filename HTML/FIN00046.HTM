Dave Brown hosts the USWA Control Center segment where he talks about
all the upcoming action the USWA has around the territory. 

July 20th the USWA will bring a loaded card back to Dallas, Texas at the
sportatorium. It's been 4 months since the USWA was in Dallas, and Eddie Marlin
promises to have a big show that night. 

Another big announcement is the return the Nashville on July 27th. We are just
days away from our Nashville TV title tournament night. The fallout from the 
tournament and Star Wars will have a big impact on this lineup. 

Dave also talks about the new USWA training center. The facility will be 
opening up in Nashville with a debut show for a limited crowd on June 30th
the day before Star Wars. The first class of students will be in action, with
some legends as trainers. We will have more on that show next week, but what
a way to kick off Star Wars weekend with the start of Power Pro Wrestling!
