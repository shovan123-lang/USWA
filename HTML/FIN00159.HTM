Lance Russell and Dave Brown are excited to kick off the show and run down
todays television program. Brown says that we got word from Eddie Marlin that
to enter the 8-man Unified Heavyweight Championship Tournament, participants
will have to qualify. We will have the first qualifying match as our TV main
event. Robert Fuller is back in the area and The Tennessee Stud will take on
the Southern Heavyweight Champion Dick Slater for an opportunity at the Unified
Heavyweight title in a few weeks. 

Lance says that we will also see Rex King taking on Sweet Daddy Falcone as
the Uptown Posse and The Southern Rockers have been going back and forth 
all around the territory as of late. Eddie Gilbert and the Dirty White Boys
will be here, which means we are sure to hear from Ronnie P Gossett who 
always seems to have an opinion on everything here in the USWA. 

Lance begins to take us to a commercial when The King, Jerry Lawler, comes 
out to a loud ovation from the studio crowd. Lance welcomes the King and 
tells the fans that Jerry has a big match at the Coliseum this Monday as
he will lead Jeff Jarrett and Jeff Gaylord into battle with The Snowman, 
Brickhouse Brown and a mystery partner. 

Lawler says that Snowman pinned him last week and he has been going around
bragging about getting that victory, but he will be singing a different tune
when he teams with Jarrett and Gaylord. 

Lance Russell notices Joey Maggs in the ring and tells Lawler that it looks
like his match is about to start so lets get to the ring.
