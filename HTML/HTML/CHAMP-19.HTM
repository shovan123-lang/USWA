Match observations: 

Championship TV Recap: 

After tonights Main Event, it is official. Andre the Giant will defend his
USWA championship against Bret Hart on March 4th. Andre defeated Rob Fuller
in our main event with the title on the line. This will be the last opportunity
that Fuller has at the title for awhile. 

The Steiners came out to talk about being fighting champions. They are prepared
to fight the Von Erichs for the titles on March 4th. Rick and Scott stayed
at ringside to watch the Von Erichs make short work out of their competition. 

Kawada racks up another victory over Honky Tonk Man on TV. These two will be
involved in a big 6 man tag in Memphis on Monday night. 

Bret Hart came out frustrated, he feels pushed aside and that people are not 
taking him seriously as a title contender. Bret said that he is going to 
take out Terry Funk next week and he is about to run ruff shot over the 
Hardliners this week, then go on to take the title from Andre. 

Doug Gilbert made his TV debut teaming with Dennis Condrey. They got a big win
over the hot Southern Rockers today. Eddie Gilbert welcomed Doug to Hot
Stuff International and said that he and Dennis will be taking on all the
best tag teams the USWA has to offer. 

Steve Austin defeated the Masked Avenger in a quick matchup. Austin will be
putting his title on the line against Jeff Jarrett in Lousiville in the 
main event this upcoming Friday night. 

Big shows coming to the territory this week as we have a main event matchup
Monday night at the Mid South Coliseum consisiting of Andre and Funk vs
Lawler and Fuller. 

The Lousiville Gardens will see Steve Austin vs Jeff Jarrett for the southern
title.
