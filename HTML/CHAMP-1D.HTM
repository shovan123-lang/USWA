B Block action continues this week with a 3 way tie for the lead between
Frontline, El Hijo Del Santo and Lizmark and Demolition. The CMLL team has
been quite a surprise in this tournament. We will see if they can keep the
momentum going.

Bret Hart competed in 1 on 1 action and took a victory over Alan Martin.

BIG news coming from Eddie Marlin this week. Gauntlet for the gold will take
place at the Mid South Coliseum on January 21st, 1991. A match where 2 men
start and every 2 minutes a new competitor enters the ring. Over the top rope
eliminations only and 1 victor. That man will get a USWA title shot in 
March. 

Marlin also announced that he is looking to sign a title match between Jerry
Lawler and Andre the Giant for Gauntlet for the gold. Currently, Robert Fuller
is the #1 contender for Lawlers title, but Lawler could be looking for revenge
on Andre for his interference at Thanksgiving Thunder. We will hear from
Lawler in the coming weeks.
