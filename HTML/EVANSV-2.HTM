Match observations: 

Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Coliseum
November 6th, 1990
Attendance: 844

With Evansville hosting the inaugural USWA Top Prospect Tournament next week,
a lot of young talent had an opportunity to showcase their skills as a 
prelude to next weeks tournament. 

Rex King, one half of the young team, the Southern Rockers picked up a victory
in our opening contest. 

Tom Burton defeated Joey Maggs, as both men will compete next week in the
tournament. 

In a grudge match of sorts, Sweet Daddy Falcone and Moondog Rex went to a draw
when the arena couldn't hold these two back. Sweet Daddy taunted Moondog 
Rex with that dog bone during the entire match, really making things
personal for the Moondog. 

Sam Houston, the newcomer suffers his first loss over Brickhouse Brown in 
a featured match. Brickhouse Brown picked up the big victory and both men
impressed USWA officials, who are looking to book this match again all
around the territory. 

Michael St John rand down the first round matches for next weeks tournament. 

Gilbert and Billy Joe Travis picked up a big win over The Nightmares. After
the match Gilbert told the fans that he found out from Eddie Marlin that the
Nightmares will get a shot at the USWA Tag Titles this week on Television.
Gilbert said he is going to make sure they don't make it to TV this weekend! 
With that Gilbert and Joe put a beating to The Nightmares, leaving them laying
in the middle of the ring. 

In our main event, Fuller and Dustin Rhodes, who are calling themselves The
Studd Stable, defeated The Midnight Express to send the fans home happy. 
Nightmare Danny Davis, even after his injury at the hands of Gilbert and 
Travis ended up coming down to ringside and assisting Rhodes and Fuller,
leading to the victory tonight in Evansville.
