After sucessfully defending the USWA tag titles against the nightmares on 
Monday night, the Steiners took time today to talk about their next opponents,
The Von Erichs. 

Rick said that they have taken on all comers since they won these titles, and 
that they are the big dogs around the USWA. They know that the Von Erichs are
two of the toughest men from Texas, but they will be no match for them on
March 4th. 

Scott said that the Steiner Brothers are all about competition, and Eddie 
Marlin should be commended, because ever since they came to the USWA, they
have faced some of the best teams in the world. The Real World tag league, 
the Nightmares, and now the Von Erichs. The Steiner Brothers have never backed
down from a challenge, and they won't back down against Kevin and Kerry.
