Match observations: 

USWA MAGAZINE: WORLDWIDE REPORT
Evansville, Indiana | Veterans Memorial Coliseum | July 10, 1991

SUPERSTAR SURVIVES! DUNDEE DIGS DEEP TO KEEP TV GOLD

Bill Dundee came to Evansville with a heavy heart and left with his title.
Four days after his own son laid out Jimmy Valiant with a chain, the
Superstar fought off Chris Adams for more than seventeen minutes, then did
something few fans expected. He hoisted the Gentleman up and dropped him
with a piledriver for the one, two, three.

It wasn't the Bill Dundee we're used to seeing. Before the match, he told
Lance Russell that what Jamie did was a coward's act, and that he would
defend the belt for Jimmy Valiant. You could see that fire in the closing
minutes. Adams had the Superstar in trouble more than once, but Dundee
refused to go down, and when the opening came, he took it with the most
devastating move in his arsenal. With Jamie's title shot set for August 3
at the Mid South Coliseum, the message to his son was loud and clear.

FLAMINGO FLIES HIGH

Scotty Flamingo announced the Flamingo Invitational Series, a weekly open
challenge he says will prove he is the best in the territory. The self-
proclaimed Franchise of Flash took a shot at Sam Houston, then called out
veteran Sweet Daddy Falcone, who made Flamingo work for every inch. It was
the best match of the night. Falcone nearly had him more than once before
Flamingo caught him with a flying sunset flip at 8:14. One down. Who's
next?

THE DOGS ARE LOOSE

The Moondogs made short work of Jos LeDuc and Kerry Brown, with Rex putting
Brown away with a bone to the head in just over five minutes. The Honky
Tonk Man then mocked Mark Canterbury as "the dog catcher who lost his
dogs," and that was enough for Eddie Marlin. Next week in Jonesboro, it's
Moondog Spot against Mark Canterbury! Canterbury stormed the ring and sent
Honky and the Moondogs running up the aisle. That leash may not hold much
longer.

POWERS PUNCHES THROUGH

A week after a tough debut against Chris Adams, Jim Powers picked up his
first win in the USWA, coming off the top rope with a cross body press on
Rob Zakowski at 8:04. Keep an eye on this young man.

ALSO ON WORLDWIDE

Stan Hansen and Dan Spivey demanded a rematch with tag champions Scott
Steiner and Tully Blanchard, and they want it in a dog collar match with no
disqualification. Kerry Von Erich, ribs taped, thanked the fans for
standing by him after Andre the Giant put him to sleep, and vowed to get
back to winning. Bret Hart said he'll have Kerry's back Saturday in
Louisville, but made it clear he still sees himself as the better man.

And trouble may be brewing in Hot Stuff International. Eddie Gilbert told
Steve Austin there are "bigger prizes" than the Southern Title, and only
Missy Hyatt stepping between them kept things calm. Austin walked out.
Missy didn't know which way to go.

NEXT STOP: LOUISVILLE

The Red, White and Bruised Tour hits Louisville Gardens this Saturday. Bret
Hart and Kerry Von Erich team up against Andre the Giant and Jamie Dundee,
Steve Austin faces the Diamond Studd, and Ric Flair has an announcement
about his USWA ring debut.
