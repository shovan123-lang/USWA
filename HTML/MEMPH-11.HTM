Match observations: 

Midsouth Coliseum 
November 5th, 1990 
Attendance: 3,500

Jerry Lawler defended the USWA Tite in a big main event over 1/2 of the
Hardliners Dick Murdoch. Murdoch looked like a force to be recon with and
has turned heads as a potential future contender for that title, but Jerry
Lawler defeated Dick Murdoch in his first ever Mid South Coliseum main event.

Murdoch turned heads thoughout the promotion proving he can work on top. Eddie
Marlin had discussed the idea of putting Murdoch in main event B shows coming
off the heels of tonights performance. 

Steve Kerin also extracted revenge over the former USWA Southern Champion
Dick Slater. Seems like it wont be long until Terry Funk has to come back
to the Territory! 

In an exibition of Top Prospect Tournament competitors, Jeff Jarrett took
the win over Scott Taylor. Hopefully, Taylor can come back next wednesday
and learn from his mistakes tonight. Jarrett looked like he is ready
for competition.

Nightmares took a victory over Condrey and Eaton. A wild one between this 
blood feud. Eddie Marlin expressed his desire to have the Nightmares face
The Dirty White Boys next week on TV. 

Robert Fuller and Dustin Rhodes defeated Eddie Gilbert and Billy Joe
Travis in the first of several matches these guys will compete in this week
in the territory. 

Dutch Mantell put a brutal beating on Moondog Rex in this recently sanctioned
Chain Match. Mantell took Moondogs dogbone that has been stolen by himself
and Sweet Daddy Falcone and worked over Rex until officials had to clear the
ring. 

In our opening match, newcomer Sam Houston defeated Brickhouse Brown, although
it is early, Houston has looked great in singles competition.
