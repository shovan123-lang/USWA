Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
December 14th, 1990
Attendance: 1,650

Terry Funk gets a victory over Sam Houston with his spinning toe hold. Funk has
made it known he wants a match with Robert Fuller. 

In a 4 way match Joey Maggs gets the victory over Action Jackson, Taylor and 
Canterbury, the young rookies of the USWA. Maggs has been working to defend
the honor of Immortal ever since they lost Dustin Rhodes. 

A Block action tonight in Louisville!
