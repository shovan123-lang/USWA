USWA WORLDWIDE: CLOSE
Jonesboro, Earl Bell Community Center | Segment 14 (3:00)

Mark Canterbury sits on the second rope, a towel pressed to his forehead,
Robert Fuller beside him. Honky Tonk Man and the Moondogs are at the top
of the aisle, still jawing.

LANCE RUSSELL: What a night here in Jonesboro. Mark Canterbury won the
match, but he's paid for it, Dave.

DAVE BROWN: Lance, that's the second time Honky Tonk Man and the Moondogs
have ganged up on that man. And tonight, Robert Fuller said enough. I
think Honky just found out Mark Canterbury isn't alone anymore.

LANCE RUSSELL: We'll see about that next week in Evansville. But first,
Saturday night, the Sportatorium in Dallas. Ric Flair against Terry Funk.
Jimmy Valiant and Jamie Dundee, no DQ. The Gilberts against the Diamond
Studd and Brickhouse Brown. And Kerry Von Erich and Bret Hart, face to
face, after everything Jerry Lawler said tonight.

DAVE BROWN: Don't you dare miss it.

LANCE RUSSELL: For Dave Brown, I'm Lance Russell. Thanks for being with
us, and we'll see you next week on USWA Worldwide!

Canterbury takes the towel away from his face, looks at the blood on it,
and stares up the aisle at Honky as the show fades out.
