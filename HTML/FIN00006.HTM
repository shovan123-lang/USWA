Evansville, Veterans Memorial Coliseum | Segment 6 (2:00)

Taped earlier. Stan Hansen and Dan Spivey stand in front of a plain
backdrop. Hansen chews tobacco, a bullrope over his shoulder. Spivey stands
behind him with his arms crossed.

with those tag team belts like you earned 'em. You didn't earn nothing. We
had you beat at Star Wars, and you know it. You got a disqualification. You

looking for a loophole.

me. We want the rematch. And we want it the same way. Chain 'em up. Dog
collars. And this time there ain't no disqualification, there ain't no back
door, there ain't no excuses.

DAN SPIVEY: Tully, you're smart. You know what happens when we get our
hands on you. Scott, you got all the muscles in the world, but muscles
don't mean much with a chain around your neck.

STAN HANSEN: You give us that match, and we're gonna drag those belts right
off you. And we're gonna drag you with 'em.
