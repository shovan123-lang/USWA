Match observations: 

Louisville Gardens
February 1st, 1991
Attendance: 1,800

The Southern Title was on the line tonight as the champion Jeff Jarrett picked
up the victory over Eddie Gilbert, who walked out on the match, upsetting the
fans. Jarrett got his hands on Gilbert and delivered a piledrive after the match
to send the fans home happy. 

In the semi main event, The Frontline defeated Andre the Giant and Terry Funk
when Ron Fuller was somehow able to apply the fuller leg lock on the Giant and
the ref called for the bell. During the match Bret Hart came out to get a closer
look at all the competitors in the tag match. 

Speaking of Hart, he had a classic with Dennis Condrey tonight. Defeating him 
after his patented elbow drop from the second turnbuckle. 

Sam Houston gains a victory over Dirty White boy, fending off outside interference
from The Dirty White Girl and Bert Prentice. 

Scotty Flamingo and Joey Maggs have been having words as of late, Flamingo
gets the victory, but this rivalry is far from over. 

The youngster Kawada defeated Scott Taylor in a show stealing opening match.
Kawada hit a devestating brain buster to pick up the win. After the match
Mark Cantebery made his way to ringside and threatened to come in the ring
and put a beating in Kawada. Kawada stood his ground and welcomed the competition.
