HOT STUFF INTERNATIONAL AND DIAMOND STUDD
Memphis, TN, Mid South Coliseum | Segment 5 (2:30)

Backstage, Diamond Studd walks toward the locker room with a towel around
his neck. Missy Hyatt steps in front of him, all smiles. Steve Austin leans
against the wall behind her with the Southern Championship over his
shoulder.

MISSY HYATT: Well, well. Diamond Studd. That was impressive out there. Hot
Stuff International could use a man like you. Money, women, all the gold
you could want. Why fight us when you could be one of us?

DIAMOND STUDD: Missy, you're a lot prettier than your partner. That's about
the only thing he's got going for him.

STEVE AUSTIN: Listen, pal. Nobody in this company gets a second offer from
Hot Stuff. You left those notes in my locker for a month. Diamonds cut
deep. Real cute. So what's it gonna be?

Studd looks at the belt on Austin's shoulder, then back at Austin.

DIAMOND STUDD: I don't need your money, Austin. And I don't need your club.
The only thing I want from you is right there on your shoulder.

DIAMOND STUDD: Louisville. Next Saturday night. They say it's non-title.
That's fine. I'll beat you anyway, and everybody in Kentucky will know who
the real champion is.

Studd walks off. Austin turns on Missy.

STEVE AUSTIN: You were awful friendly with him, Missy.

MISSY HYATT: Steve, I was trying to help you!

STEVE AUSTIN: Yeah. That's what you said about the necklace, too.

Austin storms off. Missy watches him go, then glances down the hall where
Studd went.
