BILL DUNDEE
Evansville, Veterans Memorial Coliseum | Segment 11 (3:00)

Backstage. Lance Russell stands with the USWA Television Champion,
Superstar Bill Dundee. Bill is taped up and ready, but he isn't smiling.

LANCE RUSSELL: Bill, in a few minutes you defend that Television Title
against Chris Adams. But I have to ask you first. Saturday night, your son,
Jamie, took a chain to Jimmy Valiant. What went through your mind?

BILL DUNDEE: Lance, I've been in this business a long time. I've been in
fights all over this world. I've seen grown men do things that'd turn your
stomach. But I never saw anything that hurt me like what I saw Saturday.

BILL DUNDEE: Jimmy Valiant didn't do anything to that boy. Jimmy walked
over there to calm him down. To help him. And Jamie hit him from behind
with a chain. That's a coward's act, Lance. There's no other word for it.

LANCE RUSSELL: And yet he's your son.

BILL DUNDEE: That's what makes it hurt. He's still my boy. I held him the
day he was born. I taught him how to lock up. And now he's standing next to
Paul Ellering saying his daddy never believed in him. Jamie, I believed in
you every day of your life. But you're going down a road I can't follow you
on.

BILL DUNDEE: So tonight, I'm defending this title for Jimmy Valiant. Chris
Adams is a great wrestler, and I'll have my hands full. But I'm walking out
of here with this belt. Because on August the third, at the Mid South
Coliseum, Jamie, you get your shot. And when you do, your daddy's gonna be
waiting for you.
