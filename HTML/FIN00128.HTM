1990 Tag League is going to be several weeks of action that take place starting
next week here on Television and have several stops at the Mid South Coliseum 
and Louisville, but for the most part the action will take place right here
on Television. 

The rules are as follows. 16 teams break off into 2 Blocks for round robin
tournament style matches. 8 teams consist of Block A and 8 teams consisit of
Block B. After we determine the Block winners, those two teams meet for the
Tag League Finals. The winners will go on to our large Quarter 1 show to 
earn a shot at the tag team titles. If the current champions win the tournament,
they will win the right to pick their opponents for the January show. 

The USWA has finalized all contracts and have the A and B Blocks set.
