The fans are going crazy as Eric Embry and Tom Prichard come to the ring with
long time manager Tojo Yamamoto and Miss Texas. 

Embry says that all they hear about is USWA, USWA, THE USWA is the place to be,
so what a better way to join the ranks than to do it right here in our house
the Sportatorium! 

Tom Prichard says that the Texas Bunch is officially in the USWA, and that 
Eddie Gilbert, Steve Austin, Jeff Jarrett, Lawler, Bill Dundee and the rest
of the roster should shaking in their boots right now. 

Tojo Yamamoto asks for a big Texas Chant and says that the Texas Bunch 
will soon take all the titles in the USWA. 

Miss Texas immediately calls out Ronnie P. Gossett and Missy Hyatt and tells
them that she is managing the Texas Bunch and she will make sure nothing 
stops the Texas Bunch for getting to the top!
